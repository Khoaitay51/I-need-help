The romance is a related long prose narrative. Walter Scott defined it as a fictitious narrative in prose or verse; the interest of which turns upon marvellous and uncommon incidents, whereas in the novel the events are accommodated to the ordinary train of human events and the modern state of society. The rise of the novel as an alternative to the romance began with the publication of Cervantes Novelas Exemplares (1613). It continued with Scarron 's Roman Comique (the first part of which appeared in 1651), whose heroes noted the rivalry between French romances and the new Spanish genre.
