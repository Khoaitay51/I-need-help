Flatbush Zombies (stylized as Flatbush ZOMBiES) are an American hip hop group from the Flatbush section of Brooklyn, New York City, formed in 2010. In September 2014, it was announced that Flatbush Zombies would be touring with The Underachievers, collectively known as Clockwork Indigo. Subsequently, they released a collaborative song titled Butterfly Effect, followed by a full EP titled Clockwork Indigo on October 18, 2014.
