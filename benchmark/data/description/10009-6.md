Answer: Flatbush, one of the six original towns settled on Long Island, was orignally known as Vlackte Bosch -- Dutch for (roughly) flat, wooded area or a plain with woods. After the British took over New Amsterdam in 1664 the name was anglicized to Flatbush (sort of the same way Breuckelen became Brooklyn) Flatbush was actually first known as Midwout, and was settled sometime around 1645-1651.
