Lava lamps can be fun and fascinating to watch. Your lava lamp's flow may not be as lively as that of another lava lamp, but that does not necessarily make it defective or in need of repair. Check the operation instructions that came with your lava lamp before taking corrective measures to alter a lamp that has slow or zero flow. (Nick Richards/Demand Media). Replace the bulb if you have had your lava lamp for some time and other measures do not correct your lava flow problem. Check your owner's manual to ensure the correct bulb type and size is installed in your lamp. Nick Richards/Demand Media. Allow your lamp to rest between uses.
