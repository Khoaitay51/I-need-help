Chances are, you’ve suffered from a canker sore or two in your life. These extremely painful, open ulcers on the inside of your mouth seem to take forever to heal up and can really cause some grief. One young man that I know in his early 20’s suffers excessively from these little nasties. Even if a canker sore seems to be triggered by something else, say a scrape from a dental appliance like braces, the underlying cause is STILL diet. In other words, if the person had been eating right in the first place, the scrape on the inside of the mouth would not have resulted in a canker sore!
