650 pages on this wiki. Increasing the focal length and distance of the camera to infinity in a perspective projection results in an orthographic projection. Orthographic projection is a means of representing a three-dimensional (3D) object in two dimensions (2D). Orthographic projection corresponds to a perspective projection with a hypothetical viewpoint; e.g., one where the camera lies at an infinite distance away from the object and has an infinite focal length, or  zoom .
