When excessive levels of albumin in urine are detected for prolonged periods, then it is indicative of a medical condition known as albuminuria. In general, increased quantities of different types of blood protein in urine is referred to as proteinuria. Albumin levels in Urine – Normal, High. The levels of albumin in urine in healthy people are generally in the range within 0 to 8 mg/dl. Urine samples accumulated over a time frame of 24 hours should not have albumin levels in excess of 150 mg. Read more about normal albumin levels.
