DNA exists in Chromatin form in the nucleus. The chromatin condenses to form Chromosomes during cell division. Inside the nucleus. The chromatin in the nucleus forms a ' beads-on-string ' structure or Nucleosome. The 'beads' are octamer histone proteins and the 'string' is the double-stranded DNA. About 147 base pairs wrap around the 8-part histone protein to form a nucleosome. The nucleosomes condense to form a chromatin fiber. Chromatin fiber organises into Chromosomes upon cell division. The chromatin is elaborately arranged and paired in mitosis. Sister chromatids are formed in case of meiosis along with pairing.
