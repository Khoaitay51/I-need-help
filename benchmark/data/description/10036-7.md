Scorpion fish use venom in its dorsal fin to protect itself from predators. It also uses its large mouth which has teeth to catch its prey and also protect itself. Scorpion fish have several adaptations that suit a great adaptor to the ocean. The scorpion fish lives most on reefs and rocks. It adapts itself to look like rocks as a protection device against predators. Its stays mostly on the bottom of the ocean so that it won't use its energy swimming around.
