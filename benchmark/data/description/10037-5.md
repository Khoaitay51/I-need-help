577 pages on this wiki. Nether quartz is an item added in the Update 0.6.0. It is spawned by the Nether Reactor in large quantities and is used to craft Block of Quartz. Nether quartz can also be used for crafting Granite and Diorite. Trivia. 1  Nether Quartz is also simply known as Quartz. 2  It is only obtainable in the Nether. 3  Now in the Update 0.12.1, it spawns in the Nether in the form of Nether Quartz Ore.
