Rose quartz is a pink variety of quartz that occurs in large translucent masses. It is never transparent, and it does not form crystal faces or crystals. It is not a cryptocrystalline variety, however, as it is made up of many intergrown crystal subindividuals. The pink variety of quartz that forms crystals is called pink quartz and is covered in its own section.
