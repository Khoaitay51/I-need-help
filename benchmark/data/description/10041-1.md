Introduction. The Great Chinese Famine was the period in China during 1959-1961, in which poor economic policies produced the largest famine in human history. It can be clearly seen that massive agricultural policy changes during the “Great Leap Forward” were the key factor in causing the famine. There is no denial that it was the single biggest cause which led to the economic collapse of China and brought famine across the country.
