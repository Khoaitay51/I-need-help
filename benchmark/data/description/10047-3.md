USL Welcomes Back Orlando City SC for 2016 - Sacramento Republic FC. USL News Release Thursday, October 15, 2015 TAMPA, Fla. – The United Soccer League announced today that Orlando City SC, which was a member of the league from 2011-14 before joining Major League Soccer this season, will return for the 2016 season. Sacramento Republic FC will host a UEFA Champions League watch party on Wednesday, October 21 from 11:30 a.m. – 2:00 p.m. at Hot Italian Pizzeria and Panini Bar.
