Putty is a village in New South Wales, Australia in Singleton Shire. It is north west of Sydney on the Putty Road between Windsor and Singleton. 1 Putty's proximity to the Wollemi National Park means it is sometimes used as a starting or finishing point for treks. 2  Hikers often utilise old logging trails that make it possible to walk through the national park on trips of eighty kilometres or more.
