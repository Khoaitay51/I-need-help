About. Wollemi Cabins provides accommodation in the Blue Mountains World Heritage Area with cabins and a house in the Mountain Lagoon, Bilpin, Mount Tomah and Berambing areas. The cabins have been built using environmentally sustainable principles and have spa's, barbecues, open fires and double and queen beds. Wollemi House has been designed to incorporate the feel of the Blue Mountains bush and a raw natural aesthetic has been used throughout the interior of the house.
