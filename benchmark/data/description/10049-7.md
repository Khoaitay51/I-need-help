INTRODUCTION: Wollemi National Park is one of the largest in New South Wales, taking in about 500 000 hectares of wilderness country. Located in the park at a secret location are a number of sites where the rare Wollemi Pine grows. This tree was first discovered in 1994. Dunns Swamp is found on the Cudgegong River, about 25km north of Rylstone, in the Wollemi National Park. The swamp area is caused by the Kandos Weir. The area is known for its pagoda rock formations.
