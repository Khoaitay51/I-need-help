The village lies in a wide valley. The knee-deep Putty Creek, or the Tupa, rises in north at the foot of Mt Kindarun, and runs the length of the valley before joining with the Wollemi Creek which then feeds into the Colo River. 1 Putty's proximity to the Wollemi National Park means it is sometimes used as a starting or finishing point for treks. 2  Hikers often utilise old logging trails that make it possible to walk through the national park on trips of eighty kilometres or more.
