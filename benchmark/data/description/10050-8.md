Red wolves are very adaptive to different types of ecosystems and can live in a variety of areas such as forests, coastal prairies, and sometimes swamps. They have a secretive nature and require adequate cover, so they prefer areas with brush, shrubs, or trees. Historically, the red wolf lived as far north as Pennsylvania, as far south as Florida, and as far west as Texas. Today, wild red wolves can only be seen in North Carolina’s Alligator River National Wildlife Refuge.
