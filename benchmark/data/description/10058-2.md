Arman /ar-man/ [2 sylls.] as a boys' name is pronounced ar-MAHN. It is of Spanish and Russian origin. Short form of Armando; Armani is a related Italian surname made familiar by the men's clothing designer. It is also a common Armenian name. Arman is an uncommon given name for males but a very popular surname for both adults and children (#23207 out of 150436, Top 15%). (2000 U.S. Census).
