In India, the term jati refers to a caste or a sub-group. The word is derived from the Sanskrit word for born into.. Members are thus born into a jati, and they stay within … that group, marry within it, and sometimes will not even mingle with members of other groups. The Jati or Caste System is still very much a part of Indian  culture. There are thousands of castes and individuals are expected  to be almost as loyal to their Jati as the … y are to their families.
