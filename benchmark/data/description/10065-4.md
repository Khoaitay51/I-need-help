In India, the term jati refers to a caste or a sub-group. The word is derived from the Sanskrit word for born into.. Members are thus born into a jati, and they stay within … that group, marry within it, and sometimes will not even mingle with members of other groups. Although Mahatma Gandhi tried to outlaw the caste system, it still persists today, although there is certainly more social mobility now than there was when he lived. The Jati or Caste System is still very much a part of Indian  culture. There are thousands of castes and individuals are expected  to be almost as loyal to their Jati as the … y are to their families.
