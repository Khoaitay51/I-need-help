The Role of the Fashion Buyer states that one part of a buyer's job is to negotiate prices and details of delivery with the supplier. Since this is an important aspect of a buyer's job, some retailers provide their buyers with negotiation training courses. When the buyer meets with the supplier, the sales executive of the garment manufacturer will submit a cost price for a garment. Then the buyer calculates the price that the garment will need to be sold for in order to reach the retailer's mark-up price.
