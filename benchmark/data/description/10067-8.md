Retail buyers study consumer fashion-buying trends to select products that will sell in retail stores. Retail buyers have either attended school for it or gained experience in the retail industry first. Fashion buyers should be aware of what is in style. Retail buyers might seek out different clothing lines to purchase for a store. An assistant buyer is more of a clerical job but you do get to understand the assortment patterns and the pricing that goes with each of the vendors so it will give you a ballpark of what you should look for when you become a buyer. This is really the best retail buyer training.
