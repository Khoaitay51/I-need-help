• PHYSIOLOGY (noun). The noun PHYSIOLOGY has 2 senses: 1. the branch of the biological sciences dealing with the functioning of organisms. 2. processes and functions of an organism. Familiarity information: PHYSIOLOGY used as a noun is rare. Dictionary entry details. summation ((physiology) the process whereby multiple stimuli can produce a response (in a muscle or nerve or other part) that one stimulus alone does not produce). stimulation ((physiology) the effect of a stimulus (on nerves or organs etc.)).
