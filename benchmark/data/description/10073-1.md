For example, if you entered the United States in 2001 as an F-1 student, received your green card while here in 2006 (adjustment of status), and committed a crime of moral turpitude in 2008, you are not deportable, because more than five years passed from the date of your admission as a student. Crimes of moral turpitude are not well defined in U.S. immigration law. However, the Department of State has provided guidance, noting that the most common elements of a moral turpitude crime will include fraud, larceny, and intent to harm persons or things..
