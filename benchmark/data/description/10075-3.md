1 The “quat or “polyquat algaecides are quaternary ammonium compounds (instead of a copper formula), which treat and prevent algae growth in a different way. 2  These algaecides are safer to use than a copper based algaecide because they will not stain a swimming pool. 1. Add preventative algaecide to your swimming pool on a weekly basis if you regularly experience problems with algae growth. If you have never had an algae problem in your swimming pool, there is no need for the algaecide.
