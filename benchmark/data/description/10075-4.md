If algae are present in your pool despite your regular pool care routine, which can be indicated by brown, green, blue-green, black or yellow areas on the walls of the pool or an algae bloom in the water, then using algaecide is definitely in order. While the chlorine you use for regular pool maintenance and water sanitization does have bactericidal and algaecidal properties, the addition of pool algaecide will enhance and prolong the effectiveness of the chlorine and can act as a valuable backup to the anti-algae effects of regular chlorination.
