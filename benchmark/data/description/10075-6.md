Know the different types of pool algaecide. 1  Copper-based algaecides use copper to treat algae growth, and are most effective against mustard and green types of algae. 2  Copper algaecides will not cause foam in a swimming pool, which can be a problem when using “quat algaecides. 1. Add preventative algaecide to your swimming pool on a weekly basis if you regularly experience problems with algae growth. If you have never had an algae problem in your swimming pool, there is no need for the algaecide.
