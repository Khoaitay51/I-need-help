Algaecides are used to kill algae; whereas, algae stats are a proactive treatment that is used to prevent the growth of algae. In many cases, the difference between algaecides and algae stats is simply the dosage strength of the chemical formula used for treatment. While the chlorine you use for regular pool maintenance and water sanitization does have bactericidal and algaecidal properties, the addition of pool algaecide will enhance and prolong the effectiveness of the chlorine and can act as a valuable backup to the anti-algae effects of regular chlorination.
