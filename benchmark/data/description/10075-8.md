If you have experienced any metal staining in the past, you should use a quat or polyquat algaecide to treat your swimming pool. Polyquat Algaecide. Polyquat algaecides are a highly effective treatment option for all types of algae and do not have the staining issues of copper algaecides or the foaming issues of quats. While the chlorine you use for regular pool maintenance and water sanitization does have bactericidal and algaecidal properties, the addition of pool algaecide will enhance and prolong the effectiveness of the chlorine and can act as a valuable backup to the anti-algae effects of regular chlorination.
