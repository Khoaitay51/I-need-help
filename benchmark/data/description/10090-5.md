An increased credit limit can be a temptation to overspend. A high credit score is a valuable asset since it can lower your interest rate on important purchases like a home or a car. To increase your credit score, you'll have to be responsible with your credit. An increased credit limit will affect your FICO score, which is the industry-standard credit score created by the Fair Isaac Corporation. Amounts Owed. The amount you owe on your credit cards plays a role in determining your FICO score. Along with credit utilization, the amount of debt you carry makes up 30 percent of your total score. With an increased credit limit, you may end up incurring more debt, raising the amount that you owe.
