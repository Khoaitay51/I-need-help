Vajrakilaya Mantras for Ringu Tulku. Rinpoche has asked that we accumulate as many Vajra Kilaya mantras for him as possible. I’m not very familiar with this mantra but have found two versions as below. Please let me know if there are others. I would be grateful if you could please include this request in your prayers and daily practice. Please pass this on to other Ringu Tulku friends and students. Much laterv by 2 1/2 years. I attended the teachings on Vajrakilaya given by his holiness, the Sakya Trizen, at the University of British Columbia earlier this year (2112). The mantra he taught as an intragil part of the entire sadana is: “Om Vajra Kilikilaya Sarva Vignen Banda Hum Phat”.
