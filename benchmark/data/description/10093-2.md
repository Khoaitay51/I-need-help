As an emblem of the wrathful kīla transmission the image of the scorpion took on a strong symbolic meaning in the early development of the Nyingma or 'ancient school' of Tibetan Buddhism.... Prayer flags and stone pillars throughout the country also pierce the land. Vajrakilaya is a significant Vajrayana deity who transmutes and transcends obstacles and obscurations. Vajrakila is the divine ' thoughtform ' (Tibetan: སྤྲུལ་པ། , Wylie: sprul pa) that governs the kīla.
