Scholars such as F. A. Bischoff, Charles Hartman and Martin Boord have shown that the Tibetan literature widely asserts that the Sanskrit for their term phurba is kīlaya (with or without the long i). However, as Boord describes it, all dictionaries and Sanskrit works agree the word to be kīla (or kīlaka). Vajrakilaya is a significant Vajrayana deity who transmutes and transcends obstacles and obscurations. Vajrakila is the divine ' thoughtform ' (Tibetan: སྤྲུལ་པ། , Wylie: sprul pa) that governs the kīla.
