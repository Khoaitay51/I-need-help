The vajra is used symbolically by the dharma traditions of Buddhism, Jainism and Hinduism, often to represent firmness of spirit and spiritual power. The use of the vajra as a symbolic and ritual tool spread from India along with Indian religion and culture to other parts of Asia. 1 The word vajra is given to a fictional species of alien insects that serve as the main antagonists in the anime Macross Frontier. 2  In Soul Eater, Vajra is Asura's weapon. 3  Dorje is the name of a Brighton area rock band.
