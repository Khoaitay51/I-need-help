Vajra is a Sanskrit word meaning both thunderbolt and diamond. Additionally, it is a weapon which is used as a ritual object to symbolize both the properties of a diamond (indestructibility) and a thunderbolt (irresistible force). 1 The word vajra is given to a fictional species of alien insects that serve as the main antagonists in the anime Macross Frontier. 2  In Soul Eater, Vajra is Asura's weapon. 3  Dorje is the name of a Brighton area rock band.
