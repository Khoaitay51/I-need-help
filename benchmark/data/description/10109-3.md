Earthquakes occur because of the movements of the tectonic plates. Tectonic plates are large, flat pieces of stone that form the Earth's crust. These tectonic plates are actually moving in slow motion. Movement of the Earth's crust along the plate margins causes up-heaving and instability in land or ocean masses. Plate Tectonics is what this movement of the Earth's crust is called. The Earth's plates are constantly moving and can push together in places. Sometimes they get into a traffic jam.
