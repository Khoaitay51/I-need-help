If your having a problem of fuel leaking out of your carburetor this is usually where the problem lies. You can also remove the drain bolt from the carburetor and drain some of the fuel, if the fuel is bad you will know by the smell of it right away. If you know its a fuel problem the first step is to turn the fuel valve to off. Next remove the air box, it’s usually only held on by a couple of nuts and bolts.
