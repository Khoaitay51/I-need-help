In general, the risk for developing Lyme disease after a tick bite is only 1 - 3%. The risk varies depending on different factors: 1  The longer the tick has fed, the greater the risk. 2  Nymph ticks carry a greater risk than adult ticks, probably because they are often too small to be detected (about the size of a pinhead). The exact time of year for risk depends on a geographic region's seasons and how they affect the tick's breeding cycle.
