Anyone exposed to ticks is at risk for Lyme disease and other tick-borne diseases. Pets are also at risk. Naturally, anyone who is regularly outside in areas where tick rates are high has a greater than average risk for becoming infected. Age. The highest reported incidence of Lyme disease occurs among children 5 - 14 years old and adults 45 - 54 years old. The exact time of year for risk depends on a geographic region's seasons and how they affect the tick's breeding cycle.
