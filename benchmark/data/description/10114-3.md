Recommend on Facebook Tweet ShareCompartir. Of the many different tick species found throughout the world, only a select few bite and transmit disease to humans. These maps provide general insight into the expected distribution these human-biting ticks in the contiguous United States. Populations of ticks may be found outside noted areas. Naturally occurring populations of the ticks described below do not occur in Alaska; however, the brown dog tick is endemic in Hawaii. Note that adult ticks are the easiest to identify and male and female ticks of the same species may look different.
