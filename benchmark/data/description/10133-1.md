Uses. This medication is a hormone used in boys (before puberty) to cause the normal dropping of the testicles into the scrotum. It is also used in certain boys to help with normal sexual development. It works by causing the testes to release male sex hormones (e.g., testosterone). If either is present, do not use the liquid. If you have any questions about the use of this medication, ask your doctor or pharmacist. The dosage is based on your age, weight, medical condition, and response to treatment. For boys, use this medication usually 3 times a week or as directed by your doctor.
