Genetic engineering (GE) is the modification of an organism’s genetic composition by artificial means, often involving the transfer of specific traits, or genes, from one organism into a plant or animal of an entirely different species. Specific concerns over genetic engineering include: 1  Allergic reactions. 2  There are two concerns regarding allergic reactions. 3  The first is with known allergens. 4  For example, if genes from nuts are inserted into other foods, it could cause severe reactions in people with nut allergies.
