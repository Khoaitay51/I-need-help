1 Echocardiography (echo) is a painless test that uses sound waves to create pictures of your heart. 2  This test gives your doctor information about the size and shape of your heart and how well your heart's chambers and valves are working. An echocardiogram (echo=sound + card=heart + gram=drawing) is an ultrasound test that can evaluate the structures of the heart, as well as the direction of blood flow within it.
