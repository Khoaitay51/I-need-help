Posted on April 23, 2008 by jim. MSG stands for monosodium glutamate and is an food additive that, many years ago, got significant press about how bad it was for you. First off, it’s actually the glutamic acid in the MSG that is of concern for most people and in 1959 the FDA classified MSG as “generally recognized as safe.” However, because glutamate is absorbed very quickly in the gastointestinal tract it can spike blood plasma levels of glutamate.
