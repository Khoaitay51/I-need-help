MSG has been used as a food additive for decades. Over the years, the FDA has received many anecdotal reports of adverse reactions to foods containing MSG. These reactions — known as MSG symptom complex — include: 1  Headache. 2  Flushing. 3  Sweating. 4  Facial pressure or tightness. 5  Numbness, tingling or burning in the face, neck and other areas. 6  Rapid, fluttering heartbeats (heart palpitations). 7  Chest pain. 8  Nausea.
