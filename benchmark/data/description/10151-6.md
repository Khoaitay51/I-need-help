Monosodium glutamate (MSG) is a flavor enhancer commonly added to Chinese food, canned vegetables, soups and processed meats. These reactions — known as MSG symptom complex — include: 1  Headache. 2  Flushing. 3  Sweating. 4  Facial pressure or tightness. 5  Numbness, tingling or burning in the face, neck and other areas. 6  Rapid, fluttering heartbeats (heart palpitations). 7  Chest pain. 8  Nausea.
