On a bright Sunday morning on July 21, 1861, the first real major battle in the Civil War occurred between the North, and South. This famous battle was known as the First Bull Run which took place in Manassas, Virginia. General Joseph E. Johnston-General Johnston played a pretty much big role in the battle of First Bull Run. If he did not reach Henry House Hill in time to help Gen. Beuregard, there would've been a chance where the North would've defeated the South.
