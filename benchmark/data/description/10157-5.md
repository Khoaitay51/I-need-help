How Websense technology is protecting your organization — and you. If you have been denied access to a website because of Websense, your organization is using our technology to apply its Internet use policy. These policies are typically established to ensure a safe and productive work environment. 1 Your organization is controlling access to the category of content you are trying to access. 2  Our technology enables your organization to choose which categories to allow, limit or block access to; what time of day to allow access; and how much time to allow access to any particular category (e.g., shopping).
