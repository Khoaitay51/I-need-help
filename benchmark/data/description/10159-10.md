A typical neuron consists of a cell body (soma), dendrites, and an axon. The term neurite is used to describe either a dendrite or an axon, particularly in its undifferentiated stage. There are also other types of neurons that have consistent effects on their targets, for example excitatory motor neurons in the spinal cord that release acetylcholine, and inhibitory spinal neurons that release glycine. The distinction between excitatory and inhibitory neurotransmitters is not absolute, however.
