Summary Information: Levator Palpebrae Superioris. Levator Palpebrae Superioris: The levator palpebrae superioris is a paired extraocular muscle located in the eye area. The levator palpebrae superioris originates on the sphenoid bone and inserts on the eyelid /superior tarsus. The levator palpebrae superioris elevates or opens the eyelid.
