• ALTIMETER (noun). The noun ALTIMETER has 1 sense: 1. an instrument that measures the height above ground; used in navigation. Familiarity information: ALTIMETER used as a noun is very rare. Meronyms (parts of altimeter): aneroid; aneroid barometer (a barometer that measures pressure without using fluids). Hyponyms (each of the following is a kind of altimeter): hypsometer (an altimeter that uses the boiling point of water to determine land elevation).
