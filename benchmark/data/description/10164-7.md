An instrument that measures the height above ground; used in navigation. Classified under: Nouns denoting man-made objects. Hypernyms (altimeter is a kind of...): measuring device; measuring instrument; measuring system (instrument that shows the extent or amount or quantity or degree of something). Meronyms (parts of altimeter): aneroid; aneroid barometer (a barometer that measures pressure without using fluids). Hyponyms (each of the following is a kind of altimeter): hypsometer (an altimeter that uses the boiling point of water to determine land elevation).
