A proxy is a server that you connect to that allows you to get “outside” of your network. You connect to a proxy and route your traffic through it, so that your IP is masked and traffic appears to be coming from the proxy server. 3. Know the different types of proxies. 4. Instead of visiting a website and typing URLs into their forms, you can configure most web browsers to automatically hide your IP address through a proxy server. This type of service will require you to download and install a plug-in for your web browser.
