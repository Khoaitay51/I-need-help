The typical starting dose for escitalopram in adolescents or adults is 10 mg taken once daily. It may be taken in the morning or evening, with or without food. In patients who do not respond to the initial regimen, the dose may be increased to 20 mg after at least a week of treatment. Escitalopram has no activity at muscarinic, histaminergic, adrenergic, or GABA receptors. Occupancy of serotonin reuptake transporters by escitalopram occurs in a dose-dependent manner, increasing from 60±6% with 5 mg to 75±5% with 20 mg.
