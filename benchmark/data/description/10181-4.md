The flag of Moldova is related to the Romanian tricolor, except it has a 1:2 ratio, a lighter shade of blue, a slightly different tint of yellow, and the Moldavian coat of arms in the middle. The civil ensign of Belgium uses black rather than blue. It has a width-length ratio of 2:3. The Constitution of Romania provides that The flag of Romania is tricolor; the colors are arranged vertically in the following order from the flagpole: blue, yellow, red.
