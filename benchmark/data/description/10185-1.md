1 Digestive System The process of chemically and physically breaking down foods into simpler forms that can be absorbed is called digestion. Digestive System • The digestive system can be divided into two major parts: – (1) the alimentary canal (gastrointestinal, or digestive tract) and – (2) the accessory organs. Accessory organs The accessory organs include the – (1) teeth, – (2) tongue, – (3) salivary glands, – (4) liver, – (5) gallbladder, and – (6) pancreas.
