Digestive system: The alimentary canal and accessory digestive organs. Organs of the digestive system are divided into two main groups: organs within the alimentary canal and accessory digestive organs. The alimentary canal, which is also called the gastrointestinal (GI) tract or gut, is the entire length of tube that winds through the body from the mouth to the anus. It digests, breaks down and absorbs food through its lining into the blood.
