Actually, whether kidney failure patients can use apple cider vinegar depends on their potassium level. Potassium is a very important mineral and plays a crucial role for the properly function of all cells, tissues, and organs in the human body. Apple cider vinegar is rich in potassium and for these whose potassium level is higher than the normal range, they needs to stay away from it. Kidney failure can cause elevation of potassium level, so some kidney failure patients should avoid using apple cider vinegar.
