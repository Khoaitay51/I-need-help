Primary Structure of Proteins. The linear sequence of amino acids constitutes a protein's primary structure. The sequence is written from the amino-terminal end (the first amino acid) to the carboxyl-terminal end (the same sequence in which the protein is synthesized). There are four levels of protein structure: primary, secondary, tertiary, and quaternary. These levels also reflect their temporal sequence. Proteins are synthesized as a primary sequence and then fold into secondary → tertiary → and quaternary structures.
