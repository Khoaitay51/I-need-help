The primary structure is simply a long chain of peptides (polypeptide chain). The peptide monomers are like letters in a long, long word (the polypeptide). The word, a product … of the primary structure and the peptide bonding sequence means something-whether it be, I'm a transport protein. or I'm an enzyme!. The function of a protein is directly related to its structure. For example, a protein that fights a certain bacteria might have a shape that allows it to bind to the bacteria and then destroy it.
