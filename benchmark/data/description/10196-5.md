Like all drugs related to or derived from opium, codeine is a narcotic (also known as an opiate) and is a controlled substance. The Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) classifies codeine as a Schedule II or III controlled substance. However, low doses of codeine are still sold over-the-counter (OTC) in some states. Brand names of drugs containing codeine for pain relief include Tylenol #3, Tylenol #4, Soma Compound with Codeine, Fiorinal with Codeine, Fioricet with Codeine, and Capital and Codeine. Codeine works by blocking responses to pain, suppressing your cough reflex, and causing drowsiness.
