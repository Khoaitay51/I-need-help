If you're interested in joining the booming health care field, becoming a CNA—also known as a nurses' aid, home health aide, orderly or attendant—can be a good place to start. Learn which personal and professional traits you’ll need to succeed as a certified nursing assistant. A certified nursing assistant (CNA) job has peaks and valleys. It can be physically demanding at times with some unpleasant responsibilities, but the role also allows CNAs to build strong relationships with patients.
