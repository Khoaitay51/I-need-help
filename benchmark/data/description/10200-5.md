Answers. Best Answer: Aqueous means dissolved in water. If you see the letters (aq) after a reactant or product in a chemical equation, it means that particular substance is in solution with water as the solvent. I hope that helps. Examples: cola, saltwater, rain The word aqueous is also applied to a solution or mixture in which water is the solvent.
