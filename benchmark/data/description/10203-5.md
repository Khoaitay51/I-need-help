The ribosome has two subunits; a smaller one and a bigger one. The smaller one contains 21 proteins and one rRNA molecule, and the larger subunit contains 34 proteins and two rRNA molecules. Ribosomes are found in prokaryotes and eukaryotes (although they are a little different, they have the same function). Ribosomes are made of nucleolus. Some people might think that mRNA also helps to form the structure of the ribosome, but this is not the case. rRNA makes up the ribosome of a cell, while mRNA travels to the ribosome of a cell during protein synthesis in order to code for the amino acids.
