pla•teau. 1. a land area having a relatively level surface considerably raised above adjoining land on at least one side. 2. a period or state of little or no growth or decline, esp. one in which increase or progress ceases: to reach a plateau in one's career. A relatively stable level, period, or state: Mortgage rates declined, then reached a plateau. intr.v. pla·teaued, pla·teau·ing, pla·teaus. To reach a stable level; level off: The tension seemed to grow by degrees, then it plateaued (Tom Clancy).
