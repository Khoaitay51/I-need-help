Because I make dream interpretation a key part of my psychotherapeutic work, Ill offer some comments here about this work. Freud: The Royal Road. Sigmund Freud once called dreams the royal road to. . . the unconscious, and I think that statement will remain true in psychology forever. Of course, there are other ideas about dreams besides Freudian theories. Some persons believe that dreams have certain fixed meanings. If you dream about oranges, it means good health; if you dream about onions, it means hard work, and so on.
