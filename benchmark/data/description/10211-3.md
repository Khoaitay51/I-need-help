All you can do is put the dream into words in an imperfect attempt to describe what you experienced. So, in the end, to talk about the dream you really talk about the text of your perception of the dream. The text, of course, is language, and, as such, its already a form of interpretation of the raw experience. Of course, there are other ideas about dreams besides Freudian theories. Some persons believe that dreams have certain fixed meanings. If you dream about oranges, it means good health; if you dream about onions, it means hard work, and so on.
