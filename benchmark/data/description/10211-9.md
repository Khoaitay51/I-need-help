by G. William Domhoff. Dreams are so compelling, and they often seem so weird and strange -- surely they must have a purpose; that is, an adaptive role in the maintenance of our bodily or psychological health. We have shown that 75 to 100 dreams from a person give us a very good psychological portrait of that individual. Give us 1000 dreams over a couple of decades and we can give you a profile of the person's mind that is almost as individualized and accurate as her or his fingerprints.
