Rodelle Alcohol Free Vanilla Flavoring is made in much the same way as vanilla extract, with real, high quality vanilla beans – and a lot of them. The vanilla bean extractives are in a solution made with glycerine, water and sugar that preserves their flavor and makes them easy to add to recipes. Alcohol free vanilla flavoring can’t be called vanilla extract because there are laws that govern what extract is made up of and they require that extracts be at least 35% alcohol.
