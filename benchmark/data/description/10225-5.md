Where are totem poles carved? Today the lands of the Totem People are known as: 1) southeastern Alaska, USA; 2) coastal British Columbia [B.C.] Canada, and northern Washington state, U.S.A. That's where real totem poles come from. DEFINITION: Totems originally were a series of emblems representing a Northwest Native family-clan, their kinship system, dignity, accomplishments, prestige, adventures, stories, rights and prerogatives.
