It is pronounced as-SHey- †. Shae's language of origin is Celtic and it is predominantly used in English. Shae is a form of the name Shai. Shae is also a spelling variant (English) of the English and Irish Shea. See also the related form, the English Shaelin. Shae is an unusual baby girl name. Its usage peaked modestly in 1998 with 0.012% of baby girls being named Shae. It was #925 in rank then.
