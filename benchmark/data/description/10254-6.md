While employers do not receive your credit score with employment credit reports, your credit score can be a good reference tool for yourself as you work to build or maintain your credit. If you see an unexpected drop in your scores, it’s important to check your credit reports for errors or even signs of fraud.). If you signed a release for an employment credit check, but you know that there are errors on your report, know that you may not be able to correct those errors before the employer pulls your report. It may take weeks or even months to fix.
