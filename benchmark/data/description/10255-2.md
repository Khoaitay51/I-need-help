Jackfruit is enormous and prickly on the outside. It looks somewhat like durian, though jackfruit can be even larger. It grows in Thailand and other tropical regions of Asia, and can be purchased at Asian food markets throughout North America. 4. When jackfruit is cooked, Peel off the skin. You will see seeds-these will be tossed-and pods surrounding the seeds. The seed pods can be eaten, as well as the stringy fleshy sections between pods and skin-this is the best! Dig all this out and cook with it, or bag it and freeze for future use.
