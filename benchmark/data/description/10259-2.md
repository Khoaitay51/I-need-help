Some end-user license agreement form contracts accompany shrink-wrapped software that is presented to a user sometimes on paper or more usually electronically, during the installation procedure. The user has the choice of accepting or rejecting the agreement. Many form contracts are only contained in digital form, and only presented to a user as a click-through where the user must accept. As the user may not see the agreement until after he or she has already purchased the software, these documents may be contracts of adhesion.
