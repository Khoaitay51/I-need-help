One common criticism of end-user license agreements is that they are often far too lengthy for users to devote the time to thoroughly read them. In March 2012, the PayPal end-user license agreement was 36,275 words long and in May 2011 the iTunes agreement was 56 pages long. Many form contracts are only contained in digital form, and only presented to a user as a click-through where the user must accept. As the user may not see the agreement until after he or she has already purchased the software, these documents may be contracts of adhesion.
