Aldactone (spironolactone) is an aldosterone receptor antagonist that causes the kidneys to remove water and sodium from the body, with reduced losses of potassium. Aldactone is used to reduce edema caused by heart, liver or kidney problems, hypertension, and certain patients with hyperaldosteronism. Aldactone is available in a generic form named spironolactone. Common side effects of Aldactone include skin rash, headache, dizziness, nausea, vomiting, gas, and stomach pain.
