The familiar diuretic spironolactone has taken on new life as a treatment for left-sided congestive heart failure. Spironolactone has been shown to decrease mortality in such patients who are New York Heart Association class IV. One study 3 has shown that spironolactone improves morbidity and mortality in patients with severe heart failure. The particular advantages of spironolactone for prevention are that it is inexpensive, is taken once daily and has relatively few side effects.
