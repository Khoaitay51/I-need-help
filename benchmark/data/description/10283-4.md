Base Salary Basics. If you receive a job offer with an annual base salary of $48,000, that amount is distributed to you over the year in periodic installments. If your company makes monthly payroll distributions, for instance, you receive $4,000 in gross pay with every installment. An annual base salary is a fixed income structure with regular paychecks throughout the year. Some people receive their entire earnings through a base salary, while others receive a base salary with additional types of incentive pay.
