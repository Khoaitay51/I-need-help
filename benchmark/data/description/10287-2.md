Cala Mayor provides a quiet base close to the nightlife and shops of Palma. This laidback resort features a number of good restaurants, and don't be surprised if you catch a glimpse of the Spanish Royal family who have their summer residence here. We thought Cala Mayor Main Street was very run down and lots of bars and shops are closed and up for sale. The beach is fantastic, not over crowded, very clean and the water for small children is very safe, beach bars. All very good.
