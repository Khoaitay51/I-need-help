Cala Mayor Holidays. For a beach getaway fit for a king, look no further than a holiday in Cala Mayor. The bayside resort, located just a couple of miles west of Palma, is ideal for holidaymakers dreaming of an authentic Spanish holiday with beautiful beaches, fantastic weather and modern resort facilities. The beach is backed by restaurants, cafes and bars where holidaymakers can relax, unwind, and enjoy beautiful views of the water. Tear yourself away from the sun-drenched beach and you’ll discover ample opportunities for entertainment and exploration. A 10 minute bus ride will take you to Palma, the capital of Majorca.
