The hotels in Cala Mayor are quite high buildings and many of them have an amazing seaview to the Bay of Palma. Right next to the Playa Cala Mayor and five star Hotel Nixe Palace there is great place to see how the resort looks like from a bird's eye view. Rental car is another quick way to move around the island but it is good to know that the traffic in Cala Mayor and in Palma de Mallorca is quite busy and it might be difficult to find parking space especially in Cala Mayor during the high tourist season.
