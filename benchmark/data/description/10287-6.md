Cala Mayor. Cala Mayor is one of the oldest purpose-built tourist destinations on Mallorca. The resort surrounds the large beach of the Cala Mayor bay, which has given the resort its name. Rental car is another quick way to move around the island but it is good to know that the traffic in Cala Mayor and in Palma de Mallorca is quite busy and it might be difficult to find parking space especially in Cala Mayor during the high tourist season.
