The beach at Cala Mayor is a wide sweep of golden sand and clear blue waters, nestled between two rocky outcrops, resulting in generally calm and sheltered waters. The beach is backed by restaurants, cafes and bars where holidaymakers can relax, unwind, and enjoy beautiful views of the water. Tear yourself away from the sun-drenched beach and you’ll discover ample opportunities for entertainment and exploration. A 10 minute bus ride will take you to Palma, the capital of Majorca.
