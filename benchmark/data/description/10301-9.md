While scientists have known for some time that genetics play a role in alcoholism and other addictions, they also understand the tendency to be dependent on alcohol is more complicated than the simple presence or absence of any one gene. We now have a much clearer picture of where specific traits related to alcohol dependence overlap with specific expressions in genetic code, said Dr. R. Adron Harris, director of the UT’s Waggoner Center for Alcohol and Addiction Research and co-author of the study.
