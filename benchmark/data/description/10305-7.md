Vega /ve-ga/ [2 sylls.] as a girls' name is of Arabic origin, and the meaning of Vega is falling vulture. Name of the brightest star in the group of stars called Lyra. Kreatif forms: Vegagi, Viga. Vega is a rare first name for females but a very popular last name for both adults and children (#318 out of 150436, Top 1%). (2000 U.S. Census).
