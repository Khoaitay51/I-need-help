Definition. Midlife transition is a natural stage that happens to many of us at some point (usually at about age 40, give or take 20 years). Midlife transition can include: 1  Discontentment or boredom with life or with the lifestyle (including people and things) that have provided fulfillment for a long time. These feelings at midlife can occur naturally or result from some significant loss or change—such as divorce or the death of a parent. Coming to terms with such loss or change can be difficult enough, but when it is complicated by midlife transition, the process can feel bewildering and overwhelming.
