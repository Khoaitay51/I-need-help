Common nomenclature [edit]. In the toxicological literature, naturally-occurring tetramethylammonium (anion unspecified) is often referred to by the name tetramine. Unfortunately, this non-systematic or trivial name is also used for other chemical entities, including a toxic rodenticide (Tetramethylenedisulfotetramine). From measurements taken on molecular models, it has been estimated that the diameter of the TMA ion is ~ 0.6 nm; In more recent studies, based on more accurate physico-chemical measurements, the ionic radius for TMA is given as 0.322 nm; several thermodynamic parameters for the TMA ion are also recorded.
