The site will also be a source of information via links, about many aspects of carriage driving, equipment, people, helpers, horses and ponies. The Isle of Wight is affiliated to the National RDA and is a registered charity. I hope these pages may encourage people to become involved with the group. This site is also to inform members of the Isle of Wight RDA Carriage Driving Group what's happening within the group. All present members of the Isle of Wight Group will be invited to submit content to the site enabling everyone to communicate via this facility. The committee will use the site to keep members up to date on our activities on the Isle of Wight with other RDA groups in the UK and possibly-world wide.
