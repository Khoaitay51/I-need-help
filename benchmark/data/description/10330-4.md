Overtones Music Studio is a place where you are invited to come experience music for life. Overtones offers a unique musical experience that opens the world of music to people of all ages in a fun, creative, affordable and exciting way. Through both our group and private lesson settings a unique musical experience is waiting for you. Please contact us for any questions not answered there. Kidzrock is a music program that teaches children as young as 4 how to play an instrument in the setting of a real rock band. Band members rotate on drums, electric guitar, and keyboard as they prepare to sing and play in a rock concert.
