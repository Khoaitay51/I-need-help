What is PID? Pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) refers to infection of the uterus (womb), fallopian tubes (tubes that carry eggs from the ovaries to the uterus) and other reproductive organs that causes symptoms such as lower abdominal pain. Complications of PID, such as chronic pelvic pain and scarring are difficult to treat, but sometimes they improve with surgery. Sex partners of patients who have PID should be examined and treated promptly. Follow-up: 1  Take all medications as directed.
