Antibiotics are used to treat chlamydia. If treated, chlamydia can be cured. All sex partners should be treated to keep from getting chlamydia again. Do not have sex until you and your sex partner(s) have ended treatment. But you should be tested and treated right away to protect your reproductive health. If you have chlamydia: 1  See a doctor right away. 2  Women with chlamydia are 5 times more likely to get HIV, the virus that causes AIDS, from an infected partner.
