The word phylum (plural: phyla) is a category used to classify marine organisms. In this article, you can learn the definition of phylum, how it is used, and examples of phyla used to categorize marine life. 1 In the ocean, segmented worm species include such beautiful animals as Christmas tree worms. 2  Arthropoda-Many familiar types of seafood, such as lobsters and crabs, are arthropods. 3  Arthropods have a hard exoskeleton, a segmented body, and jointed legs. 4  Brachiopoda-this phylum includes lamp shells.
