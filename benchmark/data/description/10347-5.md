Parole officers work with people who have been released from jail and are serving parole, to help them re-enter society. Parole officers monitor post-release offenders and provide them with information on various resources, such as substance-abuse counseling or job training, to aid in their rehabilitation. Probation officers and correctional treatment specialists typically do the following: 1  Evaluate offenders to determine the best course of rehabilitation. 2  Provide offenders with resources, such as job training. 3  Test offenders for drugs and offer substance-abuse counseling.
