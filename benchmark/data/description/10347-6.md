Salary. According to the BLS, the median wage for probation and correctional treatment specialists is $47,200 per year, or $22.69 per hour. The lowest 10 percent of officers earn $30,920 a year, while the highest 10 percent earn more than $80,750 a year. The highest-paid officers are those employed by state governments. The BLS defines parole officers as a type of probation officer tasked with monitoring offenders who have served a complete jail sentence followed by a conditioned release.
