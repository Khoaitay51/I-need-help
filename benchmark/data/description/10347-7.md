Meetings. A primary duty for a probation officer is to meet with his assigned offenders on a weekly or monthly basis. The court designates how often each offender must meet with his officer. At these meetings, the officer reviews the requirements established for the probation. The probation officer is part of a team of people who work to monitor and manage the offender during probation. He typically maintains relationships with other public, private or nonprofit organizations in the community to help get the probationer assistance as needed.
