Essential Information. Probation officers work with people who were convicted of crimes and sentenced to probation instead of incarceration. The work of a probation officer often involves monitoring offenders, coordinating rehabilitation services and arranging job training. Most probation officers must have a bachelor's degree. Probation Officer Salary and Career Outlook. The BLS groups probation officers together with correctional treatment specialists and estimates a one percent decline in jobs for those workers in the decade 2012-2022.
