Parole officers are state employees who are responsible for monitoring recently released criminal offenders. According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, or BLS, positions within probation and parole departments are expected to grow 18 percent between 2010 and 2020, which is about as fast as average. The BLS defines parole officers as a type of probation officer tasked with monitoring offenders who have served a complete jail sentence followed by a conditioned release.
