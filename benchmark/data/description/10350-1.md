The day-to-day functions of a nurse practitioner will depend strongly on the specialty he or she has chosen to pursue, as well as the clinical setting. However, it is possible to lay out the most common tasks and requirements for the career. At minimum, a nurse practitioner must earn a Master’s of Science degree in nursing before beginning work in the NP role. This degree and a nurse practitioner credential, are only available to those students who have already completed the requirements to work as a registered nurse (RN).
