Nurse practitioners (NPs) are registered nurses with graduate education in nursing. Most NPs have a master’s degree, which requires at least two years of full-time study beyond the bachelor’s degree in nursing. Depending upon the type of practice, working schedules may be a conventional work week or may include weekends and holidays and/or being available on call after hours. As the numbers of primary care physicians decrease and the demand for health care services increases, nurse practitioners (NPs) are highly recruited.
