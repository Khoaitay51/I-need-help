Nurse practitioners are expected to be excellent counselors, communicating well with both patients and their families on care plans and prognoses. Ultimately, what sets nurse practitioners apart from primary care physicians and other advanced practice registered nurses (APRNs) is their holistic approach to patient care. At minimum, a nurse practitioner must earn a Master’s of Science degree in nursing before beginning work in the NP role. This degree and a nurse practitioner credential, are only available to those students who have already completed the requirements to work as a registered nurse (RN).
