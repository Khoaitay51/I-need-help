Today's economic environment puts procurement professionals in the driver's seat when dealing with their suppliers and vendors. It is a buyer's market and that can mean up to a 30-40% savings that goes, dollar-for-dollar, to your bottom line. The volatility of today's economic environment, the increasing competitiveness of global markets, and the risks arising out of the credit crunch, make it imperative for organizations to focus on leading edge procurement strategies to maximize cost reductions.
