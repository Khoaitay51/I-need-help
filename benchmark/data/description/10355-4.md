Some states classify divorce records -- but not the decree itself -- as vital records. You may be able to obtain a copy of the divorce record from your state department of public health and then use the case number and information on this record to obtain copies of the decree itself. Make sure to ask for a certified record rather than just a copy. If you are not a party to the divorce and the county records office will not provide you with the decree, get a signed statement from both parties to the divorce authorizing you to view the decree.
