1 A flower head or capitulum is a very contracted raceme in which the single sessile flowers share are borne on an enlarged stem. 2  It is characteristic of Dipsacaceae. 3  A catkin or ament is a scaly, generally drooping spike or raceme. 1 A spike is a type of raceme with flowers that do not have a pedicel. 2  A racemose corymb is an unbranched, indeterminate inflorescence that is flat-topped or convex due to their outer pedicels which are progressively longer than inner ones.
