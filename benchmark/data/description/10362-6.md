* Antimony Pentafluoride is a CORROSIVE CHEMICAL and contact can severely irritate and burn the skin and eyes with possible eye damage. * Exposure to Antimony Pentafluoride can irritate the nose, mouth, throat and lungs causing coughing, wheezing and/or shortness of breath. * Antimony Pentafluoride can cause headaches, nausea, vomiting and loss of appetite. * Repeated high exposure can cause deposits of Fluorides in the bones and teeth, a condition called “ Fluorosis .” * The above health effects do NOT occur at the level of Fluoride used in water for preventing cavities in teeth. IDENTIFICATION Antimony Pentafluoride is an oily, colorless liquid.
