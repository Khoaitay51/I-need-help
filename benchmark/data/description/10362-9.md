Antimony pentafluoride is a colorless thick liquid. It is a powerful oxidizing agent and very reactive. It can help fluorine to oxidize oxygen. This is not normal as oxygen normally oxidizes other things; it does not get oxidized. Antimony pentafluoride, also known as antimony(V) fluoride, is a chemical compound. Its chemical formula is SbF 5. It has five antimony-fluorine bonds. The antimony is in its +5 oxidation state
