How soil microorganisms directly or indirectly affect plant growth and health determines if they are considered. beneficial, harmful, or insignificant to plants. Microbes that harm plants are plant pathogens since the harm that they. cause is considered disease. On the other hand, beneficial microorganisms can either enhance plant growth, suppress. plant diseases, or both. The purpose of this article is to help make better sense of the different types of beneficial. effects and some of the beneficial microorganisms responsible for producing these effects.
