Types of Harmful Bacteria. Gardeners refer to beneficial soil bacteria as probiotics. Harmful bacteria are called proteobacteria. A very large group, proteobacteria includes many bacteria that also affect humans, such as Vibro cholarae, the root cause of cholera. Beneficial bacteria can be fixed into the soil to help reduce harmful bacteria. In most instances of bacterium-based disease, the recommended action is to avoid planting the specific species of plant affected for several seasons in the affected area.
