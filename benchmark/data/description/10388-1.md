You can send city names (with or without state/country), postal codes, and state or country names. BatchGeo can even take common landmarks and business names. Most of what you type into Google Maps you can include as a location in BatchGeo. 1 Generally you would pick around 50 feet from the address block. 2  There is no way to know for sure how far back the building or building entrance is located, but 50 feet is usually close. 3  Finally, the geocoder has to choose a side of the street to place the address.
