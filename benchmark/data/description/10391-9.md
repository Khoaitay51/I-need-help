Exbury Gardens is part of a historic estate that has played a central role in the community for many years. Lionel de Rothschild first moved to Exbury in back 1912 and in 1919 bought the Exbury Estate and began creating the gardens as we see them today. The best known attractions on the estate are the gardens and the steam railway allowing visitors to see the gardens from a bygone era. The estate is 2,000 acres in size and contains farms, along with a number of agriculture and woodland activities.
