In American artistic gymnastics there are levels 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9, and 10. A gymnast is not allowed to skip a level, but may compete in more than one each year. These levels were designed to train gymnasts with specific skills that prepare them for elite competitions. Even if you never mange to reach the elite level, there are still many things that you can do with your gymnastics. You can still compete and learn exciting new skills.
