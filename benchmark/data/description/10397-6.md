Any gymnasts can ask to be tested for the elite level. Every region hosts one or more annual Elite Program Qualification tests. The test consists of a physical abilities test, compulsory, and optional test. If a gymnast succeeds by testing, they automatically qualify to compete in one to four elite meets per year. Even if you never mange to reach the elite level, there are still many things that you can do with your gymnastics. You can still compete and learn exciting new skills.
