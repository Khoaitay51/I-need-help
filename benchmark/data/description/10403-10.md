Benefits to Employers. Employers favor arbitration over court action because the arbitration process is more cost-effective and less time-consuming. While arbitration is a formal process, the rules and procedures are less onerous than those of a court of law. The right of appeal from an arbitrator's decision is limited, offering employers increased certainty. BananaStock/BananaStock/Getty Images. If a dispute arises between an employee and his employer, either party can raise a court action to resolve the issue. However, court actions are costly and time-consuming. Arbitration is an alternative method of dispute resolution.
