Method 1 of 2: Send Certified Mail from Your Post Office. 1. Visit a local post office and obtain a Certified Mail Form 3800. 1  This form contains a green and white sticker that includes a barcode, which will allow you to track your mail through the USPS. 2  The form also contains a perforated receipt, which serves as proof that you mailed the item. 1 Both first-class and priority mail can be sent via certified mail. 2  First-class mail includes envelopes and packages that weigh 13 ounces or less. 3  Priority mail service offers delivery in a timely and expedited fashion, generally within two to three days.
