A certified letter should be written just as any other business letter, but you should note above the name and address of the person to whom it is addressed Sent Certif … ied Mail, Article No. *********** The Article Number should correspond to the Certified Mail Receipt form (PS Form 3800) from the USPS. You do not mark the letter itself Certified, although you may note in the body text that you are mailing the letter Certified. Seal and address your letter as usual. At the Post Office, obtain and fill out a Certified Mail form. You likely also want to use a Return Receipt form
