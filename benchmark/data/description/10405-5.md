A certified letter should be written just as any other business letter, but you should note above the name and address of the person to whom it is addressed Sent Certif … ied Mail, Article No. *********** The Article Number should correspond to the Certified Mail Receipt form (PS Form 3800) from the USPS. A form is filled out at the post office that contains the name of the sender and the person/place where the letter is sent. The sender receives a receipt and can request they be notified when delivery is made. When delivery is made the a person must sign for accepting the mail.
