Globe Theatre Interior-the Stage The Dimensions of the Globe stage cannot be specified. Stage dimensions of Elizabethan theaters varied from 20 foot wide 15 foot deep to 45 feet to 30 feet. The stage was raised - 3 to 5 feet and supported by large pillars. There was no seating-the cheapest part of the Globe Theater and the audience had to stand. The stage structure projected halfway into the ' yard ' where the commoners (groundlings) paid 1 penny to stand to watch the play.
