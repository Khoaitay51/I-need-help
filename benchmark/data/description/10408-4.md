Older toilets look ugly and can use up to 7 gallons of water per flush. Replacing of an old, inefficient toilet with a new water-efficient model can help reduce the amount of water used for each flush by between 50 and 75 percent, according to the Tualatin Valley Water District. 5. Contact a local charity such as Habitat for Humanity, that accepts used, working building materials, such as toilets. To get rid of your old toilet, contact your local Habitat for Humanity ReStore to find out where to drop off your old toilet.
