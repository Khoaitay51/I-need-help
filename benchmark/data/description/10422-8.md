Fishbone diagrams also known as Cause & Effect diagrams or Ishakawa diagrams are used during brainstorming session of a Lean Six Sigma Project. The tool will help you organize root causes and possible failure modes. You will want to use the 5 Whys techniques to continually drill down to root causes of defects.
