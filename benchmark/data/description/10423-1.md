ANZAC Cove. To visit ANZAC Cove is to stand at the very heart of Gallipoli history. On these shores, almost 100 years ago, stories of tragedy, heroism and camaraderie were forged. Today, ANZAC Cove is revered for its defining significance to the Gallipoli campaign. It is at this diminutive cove the World War I ANZAC Corps made their first Gallipoli landing on 25 April 1915.
