The flour has a stronger flavor when compared to other types of gluten-free flour such as white rice or tapioca flours. You may want to experiment with your recipes using mesquite meal. A mere two tablespoons per cup of dry ingredients will unveil its unique taste. Recipes. Given its unique characteristics, you can cook with mesquite flour in several ways, either as a gluten-free flour substitute or as a spice. Like other flours such as coconut flour, mesquite meal will absorb some liquid in your recipes for which you will need to compensate.
