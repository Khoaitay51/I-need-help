Some minor adjustments may need to be made to a recipe when substituting honey for sugar: 1. Use equal amounts of honey for sugar up to one cup. Over one cup, replace each cup of sugar with 2/3 to 3/4 cup over honey depending upon the sweetness desired. 2. Lower the baking temperature 25 degrees and watch your time carefully since products with honey brown faster. 3. In recipes using more than one cup honey for sugar, it may be necessary to reduce liquids by 1/4 cup per cup of honey. 4. In baked goods, add 1/4 teaspoon of baking soda per cup of honey if baking soda is not already included in the recipe.
