Mesquite flour offers yet another option for making gluten-free recipes using a type of flour which does not contain wheat or gluten. Mesquite has a long history of use, primarily as food by Native Americans. Its lengthy history of usage is similar to many alternative flours. Recipes. Given its unique characteristics, you can cook with mesquite flour in several ways, either as a gluten-free flour substitute or as a spice. Like other flours such as coconut flour, mesquite meal will absorb some liquid in your recipes for which you will need to compensate.
