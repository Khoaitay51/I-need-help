Like other gluten-free flours, mesquite flour offers a superior nutritional value to regular white flour. A 1/4-cup serving contains 137 calories, which is slightly higher than the 114 calories for white, all-purpose flour. The flour is high in iron and calcium. Recipes. Given its unique characteristics, you can cook with mesquite flour in several ways, either as a gluten-free flour substitute or as a spice. Like other flours such as coconut flour, mesquite meal will absorb some liquid in your recipes for which you will need to compensate.
