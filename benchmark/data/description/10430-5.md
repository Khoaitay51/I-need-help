The notion that all serial killers are men is simply not true, but it's understandable why the public would hold this erroneous belief. As late as 1998, a highly regarded former FBI profiler said, There are no female serial killers.. The news and entertainment media also perpetuate the stereotypes that all serial offenders are male and that women do not engage in horrible acts of violence. The reality concerning the gender of serial killers is quite different than the mythology of it. Although there have been many more male serial killers than females throughout history, the presence of female serial killers is well documented in the crime data.
