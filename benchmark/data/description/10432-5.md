First trimester ultrasound examination is done to evaluate the presence, size, number and location of the pregnancy. Ultrasound can also be used for first-trimester sonographic genetic screening, as well as screening for any abnormalities of your uterus or cervix. 1 If your health care provider suspects a multiple pregnancy, an ultrasound might be done to confirm the number of babies. 2  Evaluate your baby's growth. 3  Your health care provider can use ultrasound to determine whether your baby is growing at a normal rate.
