Laura is a female given name in Latin, Western Europe and North America whose meaning ( Bay Laurel ) translates to victor, and an early hypocorism from Laurel and Lauren. In Europe, the name Laura remains as popular as it was in the past. In the early 21st century in the late 2000s, it ranked within the top 10 in countries such as Austria, Belgium, Croatia, Denmark, Estonia, Greenland, Luxembourg, Spain, and Switzerland.
