Laura. Gender: Female. Usage: Laura, of Latin origin, is a very popular first name. It is more often used as a girl (female) name. History and Origin. Laura is a female given name in Latin, Western Europe and North America whose meaning (“Bay Laurel”) translates to victor, and an early hypocorism from Laurel and Lauren. Italian, Spanish, and English: feminine form of the Late Latin male name Laurus ‘Laurel’
