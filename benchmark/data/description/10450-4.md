tawse. pl.n. Chiefly Scots. 1. A whip or leather thong used to drive a spinning top. 2. A leather whip divided at the end into strips, formerly used to punish children: Solider Aristotle played the taws / Upon the bottom of a king of kings (William Butler Yeats). 1. (Education) a leather strap having one end cut into thongs, formerly used as an instrument of punishment by a schoolteacher. vb. 2. (Education) to punish (someone) with or as if with a tawse; whip. [C16: probably plural of obsolete taw strip of leather; see taw²].
