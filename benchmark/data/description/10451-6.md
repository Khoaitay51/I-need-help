Kazakstan flag laws. Article 4. National Flag of the Republic of Kazakhstan is a rectangular piece of cloth of sky-blue colour bearing (in its centre) a design of the sun with rays, with a soaring eagle undeneath. All along the flagstaff there runs a vertical band of national ornament. First variant of the Kazakhstan flag (1992). First flag of Republic of Kazakhstan adopted by Law of June 4, 1992 had red national ornament. This Law was officially published in the newspaper 'The Kazakhstan truth' #130-131. The author of this flag-Shaken Nijazbekov.
