The most common type of infections are cystitis (bladder infection), pyelonephritis (kidney infection), prostatitis (prostate infection), and urethritis (inflammation of the tube, the urethra, that drains the bladder to the outside of the body). Other causes of dysuria include: 1  Trauma: local injury or irritation due to catheter placement or sexual contact. 2  Anatomic obstructions/malformations: obstruction due to an enlarged prostate or urethral stricture. 3  Pain due to external lesions on the genitalia: Urine touching the lesion causes pain.
