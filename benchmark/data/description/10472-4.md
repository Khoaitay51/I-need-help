The Deltoid Muscles (also known as Deltoids or Delts for short) are a group of triangular muscles which most people would recognize as the shoulder muscles and are located on top of the common shoulder joint for the Scapula, Clavicle and Humerus. 3.The posterior division, also known as the posterior deltoid, is located on the backside of the shoulder joint.
