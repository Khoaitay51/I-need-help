Humans have three pairs of major salivary glands. These consist of the parotid glands, submandibular glands, and sublingual glands. The parotid glands are located anterior to the ear, over the angle of the jaw and extending into the upper neck. The submandibular glands are smaller and are located under the jaw. Tumors of the parotid glands, the largest salivary glands, have only a 20% likelihood of malignancy, whereas tumors of the minor salivary glands have an 80% likelihood of being malignant. The most common type of benign salivary gland tumor is a benign mixed tumor, or pleomorphic adenoma.
