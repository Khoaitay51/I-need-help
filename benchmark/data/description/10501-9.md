Most positions require a bachelor's degree in energy engineering, mechanical engineering, or another relevant field of engineering from an ABET-accredited program. However, many jobs require graduate degrees, and some require doctorates. Some academic programs offer concentrations in environmental engineering. A wind energy engineer is a scientist who designs wind farms or their components. Alternatively, he or she may supervise the manufacture of rotor blades or other components. Many different types of engineers play a role in wind energy projects.
