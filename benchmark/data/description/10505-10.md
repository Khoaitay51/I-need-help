For the first time in 20 years, the NHL's playoff format is primarily based on divisions, not conferences. No more 1 vs. 8, 2 vs. 7, etc. Change is hard, and sure, the new way is a little bit complicated. How teams qualify. 1  There are still 16 overall teams in the Stanley Cup Playoffs, eight from the East and eight from the West. 2  The top three teams in each division automatically qualify for the postseason. 3  The 4 remaining teams will qualify via wild card spots: two in the East, two in the West.
