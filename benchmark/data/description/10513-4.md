According to scholars and Egyptologists, the ankh's most prevalent meaning involves the idea of life. Some believe that it symbolizes male and female genitalia united. Other theories included the ankh as a symbol for a type of sandal. More popularly, it is believed to be a symbol of everlasting life or eternity. When depicted in the hands of Egyptian deities, the ankh is read as a scepter that can resurrect the dead.
