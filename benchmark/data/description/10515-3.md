1 Inorganic arsenic is arsenic alone or combined with inorganic substances that are very toxic to most biologic systems, including humans. 2  Organic arsenic is arsenic combined with organic substances and may be non-toxic or far less toxic to many biologic systems than inorganic arsenic. 1 Chronic poisoning has a better outcome. 2  Arsenic is found in groundwater, many chemicals, and foods. 3  If arsenic is in the organic form, it is likely nontoxic or weakly toxic to humans, but inorganic arsenic can also be found in similar locations and materials and in high concentrations in industrial processes.
