1 Symptoms of arsenic poisoning vary with the type and concentration of the poison. 2  Inorganic arsenic may cause abdominal pains, destruction of red blood cells (hemolysis), shock, and death quickly. 1 Chronic poisoning has a better outcome. 2  Arsenic is found in groundwater, many chemicals, and foods. 3  If arsenic is in the organic form, it is likely nontoxic or weakly toxic to humans, but inorganic arsenic can also be found in similar locations and materials and in high concentrations in industrial processes.
