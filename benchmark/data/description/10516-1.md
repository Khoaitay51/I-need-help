There are two chemicals used in this reaction, 1,6-hexadiamine (which is in a water solution) and adipoyl chloride (which is in a hexane solution). Think back to what you learned in this course about solutions and intermolecular forces. Adipoyl chloride do not donate H-bonds, and may only accept two. Because of the 1) differences in polarity, and 2) primarily 1,6-hexanediamine's ability to form hydrogen bonds with itself they will never mix together.
