Reckless Endangerment is a serious offense that can damage your record and your reputation. In Tennessee, a person may be convicted of the crime of Reckless Endangerment if the state prosecutor proves beyond a reasonable doubt that the person: 1  Recklessly engages in conduct; 2  That places or may place another person; 3  In imminent danger of death; 4  Or serious bodily injury. However, if committed with a deadly weapon, Reckless Endangerment becomes a Felony Offense.
