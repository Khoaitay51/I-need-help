The distance between the air in the lungs and the blood in the capillaries is very small, and this allows molecules of oxygen and carbon dioxide to transfer across the membranes. The exchange of the air between the lungs and blood are through the arterial and venous system. Arteries and veins both carry and move blood throughout the body, but the process for each is very different. 1  Arteries carry blood from the heart to the body. 2  Veins return blood from the body to the heart. 1 Pulmonary embolism (PE) is the blockage of a pulmonary artery or one of its branches by a blood-borne blood clot or foreign material.
