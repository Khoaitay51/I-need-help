The definition of pulmonary hypertension is straightforward: it is an abnormally raised blood pressure in the blood vessels that go to the lungs and return oxygenated blood to the heart and, from there, to the rest of the body. Hypertension in this respiratory loop of our circulation is a progressive and serious condition. Pulmonary hypertension is not related to general hypertension, which is a separate condition of systemic high blood pressure, which affects the wider circulatory system and is measured using an arm cuff.
