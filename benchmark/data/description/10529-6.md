An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure. You use this proverb to say that it is much easier to do something to prevent a problem than to deal with it after it has happened. This vaccine costs $25, but it would cost you at least $1500 if you caught the disease and had to be treated in a hospital. An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure.
