Germany is a founding member of the European Union and one of the first-wave countries to adopt the euro on 1 January 1999. Status: Euro-area member since 1 January 1999. Fixed conversion rate : €1 = 1.95583 DEM. Adoption of the euro: The euro banknotes and coins were introduced in Germany on 1 January 2002, after a transitional period of three years when the euro was the official currency but only existed as 'book money'.
