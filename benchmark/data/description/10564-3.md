Bukowski was being investigated by the FBI for his involvement in the underground Los Angeles newspaper Open City. Bukowski had a recurring column called “Notes From a Dirty Old Man.” The tabloid was published weekly from 1967 to 1969. In an interview with the FBI, Bukowski stated that by Feb. 8, 1968, he had contributed 41 articles to Open City. He also said that each column was an “intermixture of fiction and fact” and that the prose is “highly romanticized to give the story more juice.” One of the columns is included in the 1968 file.
