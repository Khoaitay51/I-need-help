Acidic solutions of permanganate are reduced to the faintly pink manganese(II) ion (Mn 2+) and water. In neutral solution, permanganate is only reduced by three electrons to give MnO 2, wherein Mn is in a +4 oxidation state. It is now replaced with other oxidizers, due to the instability of permanganate mixtures. Aqueous solutions of KMnO 4 have been used together with T-Stoff (i.e. 80% hydrogen peroxide) as propellant for the rocket plane Messerschmitt Me 163. In this application, it was known as Z-Stoff.
