What is it. Potassium permanganate (PP) is a compound that is used by aquarists for the disinfecting of plants or ornaments before placement into aquariums. It is used to remove organic build-up in tank water, and kill bacteria and fungi. It is also employed to treat some parasites that infect fish. Potassium permanganate is the chemical compound KMnO4. The salt is also known as permanganate of potash and Condy's crystals. The permanganate ion is a strong oxidizing agent. It dissolves in water to give deep purple/pink solutions, evaporation of which gives prismatic purple-black glistening crystals (see picture below). 1  Potassium permanganate stains skin and clothing and should be handled with care
