Stool odor is the smell associated with stools, or bowel movements. Stools normally have an unpleasant odor. Stools are formed in the large intestine (colon), which is part of the digestive system. However, abnormally foul-smelling stools may also be a sign of a disease, disorder or condition. Examples include celiac disease, Crohn’s disease, cystic fibrosis, and intestinal infections.
