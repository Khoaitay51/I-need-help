Brachiopod shells come in a variety of shapes and sizes. Sometimes the bottom valve is convex like the top valve, but in many species the bottom valve is concave or occasionally conical. In some brachiopods (see photo below), the top valve is concave and the bottom is convex. Another distinctive feature of all brachiopods is that their valves are bilaterally symmetrical--in other words, the right half is a mirror image of the left half. Humans also are bilaterally symmetrical.
