Two specimens of the brachiopod genus Reticulatia, which has a concave top valve (left) and a concave bottom valve. These Pennsylvanian fossils are from the Americus Limestone Member of the Foraker Limestone in Greenwood County, Kansas. Another distinctive feature of all brachiopods is that their valves are bilaterally symmetrical--in other words, the right half is a mirror image of the left half. Humans also are bilaterally symmetrical.
