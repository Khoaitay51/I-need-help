Where you can get it. The contraceptive implant is a small flexible tube about 40mm long that's inserted under the skin of your upper arm. It's inserted by a trained professional, such as a doctor, and lasts for three years. The implant stops the release of an egg from the ovary by slowly releasing progestogen into your body. 1 In the first year after the implant is fitted, your periods may become irregular, lighter, heavier or longer. 2  This usually settles down after the first year. 3  A common side effect of the implant is that your periods stop (amenorrhoea) .
