Sprinkle and rub seasoning (Cajun/Creole Seasoning) on all sides of turkey legs. Bake in a preheated 400F oven for 20 minutes. Turn oven heat to 300F and cook for 15 to 45 minutes until 165F internally (use meat thermometer or poke with fork to see if juices run clear). Cover with foil. Place directly on rack in oven with turkey legs, then turn oven up to 350F until soft and tender (took me 20 more minutes). OR if baking alone, bake at 350F until cooked-will take 35 minutes or more depending on size.
