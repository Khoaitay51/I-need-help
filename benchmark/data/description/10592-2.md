Place the drumsticks in the oven and bake them for 2 hours. Turn them over halfway through the cooking process, ensuring that you cover them tightly again after turning. They are ready to eat when the meat is tender and falling off the bone, and they've reached an internal temperature of 155 degrees Fahrenheit. You can purchase smoked turkey legs at some butcher shops and at many grocery stores, or you can make them at home. According to the USDA Nutrition Database, a 3-ounce serving of smoked turkey leg, bones and skins removed, contains 126 calories, 17.58 grams of protein and 627 grams of sodium.
