JACKETING AND COLLARS IN STRUCTURE STRENGTHENING. Jacketing is the process whereby a section of an existing structural member is restored to original dimensions or increased in size by encasement using suitable materials. These are usually used to provide increased support to the slab or beam at the top of the column. The form for the jacket consists of timber, corrugated metal, precast concrete, rubber, fiberglass, or special fabric; and may be permanent in some cases.
