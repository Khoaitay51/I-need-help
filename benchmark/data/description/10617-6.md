Introduction. The spleen is a fist-sized organ found in the upper left side of your abdomen, behind your stomach and left ribs. Some people are born without a spleen or need to have it removed because of disease or injury. The spleen has a few important functions: 1  it fights any invading germs in the blood (the spleen contains infection-fighting white blood cells). 2  it controls the level of white blood cells, red blood cells and platelets (platelets are small cells that form blood clots).
