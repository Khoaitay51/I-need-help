A stroke is an event in which blood flow to the brain is interrupted or blocked, causing brain cells to become damaged and die. Strokes caused by blood clots are called ischemic strokes. Lacunar stroke is a type of ischemic stroke that occurs when blood flow to one of the small arterial vessels deep within the brain becomes blocked. According to the National Institutes of Health (NIH) , lacunar stroke represents about one-fifth of all strokes.
