Lacunar Infarct. without comments. A lacunar infarct is a type of silent stroke, which means it may not produce any outside symptoms. In many cases, patients are unaware when they suffer a lacunar stroke. Despite not causing any symptoms, this type of stroke still damages the brain and puts the patient at risk of major stroke attacks in future. In a lacunar stroke, there is blockage of small arteries branching from the main artery in the brain. This can cause significant damage to the cells in small parts of the brain and lead to death of certain parts of the brain tissue. The most common cause of a lacunar infarct is high blood pressure.
