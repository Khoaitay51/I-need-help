1 A physical change, such as a state change or dissolving, does not create a new substance, but a chemical change does. 2  In a chemical reaction, the atoms and molecules that interact with each other are called reactants. 3  In a chemical reaction, the atoms and molecules produced by the reaction are called products. Explain that in most chemical reactions, two or more substances, called reactants, interact to create different substances called products. Tell students that burning a candle is an example of a chemical reaction.
