Method. 1  Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). 2  Place the duck on a clean chopping board and remove any excess fat from the neck and body cavities. 3  Rinse the duck thoroughly, inside and out, under a cold running tap and pat dry with a paper towel. 1 Rub salt and pepper into the skin of the duck, inside as well as outside. 2  Position the duck on a rack placed in a roasting tray, breast side up. 3  Pour a few cups of boiling water over the duck and into the roasting tray and place into the preheated oven.
