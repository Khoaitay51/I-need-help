Utilities. To run a dog daycare business, you need lights, electricity and water for the dogs and staff. You may also have to pay for an office trash disposal service, which includes dog waste disposal. Utility deposits for a dog daycare, according to Paws, can range from $500 to $1,000. Permits and Licensing Fees. The state and county where your dog daycare business operates may require permits and licenses to run a dog-related business. Since states and counties vary, permit and licensing fees can range from $500 to $2,500. The average cost of permit and licensing fees is estimated at $1,000.
