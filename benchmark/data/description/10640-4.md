The bond is known as a peptide bond. the acid hydrolysis reaction breaks apart this bond, and replaces the 'loose ends' with an-OH group on the carbon and a-H on the nitrogen. OH + H = H2O, so formation of water is involved in the breaking apart og the peptide bond. Thus the name hydrolysis (hydro-water, lysis-break).
