Comfrey (also comphrey) is a common name for plants in the genus Symphytum. 1 Comfrey liquid fertilizer – can be produced by either rotting leaves down in rainwater for 4–5 weeks to produce a ready-to-use  comfrey tea , or by stacking dry leaves under a weight in a container with a hole in the base. 2  When the leaves decompose a thick black comfrey concentrate is collected.
