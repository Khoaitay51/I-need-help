Most hay is grown in the cool season but, where moisture is available in summer, Sudan grass and fodder sorghums can be grown. Oat is a major hay crop, especially in North Africa, often for sale to transhumant flocks or for transport to the desert fringe, and it can be grown on most well-drained soils. In arid areas, of course, all crops are grown under irrigation. Hay is a common crop in arid parts of Asia. Rainfed, sown hay in semi-arid areas is commonest on large farms, since yields are generally erratic through variations in rainfall. Lucerne can be grown on suitable soils.
