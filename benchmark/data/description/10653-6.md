Alfalfa Seed Types. In the United States, farms grow alfalfa to use as hay for cattle and other livestock food. Alfalfa is a common forage crop that provides the livestock with the nutrition they need to thrive and grow. There are many types of alfalfa varieties offered by United States seed companies. 1 Affinity alfalfa can grow in a very cold climate and strong against root rot disease. 2  If this variety is cut very close to the ground, it can grow back for another harvest. 3  Affinity is also resistant to many varieties of insect’s pests that usually give other alfalfa seeds problems.
