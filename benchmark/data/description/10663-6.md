The thyroid gland is part of the endocrine system and is responsible for producing and releasing thyroid hormones into the bloodstream. Collectively, T3 and T 4 are referred to as the thyroid hormones. Twenty percent of the body’s triiodothyronine is made by the thyroid gland; the other 80% comes from thyroxine converted by organs such as the liver or kidneys. The thyroid gland also produces calcitonin from cells called C-cells.
