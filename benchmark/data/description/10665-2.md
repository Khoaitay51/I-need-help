Turmeric Constituents and Effects. The active ingredient in turmeric is an antioxidant plant pigment called curcumin. Turmeric also contains a compound called zingiberene, which is found in its relative, ginger, as well. Turmeric Features. Turmeric comes from the plant botanically known as Curcuma longa. Widely cultivated in Asia, India and China, turmeric features oblong leaves and funnel-shaped, dull-yellow flowers. The rhizome -- yellowish on the outside and brilliant orange on the inside -- is dried and powdered to yield the spice
