Muscle tissue is made of excitable cells that are capable of contraction. Of all of the different tissue types (muscle, epithelial, connective, and nervous), muscle tissue is the most abundant in most animals. 1 Skeletal muscle, which is attached to bones by tendons, is associated with the body's voluntary movements. 2  Skeletal muscle is striated muscle. 3  Unlike cardiac muscle, the cells are not branched. 4  Visceral (Smooth) Muscle.
