Tweet. Anyone that has found hornworms in the vegetable garden knows they can literally destroy a plant in no time. These caterpillars can be a trifling nuisance for tomatoes, peppers, and some herbs. One hornworm can strip a plant of it’s leaves in just a few hours – they are eating machines. You can also plant a big patch of dill as a trap crop on one end of your vegetable garden. Hornworms absolutely love dill, and might go for the dill plants instead of the tomatoes or peppers. Diversions are legal when fighting garden pests.
