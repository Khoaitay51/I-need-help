If hornworms are found immediately remove them from the plant and either drop them in a container of soapy water or smoosh them. One thing to watch for when handling hornworms, when you grab them they will turn around and eject a dark brown or black liquid on your hand. You can also plant a big patch of dill as a trap crop on one end of your vegetable garden. Hornworms absolutely love dill, and might go for the dill plants instead of the tomatoes or peppers. Diversions are legal when fighting garden pests.
