Either pick them off by hand or go to the garden center and get a bacteria culture called BT. It is a natural product and it eats the hornworms! Kills them overnight. I use it (Too many tomato plants to hand pick the worms) because it isn't a poison. There is one time when it is good to leave the tomato hornworm on the plant, and that is if you see a number of sort of white oval things stuck to them. Those would be the eggs of a parasitic wasp-nature's way of keeping things in balance.
