Helping teenagers deal with stress can be an emotional roller coaster. Just like adults, stress affects the health of a teen. Some sources of stress that can cause stress is: a change of schools, school demands are too much to handle, problems at home, or problems with other teenagers within the school. Anything like drugs, excessive alcohol use or smoking can cause health problems later on down the road He or she may tend to be more accident. The physical signs of stress that a teen may show are lip biting, nail biting, tooth grinding, and palm sweating.
