Adults can help children and teens with stress in many ways. Two important ways are creating a low-stress environment and helping them develop positive coping skills. The following can help develop a low-stress environment: 1  Acknowledge your child's feelings. It is important to help children develop positive coping skills, as these skills are often carried into adult life. You can help by: 1  Providing a good example. 2  Keep calm and express your anger in appropriate ways. 3  Think through plans to reduce stress, and share them with your family. 4  Encouraging rational thinking.
