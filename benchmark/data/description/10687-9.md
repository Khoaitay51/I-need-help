It is important to recognize stress in children and teens and help them with healthy coping strategies. The strategies they learn often stay with them into adulthood. Generally, anything that may cause children fear and anxiety can cause stress. It is important to help children develop positive coping skills, as these skills are often carried into adult life. You can help by: 1  Providing a good example. 2  Keep calm and express your anger in appropriate ways. 3  Think through plans to reduce stress, and share them with your family. 4  Encouraging rational thinking.
