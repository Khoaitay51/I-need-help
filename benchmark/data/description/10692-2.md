Human mitochondria _____.? 1. are all inherited from the mother 2. have linear DNA 3. are inherited as an X-linked trait 4. do not contain DNA 5. are all inherited from the father. Rating Newest Oldest. Best Answer: 1. are all inherited from the mother The male gamete's mitochondria are shed outside the egg when the sperm and egg merge so the human embryo only inherits the maternal mitochondria.
