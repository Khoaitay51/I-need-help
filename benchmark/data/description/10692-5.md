As previously mentioned, mitochondrial DNA in humans is always inherited from a person's mother (Figure 4). As a result, we share our mitochondrial DNA sequence with our mothers, brothers, sisters, maternal grandmothers, maternal aunts and uncles, and other maternal relatives. Mutations in the mitochondrial genome have also been associated with diverse forms of human disease and aging. Mitochondria are spherical, double-membrane-bound organelles that rely on the distinct functions of their two membranes: the outer mitochondrial membrane and the inner mitochondrial membrane (Figure 1).
