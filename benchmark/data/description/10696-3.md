Tip. 1  If you're not going to be away for long enough to turn the refrigerator off, you can still save some energy by turning off the ice maker. 2  Remove all food items you expect to spoil while you're away, and adjust the temperature to a warmer setting. 3. Remove all food and beverages from the refrigerator and freezer. Open a box of baking soda, and stir 1 tablespoon into 1 quart of water. Dip a cloth in the mixture, and use it to clean the inside of the refrigerator. When the freezer has warmed enough, clean the inside of the freezer as well.
