A berm can be a man-made sediment barrier placed at the edge of a slope or a wall built adjacent to a ditch to guard against potential flooding. Berms are placed in flood-prone areas to protect against erosion, run off and high water. The terms berm, dike, and levee are commonly used interchangeably to describe such structures that are used for flood mitigation. The main difference between the terms is how each is built to mitigate flooding.
