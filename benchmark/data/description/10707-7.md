Browse: Home » Kehillot. Defined most simply, a kehilla is a community, a group of people who have come together with shared purpose and in fellowship. Kehilla is the singular, kehillot the plural. Following Jewish tradition, we are going one step farther. We have called them congregations, which is true but bland. Now we are returning to our sacred language, Hebrew, to call them kehillot. We also use the term kehilla to go beyond the traditional understanding of a synagogue. A sacred community can be an institution where people go to pray and mark lifecycle events.
