Add classic elegance and rich texture to your home with a Persian rug. These stunning area rugs, which date back to ancient Persia, are traditionally made in Iran, though the designs are often copied and made in other countries, too. Most Persian carpets and rugs made in Iran are handmade, many of them with hand-dyed yarn and traditional weaving techniques. These exquisite handmade Persian rugs are always made with natural fibers, such as wool, silk, or cotton, and can last for up to 80 years if cared for properly.
