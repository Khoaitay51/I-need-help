Why we use ANPR. Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) technology is used to help detect, deter and disrupt criminality at a local, force, regional and national level, including tackling traveling criminals, Organised Crime Groups and terrorists. Retention and access to stored data. At present around 7750 ANPR cameras nationally, submit between 25 and 30 million ANPR ‘read’ records to the National ANPR Data Centre (NADC) daily. ANPR data from each police force is stored together with similar data from other forces for a period of two years.
