To create a mobile hotspot with Surface 2, do the following: 1  Tap the AT&T App tile on your Surface – it should be located on your start screen but it it is not, it will be at the top of the list of apps on your Surface 2. 1 Tap Mobile Hotspot and make sure it is turned on – it will give you warning, read it and click OK. 2  You will now see the name of your Surface 2 network and a WiFi password that you will need for your other devices to connect.
