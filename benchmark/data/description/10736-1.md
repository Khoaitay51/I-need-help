Buy a used vehicle from a dealer, and the dealer takes care of all the paperwork associated with the transfer of ownership, can help arrange financing if necessary, and can even buy your old car as a trade-in. Buy a used vehicle from a private party seller, and those details are the buyer's responsibility. Buying a used vehicle from a private party seller requires meetings with strangers to test-drive the vehicle, have the vehicle inspected, and to complete the transaction for the vehicle. The majority of the time, this is not a concern.
