Buying from a Private Party Seller-PROS. Lower Prices-Used cars from private sellers can generally be bought for less money than cars at a dealership since the private sellers don’t need to maintain a business. More Negotiating Room-There’s often more room to negotiate on a car’s price with a private seller. Buying from a Private Party Seller-CONS. No Warranties-A car bought from a private party is usually sold “as is.” Private sellers want to sell their car and be totally done once the sale is complete.
