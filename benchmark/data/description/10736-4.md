When buying a used vehicle, most consumers choose to purchase from a dealership or a private party seller. A private party seller is an individual just like you, someone who owns a vehicle that they need to sell, and who is likely selling the vehicle themselves in order to extract maximum value for it. Buying a used vehicle from a private party seller requires meetings with strangers to test-drive the vehicle, have the vehicle inspected, and to complete the transaction for the vehicle. The majority of the time, this is not a concern.
