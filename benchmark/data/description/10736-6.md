When it comes to vehicle history, a private seller likely has more information than a dealer. After all, dealers can go through hundreds or thousands of cars per year, while a private seller likely knows the ins and outs of his or her vehicle, having owned it for some time. If you've tried to finance a used car, you'll know that it's not as easy as financing a new car. Interest rates are higher, and some banks won't finance vehicles past a certain age or a certain mileage limit. Some banks also won't finance vehicles through private sellers.
