0. Recipe by: SABMAN. Donairs are a delicacy prevalent in the Canadian east coast, and certainly centered on the City of Halifax, they are very simple to prepare and very delicious. Make a double batch of the meatloaf, and freeze the second loaf for later, in fact the second loaf will be better! 1 To make the sauce, combine the evaporated milk, sugar and vinegar in a jar with a lid, and shake well to blend. 2  Set aside to thicken. 3  Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat, and slice off thin slices of the meat loaf. 4  Fry the slices briefly in the hot skillet, until browned on each side.
