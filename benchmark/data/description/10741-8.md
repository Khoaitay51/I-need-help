What a Snake Bite Looks Like on a Dog. A venomous snakebite will show fang mark punctures on your dog. John Foxx/Stockbyte/Getty Images. Your dog is a curious creature who loves to sniff out new experiences and friends. Unfortunately, that curious nature can get your pooch into trouble if he encounters a poisonous snake. You may notice your dog trying to lick the area, as it produces pain and blood. If the snake was poisonous, look for fang marks. A full bite will show two close punctures, though a single puncture is possible if the snake was unable to get a direct strike.
