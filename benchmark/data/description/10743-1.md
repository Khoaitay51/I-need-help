To explain what interpreters do it is worthwhile to start by discussing the differences between interpreting and translation. (Please note: more information about interpreting and technical translation is available at Modes of Interpreting and Interpreting vs. Translation. On the surface, the difference between interpreting and translation is only the difference in the medium: the interpreter translates orally, while a translator interprets written text. Both interpreting and translation presuppose a certain love of language and deep knowledge of more than one tongue.
