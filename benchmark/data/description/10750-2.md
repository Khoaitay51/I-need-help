The most common sources of air pollution include the burning of fossil fuels and other materials in oil refineries, power plants, factories, automobiles and other forms of transportation, as well as the incinerators. Main types of pollution 2: Water Pollution. Water pollution is another one of the main types of pollution of the environment. Water pollution could be in the form of any change in the physical, chemical and biological properties of water which has a harmful effect on living things.
