Kelsey /kel-sey/ [2 sylls.] as a girls' name (also used less commonly as boys' name Kelsey) is pronounced KEL-see. It is of Old English origin, and the meaning of Kelsey is  victorious ship. Also possibly a place name referring to  island . Popular preference for Kelsey and its variants may be influenced by names like Chelsea and Casey. Kelsey is a very popular first name for women (#555 out of 4276, Top 13%) and also a very popular surname for all people (#2397 out of 150436, Top 2%). (2000 U.S. Census).
