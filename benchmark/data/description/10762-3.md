You can shut down your Windows 8 computer at desired time. You can specify times when to shut down the PC, like Shutdown at 12:10:00 every day, or Shutdown at 2011/9/26 12:10:00. If you want to set it to automatically shut down after 15 minutes, type in 15 minutes from now. To shut down your Windows 8 PC, click on Settings in the start-menu. Then on the right sidebar, click on Power, it will pop up Shut down and Restart. Click Shut down to shut down your Windows 8. If you want a more quick way, you can use hotkey combination Alt+F4 to shutdown Windows 8 OS
