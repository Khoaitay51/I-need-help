Memoir, autobiography, biography. A memoir is a special kind of autobiography, usually involving a public portion of the author’s life as it relates to a person, historic event, or thing. The text is about the personal knowledge and/or experiences of the author. A literary agent discusses the contents of a quality memoir and how it differs from an autobiography or biography.
