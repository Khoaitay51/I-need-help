Memoirs are typically classified as a subgenre of the autobiography. The main difference is that a memoir is more focused. An autobiography typically spans a person's entire life and contains intricate details like the writer's family history and childhood. A memoir, on the other hand, is much more centralized. Lesson Summary. Memoirs are a subgenre of the autobiography. They are different from standard autobiographies because they are more focused. An autobiography usually spans a person's entire life, while a memoir may concentrate on a particular time or place in the writer's life.
