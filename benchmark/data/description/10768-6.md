Memoir. As a literary genre, a memoir (from the French: memoire mémoire from The latin, memoria meaning, memory or a), reminiscence forms a subclass of – autobiography although the 'terms' memoir 'and' autobiography are almost interchangeable in modern. parlance Memoir is autobiographical writing, but not all autobiographical writing follows the criteria for memoir.Memoirs are structured differently from formal autobiographies which tend to encompass the writer's entire life span, focusing on the development of his/her personality.
