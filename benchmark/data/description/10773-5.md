The words and translations to the Cajun recordings of Jole Blon, Jole Blonde, Jole Blond, Jolie Blon, Jolie Blonde and Jolie Blond. (The early Louisiana Cajun music recordings). This is being compiled by Clarence, the webmaster for the Cajun and Zydeco Radio Guide. The first recording of the song Jolie Blon Ma Blonde Est Partie (Jolie Blonde) was made in 1928 by Amadie Breaux (born 09/07/1900), his brother Ophy Breaux and his sister Cleoma Breaux. It was recorded on the Old-Timey Records label.
