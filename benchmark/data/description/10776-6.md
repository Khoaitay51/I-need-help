Symptoms. Signs and symptoms of acute kidney failure may include: 1  Decreased urine output, although occasionally urine output remains normal. 2  Fluid retention, causing swelling in your legs, ankles or feet. 3  Drowsiness. 4  Shortness of breath. 5  Fatigue. 6  Confusion. 7  Nausea. 8  Seizures or coma in severe cases. 9  Chest pain or pressure
