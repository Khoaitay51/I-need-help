Well it is. And mostly safe. But you should know which bugs your dog can and can’t eat. Flies and moths. It’s safe for your dog to catch a few of these. In fact, he’ll get the most exercise trying to catch just one because they fly around. Crickets. There’s nothing cuter than a dog mimicking the jumps of a cricket. It’s mostly safe for a dog to eat these—in fact many humans are turning to this jumpy bug for the high protein. That being said, chemicals in your yard can poison crickets, and get in your dog’s tummy. If your yard is sprayed with chemicals, keep your pup away from crickets.
