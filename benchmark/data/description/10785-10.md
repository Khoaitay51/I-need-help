Pneumovax ® (pneumococcal polysaccharide vaccine) is a vaccine used to prevent pneumococcal disease, a group of infections (such as pneumonia or bacteremia) caused by Streptococcus pneumoniae. It is recommended as a routine vaccination for people age 65 and older, and in special situations for people age 2 and older.
