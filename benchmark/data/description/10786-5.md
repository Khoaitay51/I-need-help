CELLS NEED THE ROUGH AND THE SMOOTH. Think of a cell as a “multitude of membranes” we said in an earlier section. This statement certainly applies to the endoplasmic reticulum an organelle found in eukaryotic cells. About 50% of the total membrane surface in an animal cell is provided by endoplasmic reticulum (ER). These ions are released from the smooth endoplasmic reticulum. SUMMARY. Organelles called ‘rough endoplasmic reticulum’ and ‘smooth endoplasmic reticulum’ are separate networks of membrane enclosed flattened tubules found in eukaryotic animal and plant cells.
