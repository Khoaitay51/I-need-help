The endoplasmic reticulum (or ER) is an organelle in a cell. The ER is divided into two types--Rough and Smooth ER. Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum is important in the synthesis a … nd transport of proteins. The Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum makes lipids and is involved in drug detoxification. The ER works with the Golgi apparatus and ribosomes.
