In addition, PVDF coatings are often used in the manufacture of lithium ion batteries. In this industry, the material is used as a binder material when producing composite electrodes. PVDF is also a great conductive additive. The material is also commonly used in the roofing or building industry. You’ll find it in many famous buildings all over the world, including Malaysia’s Petronas Towers and Taiwan’s Taipei 101. In addition, it’s used within the biomedical sciences to create artificial membranes.
