Forms of PVDF Coatings. This type of coating is less expensive than many other types of fluoropolymer coatings, and it is also less dense. It comes in pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, and tubing. You can also find it in a form that’s perfect for use of wire insulation and in a closed cell foam. PVDF is also a great conductive additive. The material is also commonly used in the roofing or building industry. You’ll find it in many famous buildings all over the world, including Malaysia’s Petronas Towers and Taiwan’s Taipei 101. In addition, it’s used within the biomedical sciences to create artificial membranes.
