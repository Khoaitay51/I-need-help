Step 2. Place the tape measure down the side of the slab to measure the depth of the concrete. Typical depth for most slabs is four inches. Convert the four inches into feet. Divide four inches by 12 inches and the foot measurement is equal to 0.33 feet. Overview. All concrete is sold by the cubic yard. A cubic yard is equal to a cube that is three feet wide by three feet high by three feet deep. The total volume is equal to 27 cubic feet.
