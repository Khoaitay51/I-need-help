To calculate the volume of concrete you need, you must measure the surface area of the slab and multiply this by the thickness of the slab. You usually measure the length and width of the slab in feet, thus giving you a value for area in square feet when you multiply them, but you usually measure thickness in inches. When ordering concrete for a large project, such as the slab for a new garage, you need to quote the amount you need in cubic yards. For smaller projects, such as the floor for a garden shed, cubic feet is a more appropriate unit.
