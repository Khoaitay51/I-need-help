Click on the images for a larger view. The American pika has a small, round body, peppery brown fur, large round ears and no visible tail. It is between six and eight inches long and weighs about six ounces. The American pika is diurnal, or active in the day. It spends most of the day foraging for food; guarding its territory; watching for predators like eagles, hawks, coyotes, bobcats, foxes, and weasels and sunning itself on the rocks. The pika is a very vocal animal.
