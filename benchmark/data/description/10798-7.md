The American pika can be found in western North America from central British Columbia in Canada to Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Montana, Wyoming, Colorado, Nevada, California and New Mexico. The American pika is diurnal, or active in the day. It spends most of the day foraging for food; guarding its territory; watching for predators like eagles, hawks, coyotes, bobcats, foxes, and weasels and sunning itself on the rocks. The pika is a very vocal animal.
