The American pika, known in the 19th century as the little chief hare, has a small, round, ovate body. Their body length ranges from 162 to 216 mm (6–8 in). Their hind feet range from 25 to 35 mm (1–1½ in). They usually weigh about 170 g (6 oz). The American pika is intermediate in size among pikas. The hind legs of the pika do not seem to be much longer than its front legs and its hind feet are relatively short when compared to most other lagomorphs. It has densely furred soles on its feet except for black pads at the ends of the toes.
