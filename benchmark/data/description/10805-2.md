Housemaid's Knee. Housemaids Knee also known as prepatellar bursitis or knee bursitis is a swelling of the bursa or small sack of fluid at the front of the knee. Symptoms of Housemaids knee include pain and tenderness at the front of the kneecap and just below it. The kneecap or patella may be swollen and warm to the touch. Kneeling may be painful, hence the term housemaids knee. An abscess or lump may be visible over the patella
