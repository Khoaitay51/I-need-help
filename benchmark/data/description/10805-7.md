Causes of Housemaids knee. Acute prepatellar bursitis can be caused by a direct blow or fall on the knee. This ruptures blood vessels which bleed into the bursa causing swelling and triggering an inflammation reaction in the walls of the bursa. Symptoms of Housemaids knee include pain and tenderness at the front of the kneecap and just below it. The kneecap or patella may be swollen and warm to the touch.
