Botox is the cosmetic form of Botulinum toxin A which has been used for more than 15 years in the medical profession. The toxins attach themselves to the nerve endings. An ice pack held on the area to be injected for a few moments can help reduce any pain. Botox is injected directly into the muscle surrounding the wrinkle with a very small needle. The muscles will be paralyzed leaving the skin smoother and giving more refreshed appearance.
