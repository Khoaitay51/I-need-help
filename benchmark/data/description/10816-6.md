Most anaerobic organisms also produce catalase. The function of catalase is to break down Hydrogen Peroxide which is a harmful waste product of many biochemical processes in living cells. Catalase is used to break down hydrogen peroxide. In an environment that does not contain oxygen (such as those that anaerobic organisms live in) the organism does not necessarily need to protect against H2O2.
