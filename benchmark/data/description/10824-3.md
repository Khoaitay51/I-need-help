Gasoline /ˈɡaesəliːn/ , ˈɡæsəliːn also known as /petrol/ ˈpɛtrəl outside Of North, america is a, transparent-petroleum derived liquid that is used primarily as a fuel in internal combustion. engines The octane rating of typical commercially available gasoline varies by country. In Finland, Sweden, and Norway, 95 RON is the standard for regular unleaded gasoline and 98 RON is also available as a more expensive option.
