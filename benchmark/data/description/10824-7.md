Tim, no one is suggesting that mogas is a solution for those who need 100 octane fuels. But it is a good solution for 70%-80% of all legacy piston aircraft and essentially 100% of all new LSAs. To ignore affordable, lead-free mogas as an important aviation fuel is irresponsible. Myth #10 – Mogas at airports costs too much and takes revenue away from airports. Fact – Mogas is typically $1-$3 cheaper than 100LL and makes its seller a profit. Airports selling mogas recoup revenue lost to self-fuelers, help sport aviation and flight schools grow, and retain the same flowage fees as 100LL sales.
