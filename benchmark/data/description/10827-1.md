Routine Clinitest screening of stools from high-risk infants has been advocated. A study of healthy full-term infants found that stool Clinitest was 2+ or greater (equivalent to ≥ 0.75% reducing sugar) in 49% of the infants, and 4+ in 16% of this population, particularly if they were breast-fed. The quantification of reducing substances in stool, using Clinitest tablets, is another potential measure of bowel mucosal integrity.
