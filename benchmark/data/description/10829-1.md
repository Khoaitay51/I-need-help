The result: independent students might qualify for more aid. Being considered an independent student is not merely a matter of being responsible for your own educational expenses. You must meet certain criteria to be declared an independent student for the purposes of the FAFSA : 1 Be a graduate or professional student; 2  Be a married individual; 3  Have legal dependents other than a spouse; 4  Be an emancipated minor or in legal guardianship; 5  Be a homeless youth; 6  Be a student for whom a financial aid administrator makes a documented determination of independence by reason of other unusual circumstances.
