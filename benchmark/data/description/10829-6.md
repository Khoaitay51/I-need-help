A: A person who is at least 24 year of age or is an orphan can apply for FAFSA independent student status. Veterans of armed forces can also get the independent student status. All you need to do is to fill out the free Federal Student Aid form and submit it, you'll get your financial aid. A: It depends on the circumstance if you can get a professional judgment override on the basis of your independent student FAFSA application. Students who present a compelling case proving that their parents/guardians will not be able to help them financially, the financial aid officer can override the FAFSA results.
