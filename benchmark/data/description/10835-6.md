NBD means the patient has problems with urination. The term neurogenic refers to the nerve tissues that supply and stimulate an organ or muscle to function properly. In the case of NBD, nerves that control the bladder and muscles involved in urination cause the bladder to be overactive or underactive. NBD symptoms may include: 1  Limited or no voluntary control. 2  Involuntary urination. 3  Feel sudden urge(s) to urinate. 4  Frequent bathroom visits.
