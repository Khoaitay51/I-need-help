The lungs expand as the alveoli fill up with air. The concentration of oxygen in the air of the alveoli is higher than the concentration of oxygen in the blood inside the capillaries, which results in oxygen diffusing from the alveoli into the capillaries. At the same time the carbon dioxide level in the capillaries is high and low in the lungs. So carbon dioxide also diffuses, but in the opposite direction!
