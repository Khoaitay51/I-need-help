The strength of chemical bonds varies considerably; there are strong bonds such as covalent or ionic bonds and weak bonds such as Dipole-dipole interaction, the London dispersion force and hydrogen bonding. A strong chemical bond is formed from the transfer or sharing of electrons between atomic centers and relies on the electrostatic attraction between the protons in nuclei and the electrons in the orbitals. The types of strong bond differ due to the difference in electronegativity of the constituent elements.
