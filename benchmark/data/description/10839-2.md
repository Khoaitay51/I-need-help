One definition of a landrace applied to both plants and animals is which has developed over a long period of time and as a result has adapted to the local natural environment in which it lives.. However, in other cases, formalizing a landrace may result in the genetic resource of a landrace being lost through crossbreeding. Landraces are distinct from ancestral wild species of modern stock, and from separate species or subspecies derived from the same ancestor as modern domestic stock.
