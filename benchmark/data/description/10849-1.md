The seminal vesicles are a pair of glands that are positioned below the urinary bladder and lateral to the vas deferens. Each vesicle consists of a single tube folded and coiled on itself, with occasional diverticula in its wall. Each vesicle forms as an outpocketing of the wall of the ampulla of one vas deferens.
