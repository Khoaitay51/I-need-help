Summary. Seminal vesicles are glands that produce 60% of the fluid found in semen. Seminal fluid from these glands contains sugar, prostaglandins, metals, and other components necessary to support the activity of sperm. Seminal vesicles release up to 60% of the fluid found in semen. The other 40% is produced by the prostate and bulbourethral glands. The fluid produced by seminal vesicles will contain several key components: • Fructose, which is a sugar that is produced to provide energy for swimming sperm cells
