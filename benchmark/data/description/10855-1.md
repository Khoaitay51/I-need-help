The OH (hydroxyl) functional group of phenol (hydroxybenzene) is said to activate the benzene ring at the 2- and 4- positions. This results in the formation of 2-nitrophenol and 4-nitrophenol. 1 Paracetamol (acetaminophen) is widely used as an analgesic (pain reliever) and an antipyretic (for reducing fever). 2  The systematic IUPAC name for paracetamol (acetaminophen) is N -(4-hydroxyphenyl)ethanamide or N -(4-hydroxyphenyl)acetamide.
