1 Mitochondria-this organelle generates energy for the cell. 2  Nucleus-membrane bound structure that contains the cell's hereditary information. 3  Nucleolus-structure within the nucleus that helps in the synthesis of ribosomes. Some of these include a cell wall, a large vacuole, and plastids. Plastids, such as chloroplasts, assist in storing and harvesting needed substances for the plant. Animal cells also contain structures such as centrioles, lysosomes, and cilia and flagella that are not typically found in plant cells.
