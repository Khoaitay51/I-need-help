Jadon meaning. The name Jadon is the verb din made active: he will din. For a meaning of the name Jadon, NOBSE Study Bible Name List reads He Judges. Jones' Dictionary of Old Testament Proper Names has Whom God Will Judge. The name Jadon comes from the root-verb דין (din) meaning to judge: Abarim Publications Theological Dictionary. דיןThe verb דין (din) is one of a few verbs that may mean to judge or govern.
