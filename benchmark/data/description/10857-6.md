Meaning of name: Thankful; a judge; one judged by God; “God has heard”. The Story Behind This Biblical Christian Baby Name. There is one very brief mention of this name in the book of Nehemiah. A man named Jadon from a place called Merenoth helped to rebuild the walls and gates of Jerusalem. God has blessed me with my little Jadon on the 5th of April. Me and my 5 year old daughter, Josephine decided on this name before he was born and my 3 year old Jordan agreed with us. He is truely an angle send from heaven.
