The name Jadon in the Bible. There's only one Jadon in the Bible. He comes from a place called Meronoth and is mentioned among the men who make repairs to the wall surrounding Jerusalem under Nehemiah (Nehemiah 3:7). The name Jadon comes from the root-verb דין (din) meaning to judge: Abarim Publications Theological Dictionary. דיןThe verb דין (din) is one of a few verbs that may mean to judge or govern.
