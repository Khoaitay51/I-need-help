Love is an understanding that is so complete that you feel as if you are a part of the other person; accepting the other person just the way they are and not trying to change them to be something else. Love is the source of unity. Love is the freedom to pursue your own desires while sharing your experiences with the other person; the growth of one individual alongside of and together with the growth of another individual. Love is the source of success. Love is the excitement of planning things together; the excitement of doing things together. Love is the source of the future. Love is the fury of the storm; the calm of the rainbow. Love is the source of passion. Love is giving and taking in a daily situation; being patient with each other's needs and desires
