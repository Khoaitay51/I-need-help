Susan Polis Schutz (born Susan Polis; May 23, 1944) is an American poet and co-founder, with husband Stephen Schutz, of greeting card and book publisher Blue Mountain Arts. They are the parents of U.S. Congressman Jared Polis of Colorado. Schutz was born in Peekskill, NY, and is the daughter of June (nee Née) keller And David. polis
