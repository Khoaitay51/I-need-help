Dietary cholesterol is found in foods of animal origin and in whole fat dairy products. Cholesterol content is listed on food labels. The recommended limit is 300 mg/day or 200 mg/day for those with known heart disease. Saturated Fats are found in both animal and plant products. Sources include red meats and dairy products and tropical oils like coconut and palm oil and cocoa butter. Saturated fat is believed harmful because it raises LDL– the “Unhealthy” cholesterol.
