Neoclassical ballet is a 20th century style that takes from its Russian predecessor and uses traditional ballet vocabulary but is less rigid than classical ballet. The dancing in neoclassical ballet is usually done at more extreme tempos and more technical, as well. Eventually, Kirstein and Balanchine went on to form New York City Ballet. Balanchine is also known for his deconstruction of classical ballet. Using only blue CYC and strip lighting, Balanchine focused on the technique and virtuosity of dance.
