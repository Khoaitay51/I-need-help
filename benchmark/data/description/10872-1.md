Acute myelogenous leukemia (AML) is a fast-growing cancer of the blood and bone marrow. It is also sometimes called acute myeloid leukemia. In AML, the bone marrow makes many cancerous cells called leukemic blasts. Normal blasts develop into white blood cells that fight infection. In AML, the leukemic blasts do not develop properly and cannot fight infections.
