Europe Denmark. Although Denmark’s winters are not as bitterly cold as the winters in the rest of Scandinavia, November to March can nevertheless be an uncomfortable time to visit. The best time to visit the country, therefore, is between mid-April and November, when the weather’s good. May, June and late August or early September are definitely better, as the place has warmed up by then, but the crowds of tourists haven’t yet hit the tourist attractions.
