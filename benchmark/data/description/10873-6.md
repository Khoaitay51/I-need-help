July and August are the best times to visit, as the climate is comfortable and most of the cultural events take place in this period. Also, there are fewer crowds in late August as it is end of the vacations. Winters can be harsh. Denmark is home a number of music festivals that are held throughout the year. The Aalborg Jazz festival, in June, features free concerts for around four days. The Riverboat Jazz festival also takes place during the month. The Roskilde Festival is one of the largest rock concerts in all of Europe.
