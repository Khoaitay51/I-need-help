a·moe·ba. Plural amoebas or amoebae (ə-mē′bē). A one-celled microscopic organism that constantly changes shape by forming pseudopods, temporary projections that are used for movement and for the ingestion of food. Amoebas are members of the group of organisms called protozoans. 1. amoeba-naked freshwater or marine or parasitic protozoa that form temporary pseudopods for feeding and locomotion. ameba. rhizopod, rhizopodan-protozoa characterized by a pseudopod. Amoebida, Amoebina, order Amoebida, order Amoebina-the animal order including amoebas. endameba-any ameba of the genus Endamoeba.
