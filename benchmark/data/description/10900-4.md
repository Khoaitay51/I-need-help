Amoeba. Amoeba (also spelled ameba) is a genus of protozoa that moves by means of temporary projections called pseudopods (false feet), and is well-known as a representative unicellular organism. The word amoeba is also used to refer to both this genus and its close relatives, now grouped as the Amoebozoa, as well as to refer to all unicellular organisms that move by pseudopods. The pseudopods, or false feet, result from the extension of cytoplasm outward.
