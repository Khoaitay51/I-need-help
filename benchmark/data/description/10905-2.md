“JCAHO” is now known as the “Joint Commission” and is also involved with evaluating the health services standards of healthcare facilities, laboratories, and other health services organization in the U.S. JCAHO was established in 1951 and holds its official headquarters in the state of Illinois. Since then, JCAHO, or the Joint Commission, has already accredited almost 20,000 healthcare facilities and health services programs across the United States. JCAHO works as a non-profit organization and is run by several board members representing various sectors in the healthcare industry.
