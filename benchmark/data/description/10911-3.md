Reasons for Procedure. A transfusion is needed when the fetus's blood count falls too low. Severe anemia in a fetus can cause death. Anemia can be caused by: 1  Rh incompatibility —the mother and fetus have a different type of blood, and mother’s antibodies to fetal blood cells destroy fetal blood cells. 2  Parvovirus B19 infection —a viral infection in the mother.
