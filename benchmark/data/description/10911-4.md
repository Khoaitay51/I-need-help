An intrauterine fetal blood transfusion is done in the hospital. The mother may have to stay overnight after the procedure. 1  The mother is sedated, and an ultrasound image is obtained to determine the position of the fetus and placenta. A blood transfusion is given to replace fetal red blood cells that are being destroyed by the Rh-sensitized mother's immune system. This treatment is meant to keep the fetus healthy until he or she is mature enough to be delivered.
