Anemia is a lack of red blood cells. A transfusion means giving the fetus red blood cells from a donor. There are two types of fetal blood transfusions: 1  Intravascular transfusion (IVT)—done through the mother’s abdomen into the fetus’s umbilical cord. Severe anemia in a fetus can cause death. Anemia can be caused by: 1  Rh incompatibility —the mother and fetus have a different type of blood, and mother’s antibodies to fetal blood cells destroy fetal blood cells. 2  Parvovirus B19 infection —a viral infection in the mother.
