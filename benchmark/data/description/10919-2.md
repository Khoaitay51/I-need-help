Identifying the boundaries of the rural-urban fringe. The critical boundaries are between (a) the continuous built-up area and the rural-urban fringe and (b) the fringe and the surrounding rural area. The boundary between the continuous built-up area and the rural-urban fringe is not difficult to identify (Figure 5). A succession of large open spaces separate the London conurbation from free-standing settlements located within the rural-urban fringe. London’s rural-urban fringe.
