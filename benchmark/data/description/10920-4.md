Tendinitis. Tendinitis is the irritation or inflammation of a tendon. When this occurs in the tendons in and around the elbow, pain is felt in that area. This is one of the most common causes of elbow pain, largely because it can result from so many different activities. Sprain Or Strain. Much like tendinitis, sprains and strains are common causes of elbow pain. These conditions also have somewhat similar symptoms, so it may be difficult to tell which condition you have. Sprains and strains are not exactly alike, however, since they affect different parts of the elbow.
