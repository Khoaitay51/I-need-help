Osteoarthritis of the elbow occurs when the protective cartilage on the ends of the bones in the elbow wear down over time. In addition to elbow pain during or after using the elbow joint, this condition may also cause stiffness, tenderness or a loss of flexibility in that area. Sprain Or Strain. Much like tendinitis, sprains and strains are common causes of elbow pain. These conditions also have somewhat similar symptoms, so it may be difficult to tell which condition you have. Sprains and strains are not exactly alike, however, since they affect different parts of the elbow.
