Etymology Edit. Lindzei may be derived from the common name Lindsey or Lindsay. [view · edit · purge] Lindsey is a unisex name of Gaelic origin associated with lakes and islands, or specifically islands of Linden trees. Lindzei is mentioned by name by Vanille, in the Analects section of the Datalog in Final Fantasy XIII, and in a name of a fragment in Final Fantasy XIII-2. The aspects and traits Lindzei represents are seen throughout the Fabula Nova Crystallis series.
