Lindsay /lind-say/ [2 sylls.] as a girls' name (also used less generally as boys' name Lindsay) is pronounced LIN-zee. It is of Old English origin, and the meaning of Lindsay is Lincoln's marsh; island of linden trees . Place name. The surname of some of the major Scottish and English noble families. Lindsay has 1 masculine form: Linn. Lindsay is a very popular first name for females (#302 out of 4276, Top 7%) and also a very popular last name for all people (#1008 out of 150436, Top 1%).
