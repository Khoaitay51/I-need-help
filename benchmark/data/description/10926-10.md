Cowan Genealogy & History. Cowan is a Scottish surname. Among the several possible origins of the name include Gobhainn, which means a smith or blacksmith, and Gowan, which is a type of Scottish wildflower. These names were both derived from the Greek name Eugene, meaning well-born or born lucky. Both of these names indicate that there were originally family members who were wealthy or well off. Cowan genealogy includes the alternative spelling Cowen.
