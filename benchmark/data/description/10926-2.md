Cowan, Cowans, Cowen are common surnames in Ayrshire, Dumfriesshire and other lowland counties of Scotland. The name is one of a mix of names that evolved possibly from its earliest Gaelic form Macilchomhghain (seventh century). 2 Sept Cowan – Scotland Connection This section tells the story of the Cowan name through history from several sources and is not intended to reflect direct relationships.
