The meristem also forms a protective root cap. The cells to the upper left of this image are embryonic protoderm, ground meristem, and procambium. Apical meristems are sites of intense mitotic cell divisions. The new cells produced fuel the growth and development of new tissues. A second ring of meristematic tissue forms just below the epidermis in woody plants to produce bark. This is the cork cambium.
