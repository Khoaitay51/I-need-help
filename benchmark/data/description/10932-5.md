The deadlift is a full-body exercise that works almost all muscle groups. It must be done carefully and with good technique, however, because an incorrect deadlift can strain muscles and joints in the back. A sumo deadlift works the hip and groin muscles more than the conventional style. Any deadlift can be done with either a barbell or dumbbells. You start a barbell deadlift with your feet under the bar and roll it up your shins and thighs.
