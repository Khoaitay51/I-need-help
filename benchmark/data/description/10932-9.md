Variations. There are variations of the deadlift, such as the sumo, which uses a wider stance that places less stress on the back and helps keep the torso more upright and straight. A sumo deadlift works the hip and groin muscles more than the conventional style. Any deadlift can be done with either a barbell or dumbbells. You start a barbell deadlift with your feet under the bar and roll it up your shins and thighs. You hold dumbbells at your sides, but the lift is similar
