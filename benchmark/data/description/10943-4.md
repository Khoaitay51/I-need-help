Charmeuse (. [ʃaʁmoeːz]) ʃaʁmøːz is a lightweight fabric woven with a satin, weave in which the warp threads cross over three or more of the (backing) weft. Threads the front of the fabric has a satin—finish lustrous and—reflective whereas the back has a dull. finish Bridal gowns sometime use charmeuse; however, the fabric does not hold a shape well, so it is not used for full, flared skirts; the charmeuse tends to cling and hang against the body. It is best suited to a more fluid, slinky bias cut, and is too fragile and flimsy for more tailored clothing.
