Charmeuse is made with silk, which comes from the cocoon of the silkworm. Charmeuse is a fabric made from silk that has a satin finish. As a general rule, charmeuse can be hand-washed with cold water and gentle detergent. To make charmeuse, silk is extracted from silk worms and then woven using a satin weaving technique. The fabric is difficult to work with, since the right side of the fabric is so slippery. In addition, it shows pin holes, so garments cannot simply be pinned for sewing
