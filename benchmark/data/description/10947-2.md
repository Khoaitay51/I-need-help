Binary Fission vs Budding. Binary fission and budding are two different types of asexual reproduction methods. Other than these two, regeneration and parthenogenesis are also considered as asexual reproduction types. Production of offspring without fertilization is known as asexual reproduction. These newly formed cells contain a duplicate of the parent chromosome. Budding. Budding is also a simple asexual reproduction method seen in fungi, certain plants, and in sponges like Hydra. Here, the replication of the nucleus followed by unequal cytokinesis takes place.
