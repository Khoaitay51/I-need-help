Due to the simple structure of the prokaryotic cell, it reproduces by a method known as asexual reproduction. In most prokaryotic bacteria, this is done by the process of binary fission, where two daughter cells are formed that are a replica of the parent cell. Sometimes, this reproduction may also occur by the process of budding, where a part of the cell bulges and breaks off to form another cell. Prokaryotic cells are cells that lack a nucleus.
