Prokaryotic Cell Reproduction. Prokaryotic cells reproduce by a process that is called binary fission. The DNA in such cells is contained in a single circular chromosome called a plasmid within the cytoplasm. The reproductive process starts with the replication of the chromosome. Eukaryotic Cell Reproduction. The life of eukaryotic cells is characterized by a cell cycle with two major phases: the interphase and cell division. During the interphase, the cell takes in nutrients, grows, and duplicates its chromosomes.
