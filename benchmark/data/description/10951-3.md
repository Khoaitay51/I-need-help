The Galapagos Islands have made a name for themselves as a hotbed of biological activity ever since Charles Darwin was there in 1835 and used his observations of Galapagos finches to help formulate his ideas on evolution. Hammerheads, which can be found in the Galapagos, are very defensive, so keep your distance. The iguanas found on the Galapagos Islands aren't dangerous, but they have a tendency to clear their nostrils after a swim -- which can easily turn a nice snapshot of iguanas into a ruined digital camera.
