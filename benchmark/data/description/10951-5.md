Earthquakes and volcanoes are also a concern in the Galapagos themselves. The Galapagos are on top of an earthquake hotspot where a portion of the Nazca and Cocos plates rub together, creating regular earthquakes. The Galapagos Islands are home to several volcanoes, formed from basalt, the most common type of lava. Hammerheads, which can be found in the Galapagos, are very defensive, so keep your distance. The iguanas found on the Galapagos Islands aren't dangerous, but they have a tendency to clear their nostrils after a swim -- which can easily turn a nice snapshot of iguanas into a ruined digital camera.
