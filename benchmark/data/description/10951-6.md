Crime is not a serious threat when traveling to the Galapagos. With some 40,000 residents, it is relatively small, and the islands' crime does not compare to that of Ecuador's. Hammerheads, which can be found in the Galapagos, are very defensive, so keep your distance. The iguanas found on the Galapagos Islands aren't dangerous, but they have a tendency to clear their nostrils after a swim -- which can easily turn a nice snapshot of iguanas into a ruined digital camera.
