Gout is a condition that causes swelling and severe pain in your joints, often the joint of your big toe. Gout is a condition where a chemical produced by your body, called uric acid, forms crystals in your joints. The crystals can also appear in your tendons and ligaments, as well as beneath your skin. If the level of uric acid in your body is too high, it can form tiny crystals that collect in your tissues. These can collect in and around your joints in particular. This is what may cause your swelling and pain. When you have pseudogout, the crystals that form aren't uric acid crystals, as they are with 'real' gout.
