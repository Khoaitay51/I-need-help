Shaving Secrets. If shaving is the crux of your hair-removal routine, you can make a few tweaks to slow down leg hair regrowth. Shave just before bed, rather than early in the day, because your follicles tighten during sleep. This keeps hair from peeking out of your follicles prematurely. Alternative Methods. Though shaving might be the workhorse of leg hair removal because it's so easy, other forms of grooming cause hair to grow back significantly more slowly in comparison. Waxing is the most common alternative, especially now that there are effective home waxing kits.
