When many people hear industrial hemp, the first thing that may come to mind in marijuana. After all, there is a close association between the two, but perhaps not as close as one might think. In fact, the biggest thing that ties hemp and marijuana together is they are both members of the plant genus, Cannabis. In fact, if a hemp plant were to be grown alongside a marijuana plant, the hemp would render the marijuana sterile. Hemp seeds are used to make foods, oil, wax, resin, rope, cloth, pulp, paper, building material, and even fuel.
