Meaning: Urge on; cause to act. Classified under: Verbs of touching, hitting, tying, digging. Synonyms: egg on; prod; incite. Context example: They other children egged the boy on, but he did not want to throw the stone through the window. Troponyms (each of the following is one way to incite): goose (prod into action). halloo (urge on with shouts). goad (urge with or as if with a goad). Sentence frames: Somebody ----s something.
