The appendicular skeleton is the part of the human skeleton that includes the arms and hands, legs and feet, the shoulder girdle and the pelvic girdle. The appendicular skeleton is shown shaded pink in the diagram of the appendicular skeleton on the right. (The bones shaded greenish-yellow form the axial skeleton .). This is because the appendicular skeleton is symmetrical. For example, there are 2 humerus bones in each human appendicular skeleton-one humerus bone in each of the two upper-arms. Similarly, there are two navicular bones in every appendicular skeleton, one in each of the two ankles.
