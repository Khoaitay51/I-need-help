The axial consists of the skull, vertebral column, sacrum, coccyx, ribs, and sternum. And, the appendicular skeletal system is composed of the bones of the arms and legs. the appendicular skeleton is the limbs and limb girdles that are attached to the axial skeleton and the axial skeleton is the head and trunk. This includes the skull, ribs, sternum, and vertebral column. The appendicular skeleton are the bones of the appendages (arms, legs) including the shoulder blade and the bones of the pelvic girdle.
