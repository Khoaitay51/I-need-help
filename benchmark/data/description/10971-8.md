appendicular skeleton, the bones of the limbs and their girdles, attached to the axial skeleton. Compare axial skeleton. A term that dignifies the bones of appendages (extremities), including those of the shoulder girdle, upper extremity, pelvis and lower extremities. appendicular skeleton. the bones of the limbs. axial skeleton. the skull, spine, ribs and sternum. visceral skeleton. 1. the skeleton that forms part of an organ such as the os penis or os cordis. 2. the bony framework that protects the viscera, such as the sternum, ribs or pelvis.
