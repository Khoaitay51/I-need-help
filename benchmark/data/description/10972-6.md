Students are encouraged to use the dissertation as an opportunity to contribute to the field in a way that is meaningful to them. The Clinical Dissertation. An essential component in doctoral education is the dissertation. A clinical dissertation is a scholarly work conducted independently by the student, under the supervision of a full-time Psychology Division faculty member in collaboration with two additional committee members.
