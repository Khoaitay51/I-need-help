Definition. The word bioarchaeology was first used in 1972 by the British archaeologist Grahame Clark (see Clark 1972), who employed the term to describe his analysis of faunal remains at Star Carr, a prehistoric site in North Yorkshire, England, and was further defined in Clark 1973. With this article, Clark defined bioarchaeology both as “the archaeology concerned first and foremost with life,” (p. 464) and as “the archaeology of how men occupied territories and maintained life” (p. 466). back to top.
