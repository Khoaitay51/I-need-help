Bullous emphysema is also known as vanishing lung syndrome. It is most often treated by surgical removal of the bulla, which can grow to 20 centimeters—more than a foot—in diameter. A bulla that takes up a third or more of the space in and around the affected lung is called a giant bulla. On its way to becoming a giant bulla, however, the air pocket in the lung doesn’t show symptoms beyond what the patient is experiencing from the underlying emphysema. Symptoms from a giant bulla can include: 1  Pressure in the chest. 2  Difficulty drawing breath. 3  Soreness. 4  A bloated feeling.
