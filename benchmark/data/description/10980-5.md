Pressure and crowding can occur as a result, which has led bullous emphysema to be given its nickname, “vanishing lung syndrome”. Essentially, the bullous emphysema is caused no differently than other stages of the disease and types are. Smoking is the single biggest contributing factor. Marijuana use is also suspected as being related to the development of bullous emphysema. However other less common causes such as regular exposure to pollutants, consistent inhalation of dust and other contaminants, frequent and recurrent infections of the respiratory system and others may also exist.
