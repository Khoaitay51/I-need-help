Summary. The contribution of bullous emphysema (BE) to the functional impairment of patients with concomitant diffuse emphysema (DE) and the confounding effects of BE on functional measurements were investigated. Gould and coworkers showed that in patients with BE the major determinant of the respiratory function impairment is the severity of the emphysema in non-bullous lung. 10 In accordance with this study, we observed poor correlations between the extent of BE and the functional measurements.
