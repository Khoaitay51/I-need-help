LGN neurons project to the primary visual cortex, which is located at the back of the head mostly on the medial side. The primary visual cortex has many names: V1, area 17, & striate cortex. The primary visual cortex lies in the fold of the calcarine sulcus. Describe the structure of primary visual cortex (V1). V1 is a map of the retina composed of a grid (1 mm by 1mm) of hypercolumns.  Each hypercolumn analyses information from one small region of retina.  Adjacent hypercolumns analyze information from adjacent areas
