From the LGN, visual information is relayed to the visual cortex. Some of the axons in the tract project to the superior colliculi, paired structures located on the roof of the midbrain. The superior colliculi help coordinate rapid movements of the eye toward a target. A total of 6 separate areas in the visual cortex exist: V1, V2, V3, V3a, V4, and V5. The primary visual cortex, or V1, is the first structure in the visual cortex, where the neurons form the LGN synapse.
