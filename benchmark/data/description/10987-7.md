If the fistula involves part of the bowel, it is sometimes necessary to bring the bowel out on to the skin of the abdominal wall (tummy) to make a false opening (stoma). The stoma is called a colostomy if it uses part of the colon, and called an ileostomy if it uses the small bowel. Fistulas are more common in the pelvic area. Fistulas are a rare side effect of cancer treatment. They can also develop as the result of a cancer growing. Symptoms of a fistula depend on where it is in the body. Common symptoms include: 1  leaking urine through the vagina or back passage.
