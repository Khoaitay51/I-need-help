Bake. There’s no single best way to bake salmon in the oven, but healthier methods incorporate less oil or butter and therefore contribute fewer additional calories and less fat. Baking is ideal for fillets, steaks and whole salmon. A clean grill is of fundamental importance, as grit and grime make fish stick to the hot grate, which can result in mangled portions. Once your grill is medium-hot, brush the grate with vegetable oil. Season the salmon lightly with salt and pepper, and if desired, rub each side with a small amount of olive oil. Place the fish skin side up on the hot grill, cover it, and let it cook for about 4 minutes before turning it over to repeat the process
