The extract of the acerola fruit is believed to have many health benefits. Rain Tree Health states that the acerola plant is classified as a small tree or shrub that produces bright red fruit. These fruits are succulent and slightly sour. The use of acerola as a natural herbal remedy is a common practice in its native areas of Brazil. History as a Supplement. Acerola extract is often used in North America because of its high vitamin C content. Until the discovery of the camu-camu plant, the richest known natural source of vitamin C was the extract of the acerola fruit.
