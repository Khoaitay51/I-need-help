Burning mouth syndrome (BMS) is a painful, frustrating condition described as a burning or scalding sensation in the tongue, lips, palate, or the entire mouth, usually without clinical and laboratory findings or any detectable reason. Other names for the condition include scalded mouth syndrome and stomatodynia. 1 The acidic fluids may cause mouth irritation and pain. 2  Nerve damage (neuropathy). 3  Damage to nerves that control taste and pain in the tongue may also result in a burning sensation on tongue. 4  Endocrine disorders.
