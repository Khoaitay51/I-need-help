Because your contributions to a traditional IRA are tax deductible, they reduce the amount of taxable income for the year in which you’re contributing. Therefore, you can simply designate that your contributions, made prior to April 15th or your filing date, be attributed to the previous year. Earnings from the account aren’t taxed until you withdraw them. This is just the opposite of a Roth IRA, which can’t save you tax money now, but will save you money on your taxes in retirement.
