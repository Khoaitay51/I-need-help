Map: the Gallipoli Peninsula, 1915. Map of the Turkish forts and minefields in the Dardanelles and on the Gallipoli Peninsula 1915. This map of the Turkish Gallipoli peninsula shows the forces on both sides at the time of the landing. The Anzac area is in the centre of the map. An arrow shows the landing at Anzac Cove, with an associated text box. The attack on Gallipoli was one of the more imaginative strategies of the First World War. The German army had delivered a crushing blow to Russia at Tannenberg at the start of the war and had been driving eastwards.
