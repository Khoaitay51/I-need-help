Coping with Certain Smells. There are many things an expectant mother can to do cope with a heightened sense of smell. The first and most obvious thing to do is to remove yourself from the area where the smell is coming from. Or, if you can stomach it, you can try to remove the culprit from the area where you want to be. As estrogen and other hormones are produced rapidly during the early stages of pregnancy, life as you know it can become increasingly difficult. The heightened sense of smell is usually accompanied by morning sickness, which doesn’t help the gut-churning situation.
