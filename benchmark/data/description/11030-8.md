1 There is no specific treatment for smell disorders. 2  If the cause is due to medication, adjusting or changing the drug may relieve symptoms. 3  If an underlying illness causes the smell disorder, when that illness resolves or is treated the sense of smell usually returns. 4  Surgery can remove nasal polyps. If you experience a problem with your sense of smell, call your doctor. This fact sheet explains smell and smell disorders. Many people who have smell disorders also notice problems with their sense of taste.
