Bile derivatives like bile salts, bile salt mixtures and pure substances like sodium deoxycholate and sodium taurocholate are used as selective agents mainly to inhibit gram-positive organisms, non-intestinal organisms and spore formers while permitting the growth of gram-negative enterics. Bile is secreted through the bile duct into the intestine when food passes from the stomach to the duodenum. Most of the bile salts secreted into the upper region of the small intestine are absorbed along with dietary lipids at the lower end of the small intestine.
