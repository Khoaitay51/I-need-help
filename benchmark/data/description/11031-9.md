Bile Salts as a Solution. Much research has pointed to the use of bile salt supplements as an effective means of dealing with the digestive disorders common after gallbladder surgery. Taken with a meal, bile salts help to digest fats and aid in absorption of the fat soluble vitamins. 1  Dosage – the dosage of bile salts will depend on the brand, the type of bile salt, and the milligram content of the supplement.
