Mount Baker (USA) Facts. Mount Baker is a large, active, andesitic stratovolcano, rising to an elevation of 3285 m in the Cascade Range of northern Washington State. Its summit is approximately 100 km southeast of Vancouver, British Columbia, 30 km south of the Canada-United States border, and 50 km due south of Chilliwack, BC. Mount Baker is the largest volcanic complex in the northern part of the Cascade Volcanic Range.
