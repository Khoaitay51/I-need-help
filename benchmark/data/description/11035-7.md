From about 900,000 years ago to the present, numerous andesitic volcanic centers in the area have come and gone, eroded by glaciers. The largest is the Black Buttes edifice, active between 400,000 and 300,000 years ago and formerly bigger than today's Mount Baker. Tephra hazards at Mount Baker are less important than at neighboring Glacier Peak volcano to the south.
