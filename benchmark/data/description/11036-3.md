As mentioned, the hindbrain is comprised of the pons, medulla, and cerebellar structures of your brain, which together essentially act as the 'commander in charge' of your automated systems (talk about an important job!). Scientists refer to this 'reptilian' portion as the hindbrain; in other words, the rear portion of the brain. The hindbrain is the region of the brain formed by the pons, medulla oblongata (also known as just the medulla), and the cerebellum.
