OMICS Publishing Group is a predatory publisher of open access journals in a number of academic fields. It is part of the OMICS Group, based in Hyderabad, India. It issued its first publication in 2008. In 2012, OMICS Group had more than 200 journal titles, about 60% of which had no content. OMICS operates on an open access model, wherein the author pays for publication and the publisher makes the articles available for free.
