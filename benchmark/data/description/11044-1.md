United States, that the Government Order was not based on racial prejudice, but based on the fear of an incident similar to Pearl Harbor. Fred Korematsu was a Japanese American living in California who, after being ordered into a Japanese internment camp, refused to leave his city. Korematsu claimed that the Executive Order violated his personal rights as specified by the Fifth Amendment to the United States Constitution.
