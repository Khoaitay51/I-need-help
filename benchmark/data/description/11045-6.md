3 Policy Guidance Manual Following the recommendation of the High-Level Committee on Management in 2005, UNESCO and other UN system organizations have been working towards implementation of International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS). 4 purpose oF this manual This manual is intended to establish and give policy, practice and guidance on the application of IPSAS. It is not exhaustive and therefore should be used as a tool to complement the standards and other professional accounting pronouncements.
