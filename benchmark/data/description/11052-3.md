Potential Lumigan side effects. Lumigan may cause both temporary and permanent side effects. Lengthier, thicker lashes are a pleasant but temporary side effect. Among the unpleasant side effects include, potential darkening of the eyelids, and permanent brown pigmentation of the iris. Some negative side effects remain, including possible permanent brown pigmentation of the iris, and darkening of skin on the eyelids. Lumigan comes in two concentrations-one 0.01% and the other 0.03% Lumigan ophthalmic solution which cost approximately US$130 and US$100, respectively, for a 2.5ml bottle.
