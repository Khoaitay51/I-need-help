Users of Lumigan have reported a number of side effects experienced when taking the drug. Less serious side effects reported include mild eye discomfort and dizziness. However, more serious side effects were also reported. The serious side effects include redness, swelling, itching and discharge of the eye. Increased sensitivity to light and vision changes are also known side effects. If you experience any of the serious side effects mentioned, stop using Lumigan immediately and contact your doctor. As a treatment for glaucoma and ocular hypertension, Lumigan is a popular option which is also quite effective as attested by some users. However, side effects are commonly associated with its use. It is easy to purchase the eye solution since you do not need a doctor’s prescription.
