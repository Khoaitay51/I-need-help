Less serious side effects may include: 1  mild eye discomfort; 2  dizziness; 3  feeling like something is in your eye; 4  dry or watery eyes; or. 5  stinging or burning of the eyes after using the drops. Stop using bimatoprost ophthalmic and call your doctor at once if you have a serious side effect such as: 1  redness, swelling, itching, or pain in or around your eye; 2  oozing or discharge from your eye; 3  increased sensitivity to light; 4  vision changes.
