Symptoms of a serious allergic reaction may include: rash, itching/swelling (especially of the face/tongue/throat), severe dizziness, trouble breathing. This is not a complete list of possible side effects. Stop using bimatoprost ophthalmic and call your doctor at once if you have a serious side effect such as: 1  redness, swelling, itching, or pain in or around your eye; 2  oozing or discharge from your eye; 3  increased sensitivity to light; 4  vision changes.
