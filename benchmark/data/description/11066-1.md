Answers. Best Answer: B lymphocytes or B cells secrete antibodies and act in humoral immunity. Humoral Immunity results in the production of antibodies that circulate around the body in blood and lymph (humors). Defends against: free bacteria and viruses. Cell-mediated Immunity: defense activities are carried out by specialized cells circulating around the body. Defends against: host cells infected with bacteria and viruses, fungi and protozoans, tissue transplants, own cancer cells.
