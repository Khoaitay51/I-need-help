Hums Constantly. by Joan. (Fresno, CA, USA). My mother hums constantly, from the moment she wakes until she goes to bed. She's 87. It's not all humming either, it's grunting and hissing sounds. I've heard the same tunes and grunts and hisses for years. It's driven me bonkers. She hums while eating, while in the doctor's office, the car, anywhere.
