Pickling herring in vinegar and salt became popular as a technique to preserve filleted herring and make it last through the winter. Herbs and spices were then added to create interest. Today, pickled herring appears on the table at nearly all festivals in Sweden, with the exception of crayfish parties. Three types of pickled herring with trimmings is a popular starter in Swedish restaurants. It is usually served with some egg, a nice piece of Vaesterbottensost, västerbottensost a little sour, cream new (potatoes if in), season some crispbread and a glass of. snaps
