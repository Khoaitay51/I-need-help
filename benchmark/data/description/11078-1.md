Literally translated from the French phrase nobility obligates, noblesse oblige means that privilege entails responsibility (Oxford American Desk Dictionary and Thesaurus, 2nd ed, 2001). Similarly, The New Dictionary of Cultural Literacy, 3rd ed. Noblesse Oblige literally means 'Nobility Obligates'. The obligation of Honorable, generous, and responsible behavior associated with high rank or birth.
