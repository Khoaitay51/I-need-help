Slave to Free. Before the general emancipation of American slaves during the Civil War, many secured their own freedom through escape, self-purchase, or being freed by the slaveholder. The conditions of former slave and freeman did not occur simultaneously for many slaves, psychologically or in reality. Runaways who arrived in free territory were fugitives with an ambiguous status for weeks or years, especially after the passage of the Fugitive Slave Act in 1850.
