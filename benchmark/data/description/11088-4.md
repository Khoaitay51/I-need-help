Socially, the Civil War emancipated the blacks which gave them a sense of freedom but there was still racial discrimination, and most of them were poor. After it all it ended being mostly the same that the 1% rich landowners controlled everything. Economically, the Civil War devastated the South. They used up all their resources fighting in the war. Many many people went into debt. The crop-lien system was started which kept people in even more debt. Beside the 1% landowners and merchants most people were poor and in debt.
