Conditions that may cause belching include: 1  gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD): disorder that causes acid from the stomach to flow upward into the esophagus, or food pipe. 2  gastroparesis (also called delayed gastric emptying): disorder in which the muscles in your stomach wall are weakened. Self-Treatment. If you are belching excessively or if your stomach is distended and you cannot expel the air, lying on your side usually helps. Adopting a knees-to-chest position can also be helpful. Hold the position until the gas passes.
