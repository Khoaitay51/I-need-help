Step 2 Complete certified mail form 3800 at the post office Complete Form 3800, the certified mail form, at your local post office. Fill out the name and mailing address of the recipient of the package. At the post office, simply have the mail person calculate your fee, and pay with cash, debit, or credit card. Step 5 Keep your receipt Keep your receipt and bar code in a secure place. Quick Tip: Choose the return receipt option if you wish to be sent a signed confirmation that the package was received.
