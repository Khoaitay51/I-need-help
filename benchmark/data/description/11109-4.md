Burrough is an east Devon name in 1881 and Burrow seems to have two origins: one in Devon, the other in Cumbria. Forms with a final s are much less common in Devon than further north and east in England. Widely distributed across England, in Devon there were high numbers in Barnstaple in 1881; in 1332 only 1 in South Pool (as Herdyng) but Herding and Harding appear just north of Exeter in the Eyre of 1238.
