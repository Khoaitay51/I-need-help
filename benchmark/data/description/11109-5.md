Cayuga County, New York Surname~Cyber~Searchers-S. If you would like to be added to this surname page, please cayuga@roadrunner.com (Bernie Corcoran) be sure to include the surname that you are researching, your name, your email address, the Cayuga County location and dates that you want listed.. This page is intended to provide a web site of surnames for genealogy research in Cayuga County, NY. If the surname does not have a direct link to Cayuga County, please do not submit them here. Questions related to your genealogy research should be submitted to the Cayuga County Rootsweb/Ancestry Message Boards.
