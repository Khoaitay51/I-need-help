1 Quaternary Structure-refers to the structure of a protein macromolecule formed by interactions between multiple polypeptide chains. 2  Each polypeptide chain is referred to as a subunit. 3  Proteins with quaternary structure may consist of more than one of the same type of protein subunit. 1 There are two types of secondary structures observed in proteins. 2  One type is the alpha (α) helix structure. 3  This structure resembles a coiled spring and is secured by hydrogen bonding in the polypeptide chain. 4  The second type of secondary structure in proteins is the beta (β) pleated sheet.
