If your grandfather was younger than 70½ and his estate was listed as the beneficiary of the IRA, the estate must withdraw the entire balance within five years, which would be no later than 2013. Withdrawals by beneficiaries are not subject to the 10-percent penalty for early withdrawals prior to age 59½. The penalty is waived for inherited IRAs. Most IRAs have a designated beneficiary that is an individual, which precludes the need for a probate proceeding.
