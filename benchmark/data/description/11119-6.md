Generally, if the taxpayer receives distributions directly from the inherited IRA, the distributions are taxed, but the 10% penalty tax on premature withdrawals does not apply, even if the beneficiary is under the age of 59 1/2. Therefore, the money retained its character as an amount received by a beneficiary from an inherited IRA and the distribution was made to a beneficiary on account of a death. Thus the distribution was not subject to the 10% penalty tax.
