It is the lowest ranking General Officer between the ranks of Colonel and Major General. The Bangladesh Army introduced the rank of Brigadier General in 2001, however the grade stayed equivalent to Brigadier. Brigadier General is equivalent to Commodore of Bangladesh Navy & Air Commodore of Bangladesh Air Force. The equivalent rank for Brigadier General in Iranian army and air force is called Sar-Tip. It is above the rank of Sar-Hang (colonel) and below the rank of Sar-Lashkar (major general). Notice that Iranian Revolutionary Guards also use this rank. The difference is in salutation.
