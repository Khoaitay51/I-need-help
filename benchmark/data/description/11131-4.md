No one factor causes acne. Acne occurs when sebaceous (oil) glands attached to the hair follicles are stimulated at the time of puberty by circulating male hormones. Sebum (oil) is a natural substance that lubricates and protects the skin. 1 Dirt: Blackheads are oxidized oil, not dirt. 2  Sweat does not cause acne and is produced by entirely separate glands in the skin. 3  On the other hand, excessive washing can dry and irritate the skin. 4  Stress: Some people get so upset by their pimples that they pick at them and make them last longer
