RTI Cryogenics Inc. has the technology to produce a 100% recycled thermoplastic elastomer (TPE) pellet from recycled cryo crumb rubber and recycled post-industrial and post-consumer plastic. This pellet is called XyComTM TPE and it is sold under a 10 year take or pay, global Off Take Agreement. Our vision is to become the world leader in Value Added Tire Recycling by installing at least 15 of RTI's turnkey patent pending XyComTM TPE plants worldwide in the next 3 years.
