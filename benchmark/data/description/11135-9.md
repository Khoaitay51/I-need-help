PVC Polyvinyl Chloride. PVC sheet is medium impact, high corrosion resistant material, ideally suited where chemical resistance is necessary. PVDF Polyvinylidene Fluoride PVDF, manufactured under the names of Kynar and Hylar, is a high performance thermoplastic that combines great mechanical strength and high abrasion resistance with a broad temperature range. It is highly resistant to most environmental conditions and corrosive materials.
