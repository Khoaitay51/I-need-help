Cooking the Filet. 1  Philosophically, high heat = great meat. 2  Cook the filets at high temperature to get a great sear on the outside that is crunchy, tasty, and great looking. 3  Control the doneness (from rare to well done) with the length of time you leave the filets in the oven – Not the temperature. 1 Turn the steak over, and allow it to sear for another 3 – 4 minutes. 2  Near the end of those 3 – 4 minutes add a couple tablespoons of butter, and the herb of your choice. 3  I love rosemary, thyme also is great. 4  Baste the steaks with melted herb butter. 5  If you like your steak very rare, you can probably take it off now.
