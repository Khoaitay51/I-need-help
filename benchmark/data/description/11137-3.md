1 Take the filet mignon out of the fridge about 30 minutes before you want to cook it, and season it liberally with salt and pepper. 2  That time allows the meat to come to room temperature – which will help ensure you cook it to the right doneness. 3  Getting a good sear on the filet is critical. 1 Turn the steak over, and allow it to sear for another 3 – 4 minutes. 2  Near the end of those 3 – 4 minutes add a couple tablespoons of butter, and the herb of your choice. 3  I love rosemary, thyme also is great. 4  Baste the steaks with melted herb butter. 5  If you like your steak very rare, you can probably take it off now.
