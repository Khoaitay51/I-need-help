con•sti•tu•tion. 1. the way in which a thing is composed or made up; makeup; composition. 2. the physical character of the body as to strength, health, etc.: a strong constitution. 3. the aggregate of a person's physical and psychological characteristics. 4. the act or process of constituting; establishment. b. The document in which such a system is recorded. c. Constitution The supreme law of the United States, consisting of the document ratified by the original thirteen states (1787-1790) and subsequent amendments.
