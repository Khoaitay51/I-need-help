Product Description. This U.S. Army Departmental/Organizational flag is made to government specifications and is available in 4 sizes. Rayon Embroidered-These U.S. Army ceremonial flags feature the Army service seal fully embroidered onto both sides of the flag fabric. They are finished with a flannel-lined pole hem heading with leather tabs and golden rayon fringe for indoor display. Per US Army Flag regulations (AR-840), the 3x4Ft size flag is used when displaying this flag along side the unit's organizational (Regimental, Divison, Brigade, Corps,) flags. The 4'4 x 5'6 flag will be used when carried by an Honor or color guard and when displayed alongside the US parade flag (4'4 x 5'6.)
