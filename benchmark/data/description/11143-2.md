The heart of the brands. BMW Welt is located directly adjacent to the BMW company headquarters, the BMW main plant and the BMW Museum. An architectural masterpiece, it is both the delivery centre for new BMW automobiles and a brand experience rolled into one. The Junior Campus at BMW Welt gives young visitors an introduction to the world of mobility and is a place they can explore with zeal. In addition, BMW Welt is a much sought-after venue for numerous events.
