The Harmonic and Melodic Minor scales are similar, except the Melodic Minor contains a natural sixth instead of the flat sixth of the Harmonic Minor. The Melodic Minor scale is also similar to the Major scale (although still with a distinctly different pattern of intervals). The only difference is whether the third note makes a minor or major interval. In classical music theory, the Melodic Minor has a very unusual property, not found in any other scale type.
