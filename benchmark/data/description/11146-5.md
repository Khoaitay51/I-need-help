(4.5) Melodic Minor Scale. We have seen two minor scales so far-the Natural Minor, which is a mode of the Major scale, and the Harmonic Minor, which has a distinctly different pattern of intervals. The distinctive sound of the Harmonic Minor comes from the three semitone interval between the sixth and seventh notes. The Melodic Minor scale is also similar to the Major scale (although still with a distinctly different pattern of intervals). The only difference is whether the third note makes a minor or major interval. In classical music theory, the Melodic Minor has a very unusual property, not found in any other scale type.
