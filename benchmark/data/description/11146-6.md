The three kinds of minor scale are called the natural minor scale, the harmonic minor scale, and the melodic minor scale. For the purposes of this mini-course it is not necessary for you to understand or to hear the differences between the three kinds of minor scale. The most reliable way to tell the difference between major and minor scales is by focusing on the lower part of the scale. In particular, of the first five notes of the scale, only one, the third note, is ever different between major and minor scales.
