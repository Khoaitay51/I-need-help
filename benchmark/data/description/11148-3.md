Generally weak acids and bases are good examples of weak electrolytes. Some weak electrolytes are listed in given table. Non-electrolytes are the substances which cannot conduct electricity in their aqueous solution due to the absence of ions. Some electrolytes such as potassium chloride, sodium hydroxide, sodium nitrate are completely ionized in their constituent ions in solution. These electrolytes are known as strong electrolytes. In other words strong electrolytes are 100% dissociated in their aqueous solution.
