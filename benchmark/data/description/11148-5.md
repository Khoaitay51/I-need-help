Substances like acid, base and salts which can conduct electricity in their aqueous solution due to ionization are known as electrolytes.. The presence of ions in solution is responsible for the conduction and when the current passes through the wires, it shows the movement of free electrons through wire. Some electrolytes such as potassium chloride, sodium hydroxide, sodium nitrate are completely ionized in their constituent ions in solution. These electrolytes are known as strong electrolytes. In other words strong electrolytes are 100% dissociated in their aqueous solution.
