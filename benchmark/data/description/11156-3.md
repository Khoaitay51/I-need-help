Today, the Balkans include these independent countries: Greece, Albania, Macedonia, Bulgaria, Romania, rump Yugoslavia (Serbia and Montenegro), and Bosnia. Geographically, European Turkey, a small region around Istanbul, is located in the Balkans. Some scholars also consider Croatia to be part of the Balkans. The Balkans.
