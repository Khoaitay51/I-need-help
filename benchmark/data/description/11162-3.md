Fat-soluble vitamins, which are A, D, K and E, absorb with the help of fat and are stored in fat deposits throughout your body, as well as in your liver. Vitamin A, from dairy, dark leafy greens and eggs, includes a series of carotenoids and phytochemicals that protect your eyes and vision. These vitamins do not stay in your body, rather your system uses what it needs in a short period of time and then excretes any excess through urine. Because water-soluble vitamins do not have a storage place in the body, you need to get them from your daily diet.
