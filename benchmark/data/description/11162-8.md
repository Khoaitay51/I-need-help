Vitamins fit into one of two categories: water-soluble or fat-soluble. They are classified based on how they dissolve in your body. Water-soluble vitamins dissolve in water, while fat-soluble vitamins dissolve in fat. These vitamins do not stay in your body, rather your system uses what it needs in a short period of time and then excretes any excess through urine. Because water-soluble vitamins do not have a storage place in the body, you need to get them from your daily diet.
