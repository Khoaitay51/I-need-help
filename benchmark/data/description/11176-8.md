Thus, if you find that you or your spouse is falling out of love, you must begin at the beginning and start over: 1  Change. 2  Accept. 3  Fulfill. Falling Out of Love. Since falling in love is a process of moving from attraction, acceptance and fulfillment, falling out of love is just the opposite. When a person moves backward through the three stages (from interdependence to attraction), the feelings of love diminish.
