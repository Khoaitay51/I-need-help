The Claddagh ring (Irish: fainne Fáinne) chladaigh is a Traditional irish ring given which represents, love, loyalty and (friendship the hands represent, friendship the heart represents, love and the crown represents). loyalty These elements symbolize the qualities of love (the heart), friendship (the hands), and loyalty (the crown). A . Fenian  Claddagh ring, without a crown, is a slightly different take on the design, but has not achieved the level of popularity of the crowned version.
