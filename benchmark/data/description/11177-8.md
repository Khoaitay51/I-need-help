Claddagh Ring Meaning. The Claddagh's distinctive design features two hands clasping a heart, and usually surmounted by a crown. The elements of this symbol are often said to correspond to the qualities of love (the heart), friendship (the hands), and loyalty (the crown). Hands for friendship, a heart for love, and a crown for loyalty. Claddagh Rings have embodied the spirit of connection for hundreds of years. This traditional design is just as powerful today. Whether to signify friendship, engagement or matrimony, our Claddagh rings are fitting for any occasion.
