• DEPICTION (noun). The noun DEPICTION has 4 senses: 1. a graphic or vivid verbal description. 2. a representation by picture or portraiture. 3. a drawing of the outlines of forms or objects. 4. representation by drawing or painting etc. Familiarity information: DEPICTION used as a noun is uncommon. Meaning: A graphic or vivid verbal description. Classified under: Nouns denoting communicative processes and contents.
