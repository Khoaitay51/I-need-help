Lorenz /lo-renz/ [2 sylls.] as a name for boys. Lorenz's name origin is Latin, and the meaning of Lorenz is from Laurentum. Lorenz is a variant form of Lawrence (Latin) with the prefix Lo- . Lorenz is also a variant of Lorenzo (Spanish, Italian, Latin). Kreatif forms: Lerenz, Lirenz. [ more ]. Lorenz is an uncommonly occurring given name for men but a very prominent last name for all people (#2295 out of 150436, Top 2%). (2000 U.S. Census).
