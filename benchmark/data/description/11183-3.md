Swiss Cheese. A popular term used in various specialties referring to a gross appearance, microscopic pattern, or morphology characterised by multiple, sharply demarcated variably sized and randomly scattered rounded spaces superimposed on a relatively homogeneous or solid background, thus being likened to Swiss cheese. Liver Swiss cheese refers to the changes in the liver in peliosis hepatis which is punctuated by multiple 0.2 mm in diameter blood-filled lacunae, often devoid of endothelial lining, which may represent distended sinusoids or vessels.
