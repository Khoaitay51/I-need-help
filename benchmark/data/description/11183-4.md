Figure 1. Axial T2 (A) and FLAIR (B) weighted MRI demonstrating dramatically enlarged Virchow Robin spaces. This swiss cheese appearance is caused by unusually large Virchow-Robin spaces (VRS), normal perivascular extensions of the subarachnoid space. Large VRS can be a phenomenon of aging or cerebral atrophy, particularly over the hemispheric convexity. VRS 2 cm in diameter have been described in normal patients. VRS are FLAIR and T1 hypointense, and T2 hyperintense
