Dementia: A Brain Made of Swiss Cheese. What a terrible condition dementia is. It takes the brain of a bright, loving, proud, communicative – add your own adjectives – individual and turns him or her into an infant. He has nothing to say to them and hands the phone over to me. It's sad and frustrating to watch the brain turn to Swiss cheese, letting all the things learned over a lifetime slowly fall through the holes. But that's the way with dementia. We learn to live with it and cope the best we can
