An associate degree is available from a community college or university; the program lasts two years, and a graduate RN is eligible to take the licensing exams after graduation. Diploma nurses complete two-or three-year programs at a hospital-based school of nursing. MAs work in doctors’ offices or clinics, where they provide basic administrative and clinical support. Registered nurses, on the other hand, are highly educated licensed professionals who manage complex and often critically ill patients.
