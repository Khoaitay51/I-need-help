A medical assistant who wants to become a registered nurse must follow the traditional path of becoming a nurse by going to nursing school and passing the required registered nursing license exam. Becoming a registered nurse can greatly increase a medical assistant's earnings. According to 2014 U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' data, median pay for medical assistants was $29,960, and the median salary for registered nurses was $66,640. Registered nurses, on the other hand, must have at least an associates degree in nursing. Many have bachelor's degrees. This means a medical assistant needs to complete additional education and training to become a registered nurse.
