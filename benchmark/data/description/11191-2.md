The amount of oxygen dissolved in water is expressed as a concentration, in milligrams per liter (mg/l) of water. Dissolved oxygen levels are also often reported in percent saturation. Temperature affects DO concentrations, and calculating the percent saturation will factor out the effect of temperature. The saturation level is the maximum concentration of dissolved oxygen that would be present in water at a specific temperature, in the absence of other factors.
