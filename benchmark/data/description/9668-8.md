Irie (I-rie \I ' -ree) is the word in Jamaican Patois that means, alright. The term can be used to mean 1: powerful and pleasing; 2: excellent, highest; n 3: the state of feeling great. It is commonly used by members of the Rastafari movement (see Iyaric). Irie may also refer to: 1  Irie Maffia, Hungarian band formed in 2005. 2  Irie Time, American reggae band formed in the early 1990s. 3  Muroran Irie Stadium, athletic stadium in Japan.
