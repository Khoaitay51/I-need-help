This topic discusses how Permanent Residents can obtain, replace or renew their Permanent Resident Card. The Permanent Resident Card, or “PR Card”, is an identification card which shows a person has permanent resident status in Canada. Permanent Residents have the right to apply for citizenship after three years of physical residency in Canada and meeting other eligibility requirements. The process to obtain a PR card, or to replace or renew it, is the same. The applicant must apply while physically present in Canada and prove entitlement to Permanent Resident status by accounting for all absences from Canada in the last five years
