Note: If you are a conditional permanent resident you cannot replace your card if it is expired, or within 90 days of expiring. A conditional permanent resident whose card is expiring may apply to have the conditions on his or her permanent resident status removed. Once USCIS receives your Form I-90, we will process your application and then you will receive: A receipt notice of your Form I-90, Application to Replace Permanent Resident Card, by mail at the address you provided on your application.
