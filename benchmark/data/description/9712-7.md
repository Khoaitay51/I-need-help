Rotation notation is usually denoted R(center, degrees).  1  Center  is the 'center of rotation.' This is the point around which you are performing your mathematical rotation.  2  Degrees  stands for how many degrees you should rotate. 3  A positive number usually by convention means counter clockwise. In math, a rotation lives up to its name! To rotate an object you need a center of rotation and how much you want to rotate it.
