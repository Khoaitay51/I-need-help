English for Students. (Adjective) Just a polite remark is enough to correct his mannerism. Synonyms: Elegant Polished Urbane Genteel Gallant Courtly Courteous Civil Complaisance Chivalrous Cultivated Contextual Examples: The table was covered with an elegant lace cloth. My aunt Mary was the most genteel woman I ever knew. The Synonyms and Antonyms form an integral part of the English Language. Acquaintance with the vocabulary of the English language is a necessity for effective expression either in written or in an oral from. Synonyms are nothing but the similar meanings of a particular word or its semantic relation.
