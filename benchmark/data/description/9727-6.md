♦ politeness n-uncount She listened to him, but only out of politeness. 2 adj You can refer to people who consider themselves to be socially superior and to set standards of behaviour for everyone else as polite society or polite company. ADJ n Certain words are vulgar and not acceptable in polite society. The actual say is: You can catch more flies with honey than with vinegar This means that it is easier to persuade people if you use polite arguments and flattery than if you are confrontational.
