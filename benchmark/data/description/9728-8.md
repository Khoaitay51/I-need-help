1 Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura. 2  This is a rare condition that occurs when small blood clots suddenly form throughout your body, using up large numbers of platelets. 3  Hemolytic uremic syndrome. 4  This rare disorder causes a sharp drop in platelets, destruction of red blood cells and impairment of kidney function. Platelets are produced in your bone marrow. If production is low, you may develop thrombocytopenia. Factors that can decrease platelet production include: 1  Leukemia. 2  Some types of anemia. 3  Viral infections, such as hepatitis C or HIV. 4  Chemotherapy drugs
