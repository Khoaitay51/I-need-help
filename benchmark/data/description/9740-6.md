Guide. An immunoglobulins test is done to measure the level of immunoglobulins, also known as antibodies, in your blood. Antibodies are substances made by the body's immune system in response to bacteria, viruses, fungus, animal dander, or cancer cells. 1 IgM antibodies are the largest antibody. 2  They are found in blood and lymph fluid and are the first type of antibody made in response to an infection. 3  They also cause other immune system cells to destroy foreign substances. 4  IgM antibodies are about 5% to 10% of all the antibodies in the body.
