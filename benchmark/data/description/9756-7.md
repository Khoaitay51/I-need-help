Felicity /fe-lic-i-ty/ [4 sylls.] as a girls' name is pronounced fa-LISS-a-tee. It is of Old French and Latin origin, and the meaning of Felicity is lucky. From felicitas (see Felix). A virtue name first used in the 17th century. Charted below is the birth name popularity of Felicity for girls. Felicity entered the list in 1998 and reached its peak position of #390 in the U.S. in the year 1999, and is presently at #497. (2014 Birth Statistics).
