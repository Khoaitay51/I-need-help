determine verb (DECIDE). C1 [T often passive] to ​control or ​influence something ​directly, or to ​decide what will ​happen: The ​number of ​staff we can take on will be determined by how much ​money we're ​allowed to ​spend. Your ​health is determined in ​part by what you ​eat. Eye ​colour is ​genetically determined. › to discover the facts or truth about something: determine whether/why/how, etc. The ​office will ​conduct a ​follow-up ​audit to determine whether ​changes and ​improvements have been made.
