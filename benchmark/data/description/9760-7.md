determine. 1. (= ascertain, define) [+ cause, meaning ] → determinar; [+ price, date ] → fijar, determinar; [+ scope, limits, boundary ] → definir, determinar. to determine what is to be done → determinar or decidir lo que hay que hacer. to determine whether sth is true → determinar si algo es verdad. 1. to settle or decide (an argument, question, etc) conclusively, as by referring to an authority. 2. (tr) to ascertain or conclude, esp after observation or consideration. 3. (tr) to shape or influence; give direction to: experience often determines ability.
