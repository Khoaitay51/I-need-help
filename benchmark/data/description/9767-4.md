3.1 Mean packet to packet delay variation. Packet to packet delay variation is used as a basis for the RTCP (RFC1889) jitter measurement. If the delay of two successive packets is t1 and t2 then the packet to packet delay variation is abs(t2-t1). (i) Type A – constant jitter. This is a roughly constant level of packet to packet delay variation. (ii) Type B – transient jitter. This is characterized by a substantial incremental delay that may be incurred by a single packet.
