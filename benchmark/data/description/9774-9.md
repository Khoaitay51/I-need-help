The World Rhodium Market. Rhodium — along with platinum, palladium, ruthenium, iridium, and osmium — is part of a group of elements referred to as the platinum group metals (PGMs) or platinum group elements (PGEs). Platinum group metals have similar physical and chemical properties, but rhodium is the rarest. 150 times more expensive than silver. In fact it's the most expensive of all precious metals. Yet no matter its rarity or cost, this metal is absolutely vital to the rapidly recovering automobile industry. The metal I'm talking about is rhodium.
