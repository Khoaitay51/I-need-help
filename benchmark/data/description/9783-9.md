From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia. Lateral surface of the brain with Brodmann's areas numbered. Medial surface of the brain with Brodmann's areas numbered. A Brodmann area is a region of the cortex defined based on its cytoarchitecture, or organization of cells. Brodmann areas were originally defined and numbered by Korbinian Brodmann based on the organization of neurons he observed in the cortex using the Nissl stain
