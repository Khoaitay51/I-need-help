In biology, tissue is a cellular organizational level intermediate between cells and a complete organ. A tissue is an ensemble of similar cells from the same origin that together carry out a specific function. Organs are then formed by the functional grouping together of multiple tissues. The study of tissue is known as histology or, in connection with disease, histopathology. Functions of epithelial tissue: 1  the cells of the body surface form the outer layer of skin. 2  inside the body, epithelial cells form the lining of the mouth & alimentary canal & protect these organs. 3  epithelial tissues help in absorption of water & nutrients.
