At their most basic, firewalls work like a filter between your computer/network and the Internet. You can program what you want to get out and what you want to get in. Everything else is not allowed. There are several different methods firewalls use to filter out information, and some are used in combination. The need of Firewalls for Personal Use. 1  For home use, firewalls work much more simply. 2  The main goal of a personal firewall is to protect your personal computer and private network from malicious mischief.
