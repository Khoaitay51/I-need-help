Look after your eyes » Eye conditions » Squint. Squint (also known as strabismus) is a condition that arises because of an incorrect balance of the muscles that move the eye, faulty nerve signals to the eye muscles and focusing faults (usually long sight). 1 This can happen in either eye and the misalignment can be in any direction. 2  A squint may occur for a number of reasons. 3  Sometimes, one or more of the muscles that move the eyes is in the wrong place. 4  Sometimes the muscles do not work as they should.
