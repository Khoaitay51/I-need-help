Description. The word Bandogge is the generic name for any Bulldog-type Mastiff breed. The Bandogge has a very prominent musculature and an angulation that gives the dog a very athletic and agile look. Most are black brindle but others colors include black, blue, red and tawny. Mr. Vegas the Bandogge Mastiff at 4 years old. Diesel the American Bandogge at 2 years old. This is my boy Mr. Yanyeroo at 10 months old. He's very affectionate with a good temperament; a nice-looking dog who loves to play outdoors.
