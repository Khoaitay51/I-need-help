Constipation is very common and affects about 30% of children. Soiling describes the leakage of liquid or formed stool in underwear. Over one-half of children with functional constipation and soiling recover after 6 to 24 months of closely following treatment recommendations. The treatment includes:  Medical evaluation.  Teaching for you and your child about the constipation problem.  'Clean out' if there is an impaction of stool in the rectum.
