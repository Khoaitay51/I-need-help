Functional Constipation. Pathogenesis and Management. Constipation is a very common clinical problem, and a questionnaire-based study in Olmsted County, Minnesota, demonstrated that up to 20% of patients report symptoms consistent with functional constipation. Approximately 40% of such patients have historic evidence of needing to strain excessively to evacuate the bowel.
