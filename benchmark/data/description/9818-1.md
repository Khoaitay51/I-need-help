Some less common causes of hoarseness include: 1  polyps (abnormal growths) on the vocal cords. 2  throat, thyroid, or lung cancer. 3  damage to the throat, such as from the insertion of a tube. 4  male adolescence (when the voice deepens). 5  poorly functioning thyroid gland. Common Underlying Causes of Hoarseness. Hoarseness is typically caused by a viral infection in the upper respiratory tract. Other common factors that can cause, contribute to, or worsen your condition include: 1  acid reflux. 2  smoking. 3  drinking caffeinated and alcoholic beverages.
