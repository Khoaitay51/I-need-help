The English word bear comes from Old English bera and belongs to a family of names for the bear in Germanic languages that originate from an adjective meaning  brown . In Scandinavia, the word for bear is bjoern (björn or), bjoern bjørn and is a relatively common given name. for males The IUCN lists six bear species as vulnerable or endangered, and even least concern species, such as the brown bear, are at risk of extirpation in certain countries. The poaching and international trade of these most threatened populations are prohibited, but still ongoing.
