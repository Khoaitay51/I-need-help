Bear Hands is an American post-punk and indie rock band consisting of Dylan Rau (vocals and guitar), Val Loper (bass), Ted Feldman (guitar), and TJ Orscher (drums). Hailing from Brooklyn, New York, and formed in 2006, the band signed with Cantora Records in 2010 upon the release of its single, What a Drag. After releasing their debut LP Burning Bush Supper Club in 2010, Bear Hands toured as the opening act for Passion Pit, Gza, and We Were Promised Jetpacks.
