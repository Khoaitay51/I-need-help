The word carmel is a variant of caramel. Caramel is derived from the caramelization of sugar. It is made by heating sugar slowly to around 170 °C (338 °F). As the sugar melt … s and approaches this temperature, the molecules break down and re-form into compounds with a characteristic caramel color and flavor. Carmel is an English feminine name of early Christian origin. It is a reference to  Our Lady of Carmel , one title given by Christians to the Virgin Mary. Carmel means garden or orchard in Hebrew. Carmel is the name of a mountain near modern Haifa, Israel, where hermits lived in very early Christian times. These later became the Carmelite Order of monks
