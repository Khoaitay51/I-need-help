The CNS consists of your brain and spinal cord. The brain and spinal cord are inside your skull and vertebrae (the vertebrae make up your backbone). These bones protect the CNS when you get into accidents. The other part of the nervous system is the Peripheral Nervous System (or PNS). The PNS consists mainly of the nerves that go to and from the CNS. Unlike the CNS, though, there is no bony protection for the PNS. Have you ever hit your funny bone?. That odd feeling was you pinching one of the nerves in your arm
