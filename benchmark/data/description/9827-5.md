To become a registered biomedical scientist, you will need: 1  an Institute of Biomedical Science (IBMS) accredited BSc (Hons) degree. 2  at least one year’s experience at an approved laboratory. 3  registration as a biomedical scientist with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC). Starting salary. £21,692 + per year. Biomedical scientists test samples from patients to support doctors and healthcare professionals in the diagnosis and treatment of disease. If you enjoy science and you are interested in medicine and health care, this could be a great job choice for you.
