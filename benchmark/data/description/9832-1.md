Main Image: Martensite is a hard form of steel crystalline structure formed through the displacive transformation and have a distinguishing body-centered tetragonal crystalline structure. Martensites consist of a group of hard minerals which occur as plate-shaped crystal granules. 1 Martensite has a lower density than austenite, so that the martensitic transformation causes a moderate volume change. 2  More important factor than the volume change is the shear strain that has a magnitude nearly 0.26 that find out the shape of the martensite plates.
