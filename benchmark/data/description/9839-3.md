There is no 1 way to cook adobo. Depending on the region or the household, or how they want it to taste, they have their own way to cook it. However the standard ingredients in any version are the vinegar, soy sauce, garlic and peppercorn. Some filipinos even cook it without soy sauce just plain salt. Cover and marinate chicken for 1-3 hours. Bring to boil, then lower heat. Cover and let simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Uncover and simmer until sauce is reduced and thickened, and chicken is tender, about 20 more minutes.
