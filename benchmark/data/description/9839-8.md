Pulaw/Flickr. Definition: Adobo comes from the Spanish word adobar, which literally means to marinate. It is a common all-purpose seasoning that is used to flavor and/or marinate meat, chicken, or fish. Adobo can be prepared a couple of ways: a dry spice mix and a wet rub paste. The central ingredients of adobo are garlic, oregano, black pepper, and turmeric.
