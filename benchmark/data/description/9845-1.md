1 – 2 Tbs poppy seeds. Directions: 1. In a small bowl mix mustards together with a fork. Stir in honey and vinegar. 2. Whisk in Olive Oil until fully incorporated. 3. Stir in poppy seeds. From here you can move this over into some sort of “dressing” container. But I won’t judge you if you just keep it in the same bowl. And so easy to make. And not full of rancid PUFA grossness. This poppy seed honey mustard dressing is one of my favorites. I love the flavor it brings to whatever I put it on. It’s perfect for salads or used as a dip. My husband puts it on everything.
