There are four valves in your heart including the mitral, tricuspid, aortic and pulmonic valves. The aortic valve is located between the left ventricle (lower heart pumping chamber) and the aorta, which is the largest artery in the body. Valves maintain one-way blood flow through the heart. 1  Click here to learn more about the heart valves. Aortic valve disease occurs when the aortic valve does not work correctly.
