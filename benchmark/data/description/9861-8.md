Extensor tendinopathy or as it is most commonly know “tennis elbow” is pain in the outside of the elbow that comes from inflammation and degeneration of the tendons on the outside of the elbow. The tendons are part of the muscles that control movements of the wrist, hand and fingers. Extensor Tendinopathy is classified as an overuse injury occurring at the common extensor origin (CEO) on the outside of the elbow. The wrist extensor muscles are attached to the CEO via a common tendon. This overuse condition occurs due to repetitive forces at the CEO.
