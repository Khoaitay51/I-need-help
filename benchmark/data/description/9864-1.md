Whether sediment will be eroded, transported or deposited is depended on the particle size and the flow rate of the water. Flow (ft 3 /s) =Area (ft 2) * Velocity (ft/s). OR Flow (ft 3 /s) =Volume (ft 3)/ Time (s). The equations describing the relationship of water flow and sediment transport are a bit more complex. Organic sediment transport is will vary by location and season.
