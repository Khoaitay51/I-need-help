A taxis (plural taxes /ˈtaeksiːz/ , ˈtæksiːz from The Ancient greek, τάξις meaning) arrangement is the movement of an organism in response to a stimulus such as light or the presence of. food 1 Phototaxis is the movement of an organism in response to light: that is, the response to variation in light intensity and direction. 2  Negative phototaxis, or movement away from a light source, is demonstrated in some insects, such as cockroaches.
