First, let's look at the VIN plate off my 1968 Mustang Fastback: The VIN is...8T02C199168. There are two areas to plates to decode a '68 Mustang. The First is the VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) typically found on the dash as you look into the 68 Mustang from the Passengers side windshield. The second is the the Warranty Plate found on the Drivers door just below the door latch (as seen below). So if we decode the VIN on my Mustang, you will found out that it was made in 1968 in Metuchen, NJ. The Mustang was a Fastback with Bucket seats and a 289 2V. It was number 1199168 to roll off the assembly line.
