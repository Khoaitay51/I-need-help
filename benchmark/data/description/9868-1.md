A belief that 'small' hydropower systems are a source of clean energy with little or no environmental problems is driving the growing interest in mini, micro, and pico hydro systems that generate from less than 5 kilowatts up to 10 megawatts of energy. In a report on the environmental implications of renewable energy sources, the International Energy Agency (IEA) notes: Small-scale hydro schemes (SHS) tend to have a relatively modest and localised impact on the environment.
