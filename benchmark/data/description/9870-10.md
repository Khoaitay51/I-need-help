Most people who develop mesothelioma have worked in careers such as mining, where they inhaled or ingested asbestos fibers, or were exposed to airborne asbestos dust and fibers in other ways. Washing the clothing of a family member who worked with asbestos also creates a risk for developing mesothelioma. Symptoms or signs of mesothelioma may not appear until 20 to 50 years (or more) after exposure to asbestos.
