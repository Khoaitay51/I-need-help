The Kelpie is a soft-coated, medium-sized dog, generally with prick ears and an athletic appearance. Coat colours include black, black and tan, red, red and tan, blue, blue and tan, fawn, fawn and tan, cream, black and blue, and white and gold. The breed has been separated into two distinct varieties: the Show (or Bench) Kelpie and the Working Kelpie.
