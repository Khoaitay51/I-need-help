Answers. Best Answer: In short, a fingernail is made up of a globular protein called keratin which makes them impermeable (resistant) to water. Keratin is also the protein which makes your hair! So fingernails and toenails are actually a different kind of hair. Your nails grow from the area under your cuticle (matrix). As new cells grow, older cells become hard and compacted and are eventually pushed out toward your fingertips
