Nasal polyps can also cause sinusitis in the frontal sinus. Nasal polyps are caused by recurring inflammation of the sinuses, such as that resulting from asthma or allergies. They resemble small non-cancerous sacs, and are found in the sinuses or nasal passageways. They are lined with mucus membrane, which secretes fluid that moistens and protects the areas it covers. The size and shape of the frontal sinus can vary from person to person. These spaces fill up with mucus, which then drain into the nose.
