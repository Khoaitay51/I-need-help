Epistle. An Epistle (greek: ejpistolhv 'epistole') is a letter. The Epistles in the Bible are such books as Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Philippians, 1 Thessalonians, 2 Thessalonians, Philemon, 1 John, 2 John, and 3 John. The word 'epistole' occurs 24 times in the Greek New Testament.
