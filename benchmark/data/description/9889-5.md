Full Definition of EPISTLE. 1. capitalized a: one of the letters adopted as books of the New Testament b: a liturgical lection usually from one of the New Testament Epistles. 2. a: letter; especially: a formal or elegant letter b: a composition in the form of a letter. — epis·tler \-ˈpi-sə-lər\ noun.
