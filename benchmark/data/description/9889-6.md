A written communication; a term inclusive of all forms of written correspondence, personal and official, in vogue from an early antiquity. As applied to the twenty-one letters, which constitute well-nigh one-half of the New Testament, the word epistle has come to have chiefly a technical and exclusive meaning. e-pis'-'-l (epistole, a letter, epistle; from epistello, to send to): \Contents. 1.
