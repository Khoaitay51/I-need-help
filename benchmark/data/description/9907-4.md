[ 2 syll. myah, my-ah ] The baby girl name Myah is pronounced as M AY aa- †. Myah's language of origin is Latin and Burmese. It is predominantly used in English. Myah is a variation of Maya (English). Myah is also a variation (English) of Mya (English and Burmese). Myah is a rather popular baby girl name. The name's popularity has been growing since the 1990s; before that, it was of only irregular use.
