Etymology & Historical Origin-Myah. Myah is an elaboration of Mya which is considered a variant of Mia or Maya (see those names for more detailed information). Mia is considered a variant form of Miriam/Mary which were probably developed from the ancient Egyptian word “mry” meaning “beloved” (“mia” is also the Italian word for “mine”). Popularity of the Name Myah. Myah has not done nearly as well as Mya on the U.S. naming charts, but she seems to be holding her own just fine. The name first appeared on the Top 1000 list of female baby names in 1998 and has grown to modestly low levels of usage.
