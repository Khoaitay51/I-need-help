The arteries and veins. The ulnar and radial arteries carry blood down through the forearm into the wrist, where they anastomose (join together) to form arches. These arches, along with several branches, supply blood to the hand and digits. Here are the arteries that enter the wrist: Anterior interosseous artery: This artery runs from the ulnar artery anterior to the interosseous membrane. It pierces the membrane distally to join the dorsal carpal arch.
