The Gaskin Maneuver, also called all fours, is a technique to reduce shoulder dystocia. Gaskin introduced it in the U.S. in 1976 after learning it from a Belizean woman who had, in turn, learned the maneuver in Guatemala, where it originated. In this maneuver, the mother supports herself on her hands and knees to resolve shoulder dystocia. Ina May Gaskin at Nambassa 3 day Music & Alternatives festival, New Zealand 1981. Ina May Gaskin, CPM, (born March 8th, 1940) has been described as the mother of authentic midwifery ..
