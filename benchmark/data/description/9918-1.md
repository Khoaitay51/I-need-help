Similarly, the acronym TMA, which is frequently used for tetramethylammonium in the pharmacological literature, may also refer to the investigational drug 3,4,5-trimethoxyamphetamine, which, being a close structural analog of mescaline, has been the subject of numerous publications. LD 50 for TMA chloride: 25 mg/kg (mouse, i.p.); 40 mg/kg (mouse, s.c.). LC 50 for TMA chloride: 462 mg/L for 96 hrs. (Fathead minnow, Pimephales promelas).
