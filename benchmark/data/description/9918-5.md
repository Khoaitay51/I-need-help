Common nomenclature [edit]. In the toxicological literature, naturally-occurring tetramethylammonium (anion unspecified) is often referred to by the name tetramine. Unfortunately, this non-systematic or trivial name is also used for other chemical entities, including a toxic rodenticide (Tetramethylenedisulfotetramine). LD 50 for TMA chloride: 25 mg/kg (mouse, i.p.); 40 mg/kg (mouse, s.c.). LC 50 for TMA chloride: 462 mg/L for 96 hrs. (Fathead minnow, Pimephales promelas).
