Now click on the printer that you have under the Printers and Faxes icon. b. Double-click on the icon of the printer you want to change to online. A pop-up window detailing all current print jobs will open. c. Go to Printer in the menu bar of the pop-up window and uncheck Use Printer Offline. This action will change the printer from offline to online.
