A typical swordfish meal would be a simple olive oil-based marinade, then a time on the grill, then served simply with lemon, salt and herbs. Good sword needs nothing more than this. Cook swordfish like you would a rare steak: Use high heat to sear the outside, and let it stay a little rare in the middle. I cook it about 5 minutes on one side, then 2-3 minutes on the other for an inch-thick steak.
