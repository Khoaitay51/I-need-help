The roots of the Gullah culture began in Sierra Leone, Gambia, Senegal, and Angola. Of the many influences that came from this region, music played a major role. African songs and the foundation from which they came are deeply rooted in what evolved into Negro spirituals, slave songs, and now celebrated as Gullah music. This intermingling produced a rich diversity of language, food, art, music and religion with its epicenter being Charleston, Beaufort and the surrounding Sea Islands of South Carolina.
