The main  tissue is cardiac muscle cells. These are striated, branched muscle  cells held together with intercalated … discs. Connective tissue  forms a sac around the heart, and epithelial tissue forms blood  vessels within the heart. Therefore, the heart contains all four  types of tissue. It is mostly muscle tissue. Connective tissue  forms a protective sac and holds the heart together, epithelial  tissue forms the blood vessels, and nervous tissue carries impulses  to and away from the heart.   this is all true!
