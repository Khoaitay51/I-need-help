Measure a length of hat tape that stretches temple to temple on your face. Gently place foam tape between hat band and crown in the front of your hat. This should take up at least one hat size for you. If you own a hat you love, but it is still too large, cut tape to go entirely around your head. When satisfied with the new size, remove the backing and stick to the reverse side of the hat band, not to the hat itself. Try hat on once more for fit, removing excess tape if necessary
