Hat Sizing. Is the first step in owning a hat that feels like it was made just for you. It is the Fit of a hat that makes for a superior wearing experience. Our Perfect Fit System is here to help you get a hat that’s not too small or too large, but is a perfect fit. Handmade artisan hats vary slightly from hat to hat. It’s the nature of anything handmade. Be sure not to pull the tape or string too tight. This is the most common error people make when figuring out their hat size. A good fitting hat feels snug and secure, not tight or pinching. Keep in mind, the Perfect Fit System allows you reduce the size of your hat if it’s a bit big.
