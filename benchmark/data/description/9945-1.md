The liver, which is the largest organ in the abdominal cavity, lies mainly in the upper right section of the abdominal cavity, just inferior to the diaphragm. Liver Structure The liver has two main lobes, the right lobe and the smaller left lobe, separated by a ligament. The stomach is a thick-walled, J-shaped organ that lies on the left side of the abdominal cavity deep to the liver and diaphragm. The stomach is continuous with the esophagus above and the duodenum of the small intestine below.
