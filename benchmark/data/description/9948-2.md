The term redox comes from two concepts involved with electron transfer: reduction and oxidation. It can be explained in simple terms: 1  Oxidation is the loss of electrons or an increase in oxidation state by a molecule, atom, or ion. 2  Reduction is the gain of electrons or a decrease in oxidation state by a molecule, atom, or ion. The reduction potential is a measure of the tendency of the oxidizing agent to be reduced. Its value is zero for H + + e − → ½ H 2 by definition, positive for oxidizing agents stronger than H + (e.g., +2.866 V for F 2) and negative for oxidizing agents that are weaker than H + (e.g., −0.763 V for Zn 2+).
