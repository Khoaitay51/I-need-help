1 Cystine stones: These are the rarest of kidney stones; cystine is one of the building blocks that make up muscles, nerves and other parts of the body. 2  Cystine can build up in the urine to form a stone. 3  The disease that causes cystine stones run in families. There are 4 major types of kidney stones: 1  Calcium stone: The most common type of stone contains calcium, and calcium is a normal part of a health diet. 2  Calcium that is not used by the bones and muscles goes to the kidneys. 3  In most people, the kidneys flush out the extra calcium with the rest of the urine.
