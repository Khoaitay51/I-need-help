When the keyboard is nice and clean replace the keys according to your notes or if you have just removed the letter keys use fig 1.4 as a guide. To put the keys back on, position the key in place and press gently but firmly until it clicks home. Use the cotton bud to clean the sides of the keys as seen in fig 1.1. After cleaning the sides of the keys take your lint free cloth and dampen it with your cleaning fluid, never put the liquid directly on the keyboard.
