For a quick cleaning, turn the keyboard upside down and use a can of compressed air to blow out any foreign matter. Be sure to do this in a location where falling debris can be cleaned up easily. Turn the keyboard upside down and tap on it a few times. You should see a bit of dirt fall out. 1 Doing so can cause liquid propellant to spray into the keyboard and damage it. 2  Also, be sure to use the compressed air in a well ventilated area. 3  If you remove the keys, keep them away from small children or pets. 4  Loose keys pose a choking hazard.
