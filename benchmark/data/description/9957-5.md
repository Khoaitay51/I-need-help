Basmati is an aromatic variety of rice. Whether it is brown or white depends on how it is processed. All rice, and all grains for that matter, are born with their grains intact. Intact grains are called brown when referring to rice, or whole when referring to other grains. There is a processing method called polishing that removes two components of the rice grain; the bran and the germ. This polished rice is called white rice
