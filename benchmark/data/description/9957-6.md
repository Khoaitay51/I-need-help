Brown basmati rice is full of different nutrients. It contains a mixture of vitamins and minerals that correspond to its rich flavor and nutty aroma. In general, brown rices are more full of nutrients than their white rice relatives, and brown basmati rice is one of the most nutrient-rich of them all. Brown Basmati Rice. Brown basmati rice is related to white basmati rice. These types of rice plants provide grains of rice, which are generally longer and thinner than regular brown or white rice. In comparison with other types of rice, brown basmati rice generally cooks more quickly. It is full of aroma and flavor, making it a popular choice for many dishes and sides
