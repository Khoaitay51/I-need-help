To understand more about the difference between brown basmati rice and white basmati rice, let's look at the difference between brown and white rice in general. An important first question to ask about all rice—and for that matter, most foods—is how much it has been processed. Just like all species of rice, basmati is available in white or brown versions, depending on the extent of the milling process. Like jasmine rice, it has its own unique smell.
