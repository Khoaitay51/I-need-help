Useful to help to improve your breathing and lung capacity, an incentive spirometer is a simple and inexpensive medical device you can use easily in the comfort of your own home. While it may look a little daunting to someone who has never seen or used one before, it is a simple piece of equipment to use. The more you use your incentive spirometer, the more your breathing ability improves. When using the device several times, you might find yourself becoming lightheaded or dizzy. In such case, simply remove the mouthpiece from your mouth and take a few normal breaths.
