Spirometry. Spirometry is the first and most commonly done lung function test. It measures how much and how quickly you can move air out of your lungs. For this test, you breathe into a mouthpiece attached to a recording device (spirometer). The more common lung function values measured with spirometry are: 1  Forced vital capacity (FVC) . 2  This measures the amount of air you can exhale with force after you inhale as deeply as possible. 3  Forced expiratory volume (FEV) . 4  This measures the amount of air you can exhale with force in one breath.
