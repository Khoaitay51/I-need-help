A grantor transfers property, which includes money and assets, to a trust for the benefit of the trust's beneficiaries. The trustee will distribute property to the beneficiaries in accordance with the terms of the trust, as written by the grantor. A trust can have one beneficiary or multiple beneficiaries. The grantor can appoint one of the beneficiaries to be the trustee. However, the grantor cannot be the trustee of an irrevocable trust.
