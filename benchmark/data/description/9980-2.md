Types of satin [edit]. 1  Baronet or baronette has a cotton back and a rayon or silk front, similar to georgette. 2  Charmeuse is a lightweight, draping satin-weave fabric with a dull reverse. 3  Double face(d) satin is woven with a glossy surface on both sides. 4  Duchess(e) satin is a particularly luxurious, heavy, stiff satin. 5  Faconne is jacquard woven ... If a fabric is formed with a satin weave using filament fibres such as silk, nylon, or polyester, the corresponding fabric is termed a satin, although some definitions insist that the fabric be made from silk. If the yarns used are short-staple yarns such as cotton, the fabric formed is considered a sateen.
