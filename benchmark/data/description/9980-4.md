Product Range. Semi Dull: Semi dull polyester textured yarns are manufactured using partially oriented yarn (POY) having lower luster when compared to POY used for bright yarns. These yarns are known for their strength and durability. They are extensively used for weaving and embroidery work in garments and apparels industry. Our range of semi dull polyester textured yarns is available in various deniers and filaments. Full Dull: Full dull polyester textured yarn has the qualities of high tenacity, strength and looks like cotton fiber. The raw material for these yarns has been chemically and/or physically modified to reduce their normal luster. Our range of yarn has features of good pendent, soft visual appeal and a strong covering
