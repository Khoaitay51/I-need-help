The Military Reconstruction Act of 1867 was a post-Civil War attempt by the North's Radical Republicans to force the Southern States to be governed the same as the Northern states. The previously Confederate states were divided into five districts and placed under Union military control. The Military Reconstruction Act is important to history for many reasons. This act was a way that the Radical Republicans in Congress could punish the southern states that had previously made up the Confederacy. It was also a way that they could ensure rights for the free blacks in the South.
