There are many differences between the due process model and the crime control model: In the due process model people that are arrested are perceived to be innocent until proven in a court of law. The crime control model believes that the people that are arrested are guilty and punishment by the government is needed. Due process model believes that policing within the criminal justice system is essential to maintaining justice within society. Crime control model believes that the arresting of people in the criminal justice system has a negative effect and slows down the process of the criminal justice system. Due Process vs. Crime Control Model Two crime control models: the due process model and the crime control model have been debated for a long time.
