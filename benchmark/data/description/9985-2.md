THE DROSTDY MUSE NEWS Volume 3, Number 1 10 January 2007 Editorial We wish all our friends and associates a healthy, prosperous and peaceful 2007. Amidst all the hustle and bustle, we need to take time out to reflect on our objectives. Let's make it a good one! Opening of Drostdy Museum Slave Exhibition On Reconciliation Day, 16 th December 2006, the Drostdy held a special function to mark the opening of its new slave exhibition in one of the old barns above the residence. Much research both in the archives in Cape Town and here in Swellendam preceded this event
