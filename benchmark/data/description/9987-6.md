Block or allow pop-ups. Once you've downloaded Google Toolbar on Internet Explorer, you can block or allow pop-ups: 1  On the right side of the Toolbar, click the wrench. 2  Go to the Tools tab. 3  To block pop-ups, check the box next to Pop-up blocker.. 4  To allow pop-ups, uncheck the box next to Pop-up blocker.. 5  Click Save.
