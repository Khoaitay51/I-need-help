Proton. Along with neutrons, protons make up the nucleus, held together by the strong force. The proton is a baryon and is considered to be composed of two up quarks and one down quark. It has long been considered to be a stable particle, but recent developments of grand unification models have suggested that it might decay with a half-life of about 10 32 years. When we say that a proton is made up of two up quarks and a down, we mean that its net appearance or net set of quantum numbers match that picture.
