Michigan residents who wish to become certified nursing assistants are required to complete a CNA training program that is accredited and approved by the Michigan Department of Community Health. Aspiring CNAs must also pass the Michigan state nurse aide examination. Step 1: Find an approved program in your state: Find a CNA Training Program. To earn your CNA certification in Michigan, you will need to complete a minimum 75-hour nurse aide training program that is approved by the state.
