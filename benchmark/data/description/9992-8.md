How to Become a CNA. All you need to do to start studying to become a CNA is a high school diploma or GED and a CNA certification. The CNA training certificate program takes about 6-12 weeks to complete. CNA training and certification programs can be found in community colleges and medical facilities. Usually, you can call them to ask about admission procedures. The Work of a CNA. The work of CNAs is important. They not only monitor their conditions, but they provide emotional support as well. The elderly and sick need nurses that care about their well-being overall. Since CNAs are assistants to nurses, they help with medical equipment.
