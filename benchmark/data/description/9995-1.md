Possible health benefits of consuming cilantro. Learning how to use fresh herbs and spices like cilantro when cooking can help to cut down on sodium intake. Consuming plant-based foods of all kinds has long been associated with a reduced risk of many lifestyle-related health conditions. Many studies have suggested that increasing consumption of plant foods like cilantro decreases the risk of obesity, overall mortality, diabetes and heart disease while promoting a healthy skin and hair, increased energy and overall lower weight.
