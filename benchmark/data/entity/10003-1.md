The fossilized coral lived approximately 350 million years ago during the Devonian Age, in the sea which used to cover Michigan. More specifically the Petoskey Stone is a rock and a fossil, often pebble-shaped, that is composed of a fossilized rugose coral, Hexagonaria percarinata. The Petoskey Stone Story. The Petoskey Stone was adopted as the Official State Stone in 1965. A Petoskey Stone is a Tetracoral (Colony Coral): In that many animals lived together in a colony. “Hexagonaria” is it’s scientific name. In each hexagon chamber are the prehistoric remains of salt water marine life
