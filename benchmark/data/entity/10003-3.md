What is a Petoskey Stone? The “Petoskey Stone,” which became the official state stone of Michigan in 1965, is actually a petrified fossil coral of the genus hexagonaria. Approximately 350 million years ago, a warm, salt water sea covered Michigan. In the depths of this sea, the members of the colony coral lived. This silt is seen as dark streaks on the Petoskey Stone.In a time before Native American Indians moved their teepees across the hills and dunes of Northern Michigan, before even the dinosaurs roamed the Earth, a warm, salt sea covered what is now Lake Michigan.
