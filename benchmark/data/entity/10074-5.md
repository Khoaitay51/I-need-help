The corneal epithelium (epithelium corneae corneæ anterior) layer is made up of epithelial tissue and covers the front of the. Cornea it acts as a barrier to protect the, cornea resisting the free flow of fluids from the, tears and prevents bacteria from entering the epithelium and corneal. stroma The corneal epithelium consists of several layers of cells. The cells of the deepest layer are columnar, known as basal cells. Then follow two or three layers of polyhedral cells, commonly known as wing cells. The majority of these are prickle cells, similar to those found in the stratum mucosum of the cuticle.
