Overview / Basic structure of the eyeball. The eyeball consists of three principal layers. 1  The outer layer, or sclera, consists of dense fibrous connective tissue. 2  The sclera is white of the eye. 3  The sclera is continuous with the transparent substantia propria of the cornea. 1 The exposed front surface of the eye, including the cornea, is also covered by a thin, non-keratinized stratified squamous epithelium. 2  The next layer, or choroid, consists of heavily pigmented loose connective tissue, including many melanocytes. 3  The choroid is normally not visible behind the white of the sclera.
