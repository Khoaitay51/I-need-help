It is an example of an improper ferroelectric material in which the phase transition from the paraelectric phase is controlled by the softening of a zone boundary mode, which would lead to a nonpolar low-temperature phase, which is in fact ferroelastic. Virtually all ferroelectric materials need to be electrically poled before use. In the case of LiTaO 3, silver electrodes are applied to faces perpendicular to the polar (c) axis and a pulsed field of about 10 V/cm (1 ms with a 50/50 duty cycle) applied while the crystal is cooled slowly through T C (620 °C).
