THE BARITE GROUP OF MINERALS. The barite group is composed of minerals with a similar orthorhombic structure and related chemistry. It is named for its most common member, barite. The general formula for this group is A(SO 4). The A can be either barium, lead or strontium. The structures are of these minerals are rather similar and therefore they form similar crystal habits. The following are the more common members of the Barite Group of minerals: 1  Anglesite (Lead Sulfate). 2  Barite (Barium Sulfate). 3  Celestite or celestine (Strontium Sulfate).
