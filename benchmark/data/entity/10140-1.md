Alaska Range. The Alaska Range is perhaps the most well-known mountain range in Alaska. It is home to Denali National Park and the giant mountain itself, Denali (you may know it better by its white-man name, Mt. McKinley). In fact, Denali is one of the most massive mountains in the world. Highest Peak: Denali, 20,320 feet (6,194 meters). Notable Mountains: 1  Denali, tallest mountain on North American continent; measured base-to-peak, one of the tallest
