Photographing Alaska's mountains is a never ending endeavor. There are massive ranges that crisscross the state, offering landscape photographic opportunities in an endless fashion: The Brooks Range, the Alaska Range, The Kenai Mountians, The Chugach Mountains, The Wrangell Mountains and many more. North America's tallest peak (Mount McKinley) is located in the Alaska Range, and many of North America's tallest peaks are in the Wrangell St. Elias range.
