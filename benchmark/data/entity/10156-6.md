If there is too much glucose in the blood (more than the body needs), it is mainly stored in the following organs:   * the liver  * skeletal muscles (all the muscles that are attached to the bones)   A small amount is also stored in the kidneys. The stored form of glucose is called glycogen. The body stores carbohydrates in two ways; as glycogen in the liver, and as fat after converting the carbohydrates into fat.
