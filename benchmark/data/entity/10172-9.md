1 High folic acid levels can also mean a vitamin B12 deficiency. 2  Body cells need vitamin B12 to use folic acid. 3  So if vitamin B12 levels are very low, folic acid can't be used by the cells, and high levels of it may build up in the blood. 4  But a folic acid test is not a reliable way to test for a vitamin B12 deficiency. Folic acid is found in foods such as liver; citrus fruits; dark green, leafy vegetables (spinach); whole grains; cereals with added B vitamins; beans; milk; kidney; and yeast. Folic acid can also be measured as the amount in the red blood cells.
