Vikramaditya (IAST: Vikramāditya) was a legendary 1st-century BCE emperor of Ujjain, India, famed for his wisdom, valour and magnanimity. According to the Bhavishya Purana, he was the second son of Ujjain's King Gandharvasena of the Paramara dynasty. This is the most popular and celebrated of the Vikramaditya cycles, existing in four Sanskrit recensions, numerous Indian vernacular versions, several English translations (both from Sanskrit and Hindi), and more.
