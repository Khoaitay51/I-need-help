Vikramaditya is the subject of several collections of legends and stories in both Sanskrit and regional languages in India. The two most famous are Vetala Panchvimshati (Twenty-five [Tales] of the Vetala ) and Simhasana-Dwatrimshika (Thirty-two [Tales] of the Throne). This is the most popular and celebrated of the Vikramaditya cycles, existing in four Sanskrit recensions, numerous Indian vernacular versions, several English translations (both from Sanskrit and Hindi), and more.
