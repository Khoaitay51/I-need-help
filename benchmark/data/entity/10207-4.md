All types of Qualicaps ® capsules are gluten-free and can be taken safely by people on special diets. Gluten is a protein found in wheat, rye, barley and oat products, none of which are used in the manufacture of two-piece capsules. Hypromellose is the official Pharmacopeia name for hydroxypropyl methylcellulose (HPMC). It is a cellulose derivative made from plants and is used to make capsules without using materials of animal origin. Qualicaps®’ Quali-V® capsules are made using hypromellose.
