These 10 facts about space will blow your mind. Hydroxypropyl methyl cellulose (HPMC) is a polymer used for multiple applications in fields such as construction and ophthalmology. It is also known as hypromellose, which is the international, non-proprietary name (INN) for this substance. One of the most important properties of hydroxypropyl methyl cellulose is that, in a water-based solution, it does not have a gelation property when heated. Instead of precipitating out as methylcellulose does, it causes the entire solution to congeal into a semi-flexible mass that can no longer flow.
