Carbohydrates include sugar, starch and fiber. When counting carbohydrates, consider your total carbohydrate intake -- include the amount of fiber and starch as well as the sugar in a food item. A medium banana, for instance, contains 14.4 g of sugar, 6.35 g of starch and 3.1 g of fiber. Intake Recommendations. If you have prediabetes, carbohydrates should comprise about 50 percent to 60 percent of your total daily caloric intake. Carbohydrates contain 4 calories per gram. Based on a 2,000 calorie diet, this means you should consume approximately 1,000 to 1,200 calories from carbohydrates.
