Cytomel (liothyronine sodium) Tablets contain liothyronine (L-triiodothyronine or LT 3), a synthetic form of a natural thyroid hormone, and is available as the sodium salt. Cytomel (liothyronine sodium) Tablets may be used in preference to levothyroxine (T 4) during radioisotope scanning procedures, since induction of hypothyroidism in those cases is more abrupt and can be of shorter duration. It may also be preferred when impairment of peripheral conversion of T 4 to T 3 is suspected.
