Symptoms of osteoarthritis. The main symptoms of osteoarthritis are pain and stiffness in your joints, which can make it difficult to move the affected joints and do certain activities. Other symptoms you or your doctor may notice include: 1  joint tenderness. 2  increased pain and stiffness when you have not moved your joints for a while. 3  joints appearing slightly larger or more 'knobbly' than usual. 4  a grating or crackling sound or sensation in your joints.
