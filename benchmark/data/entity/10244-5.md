Symptoms of osteoarthritis can range from mild to severe. They may include: 1  Pain. 2  Your joints may ache, or the pain may feel burning or sharp. 3  For some people, the pain may come and go. 4  Constant pain or pain while you sleep may be a sign that your arthritis is getting worse. 5  Stiffness. 6  When you have arthritis, getting up in the morning can be hard
