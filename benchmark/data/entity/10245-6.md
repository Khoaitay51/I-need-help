Plant Taxonomy of Columbine Flowers: Plant taxonomy classifies columbine plants, in general, as Aquilegia. For instance, Aquilegia canadensis is the red columbine. Plant Type: Columbine plants are herbaceous flowering perennials. Facts About Columbine Plants: Columbines come in many colors; some are even bi-colored. These perennials can have red, yellow, white, blue, pink or purple blossoms
