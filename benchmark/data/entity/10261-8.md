A single-malt Lagavulin may contain whiskies from many barrels produced at the Lagavulin distillery, but it will contain only whiskies produced at Lagavulin. A single-grain scotch whisky varies only in that it contains barley and one or more other cereal grains, usually wheat or corn. Formally, however, a blended whisky is a product that contains a mix of barrel-aged malt and grain whiskies. There's a common misconception that because a certain whisky is labeled single malt, it must be the product of just a single batch or barrel of whisky.
