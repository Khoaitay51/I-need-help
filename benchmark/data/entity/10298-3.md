Ficus' tropical origins make it a particularly welcome companion during winter weather. While you can get this effect indoors by growing ficus as a houseplant, some small tropical trees tolerate the cold well enough for outdoor planting. Several tropical thrive with moderate care in USDA hardiness zones 5 to 8. This restricts permanent outdoor planting of ficus to USDA plant hardiness zone 10b or warmer. In zone 10a, average annual cold temperatures reach between 30 and 35 degrees Fahrenheit; in zone 10b, lows are between 35 and 40 F, warm enough to minimize chances of exposure to even occasional temperature lows.
