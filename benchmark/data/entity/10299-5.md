The DNA you inherited from your parents is a mixture of the DNA they inherited from each of their parents. The mixing occurs randomly. But long blocks of DNA tend to stick together, often for several generations. This new type of autosomal DNA test checks about 700,000 pairs of locations in your autosomal DNA. These locations are called single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs). The lab then compares your autosomal DNA with that of other customers and looks for identical segments with many common SNPs.
