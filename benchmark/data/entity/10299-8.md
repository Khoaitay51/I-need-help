Your autosomal DNA, therefore, contains random bits of DNA from your great-grandparents, great-great grandparents, and so on. Close relatives will share large fragments of DNA from a common ancestor. Connections arising from more distant relatives will result in smaller fragments of shared DNA. Therefore, your autosomes essentially contain a complete genetic record, with all branches of your ancestry at some point contributing a piece of your autosomal DNA. Used For: Autosomal DNA tests can be used to search for relative connections along any branch of your family tree.
