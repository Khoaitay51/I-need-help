Twenty-two of these matched pairs of chromosomes are called autosomes, while the 23rd pair determines your sex (male or female). Autosomal DNA is inherited from both parents, and includes random contributions from their parents, grandparents, and so on. Therefore, your autosomes essentially contain a complete genetic record, with all branches of your ancestry at some point contributing a piece of your autosomal DNA. Used For: Autosomal DNA tests can be used to search for relative connections along any branch of your family tree.
