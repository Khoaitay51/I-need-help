Raccoons are typically from 2 to 3 feet long, 9 to 10 inches tall, and weigh between 10 and 30 pounds. Males are typically bigger than females, and individuals living in the north are typically larger than those who live in the south. The back feet of a raccoon are larger than the front feet. In the wild, however, a life span of 2 to 3 years is more typical. Diseases, accidents, and the impacts of large predators (like great horned owls, hawks, eagles, bobcats, wolves, cougars etc) and human hunting and trapping are typical causes of death in a raccoon population.
