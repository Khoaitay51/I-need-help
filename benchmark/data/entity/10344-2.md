If sucrose or lactose is fermented, the reaction will be A/A (acid slant over acid butt). Because of the high concentration of these sugars, enough acid is produced by fermentation in the butt to lower the pH of both the butt and the slant, turning both yellow. Principle: TSI contains three sugars – glucose, sucrose, and lactose. Phenol Red is the indicator that turns yellow at an acid pH resulting from fermentation. The glucose concentration is 0.1% and the lactose and sucrose concentrations are 1%.
