MTHFR (methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase). MTHFR is an abbreviation for a gene that codes for an enzyme called methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase. Mutations in the MTHFR gene are associated with thromboembolic and cardiovascular disease, which can lead to blood clots, stroke, embolism, and/or heart attacks. Two mutations of MTHFR are the most common. The MTHFR enzyme helps the body to manufacture proteins.
