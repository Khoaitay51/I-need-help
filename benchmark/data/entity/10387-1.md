Overview. Noxzema is a skin cleanser designed to remove dirt, oil and makeup from facial skin. It has a distinct fragrance and a characteristic tingle. The original deep cleansing cream was developed in 1914, as a sunburn remedy by a doctor named Francis Townsend in Maryland. Glycine Soja Oil. Glycine soja oil is also known as glycine soybean oil. Soybean oil is extracted from soybeans and consists mainly of the triglycerides of oleic, linoleic, linolenic and other saturated acids.
