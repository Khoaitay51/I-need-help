Almost all Noxzema products contain camphor, eucalyptus oil, and menthol. These ingredients are practically a trademark for the company. You open the jar or squeeze the tube and you instantly know it’s Noxzema. The problem with these ingredients is that they irritate the skin. Camphor is an aromatic substance made from the bark of a tree that is closely related to cinnamon. Applied to the skin, it dilates blood vessels and produces a cooling effect.
