Different and popular types of bedding: All those who are into small farming or backyard chicken keeping, adore pine shavings, this makes it a popular type of chicken coop bedding. The most attractive feature of these pine shavings is that they have the ability to absorb the entire wetness and smell as well. In order to keep your birds healthy, always use the food-grade DE. Cedar shavings also act perfectly to fill the needs of the chicken bedding. They carry very pleasant aroma, but it is said that they can even affect the birds. For chicken coop floor covering ideas, bamboo sticks can even be used.
