Wood shavings, chips and bark, chopped straw and shredded paper all make effective chicken bedding and I will consider these in turn. Wood shavings. Wood shavings (not sawdust) are one of the most popular bedding materials for chickens, as they are very absorbent and easy to use. However unless you are lucky enough to know someone with a sawmill, these are becoming more and more expensive as the building industry struggles. Whatever type of bedding you choose always keep it as clean and as dry as possible as the happier and more comfortable your chickens are the more eggs they will give you.
