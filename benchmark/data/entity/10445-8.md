Vitamin C is one vitamin that helps to retain your skin's elasticity. This vitamin, also called ascorbic acid, helps to boost the production of a protein called collagen within your skin. Collagen helps to maintain your skin's strength, and provides a supportive framework around which your skin cells can grow. The vitamin A that you consume as part of your diet interacts with your skin cells and helps to regulate the production of natural oils within your skin. These oils are important to your skin's elasticity, since moisturized skin retains its elasticity better than dry skin.
