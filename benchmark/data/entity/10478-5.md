Hawaii's Hurricane History. The last time a category 4 hurricane hit Hawaii was 15 years ago when Hurricane Iniki swept over the islands. The eye of the hurricane passed over Kauai on September 11, 1992. It caused over $1 billion in damage and six deaths.
