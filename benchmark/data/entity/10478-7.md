CBS News correspondent Bigad Shaban reports from Hilo, Hawaii, the worst of Tropical Storm Iselle's damage was in Pahoa, a small town on the Big Island's east coast. Wind gusts of up to 85 mph brought down power lines and uprooted massive albizia trees, tossing some onto homes. While it lacked power, Iselle was the first tropical storm to hit Hawaii in 22 years. Hurricanes or tropical storms had directly hit Hawaii only three times since 1950, and the last time was in 1992, when Hurricane Iniki killed six people and destroyed more than 1,400 homes in Kauai.
