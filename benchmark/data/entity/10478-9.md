HURRICANES In Hawaii. HURRICANES form in areas of enhanced thunderstorms over warm, tropical oceans and are the most destructive storms on Earth (Figure 1). The destructive fury of hurricanes comes from a combination of high winds, heavy rains, and abnormally high waves and storm tides. Hurricane Dot (1959) caused damage to Kauai. Hurricane Estelle (1986) produced very high surf on Hawaii and Maui and floods on Oahu. Kauai also received the brunt of Hurricane Iwa, which struck on 23 November 1982 and produced an estimated $234 million in damage.
