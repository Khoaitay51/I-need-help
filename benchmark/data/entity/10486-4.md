Regardless of the ads, I wondered if teenagers need a vitamin of their own and, if so, whether boys' and girls' needs differ. To begin with, there's broad debate over whether healthy, nonpregnant adults without any specific deficiencies benefit from any kind of multivitamin or other supplementation. But there is really no need for a vitamin aimed specifically at teens, says David Schardt, senior nutritionist at the Center for Science in the Public Interest. Most of our nutrient needs are pretty similar, he says, varying chiefly by weight and body size.
