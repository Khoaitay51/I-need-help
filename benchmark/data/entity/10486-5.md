Grains and Fats. Teenagers need a variety of foods from the grains and healthy fats, or oils, groups daily. Teens eating 2,200 calories daily need 7 ounces from the grains group and 6 teaspoons from the oils group, while teens consuming 2,800 calories daily should aim for 10 ounces of grains and 8 teaspoons of oils. The Institute of Medicine notes that teen boys ages 14 to 18 need 52 grams of protein daily, while teen girls generally require 46 grams of protein per day. Active teens and teen athletes often need additional protein. Healthy, protein-rich options include poultry, lean meat, fish, eggs, soy products, low-fat dairy foods, legumes, seeds, nuts and seitan
