By the end of adolescence, teens attain most of their adult height and weight. Although adolescence spans a period of five to seven years, teens do most of their growing during an 18-24 month period called the growth spurt.. To support this rapid growth, teenagers need to consume lots of calories and other nutrients. Adolescence is a period of rapid physical, emotional, intellectual, and social maturation. To support this growth, teenagers need extra calories, calcium, and iron, and sufficient protein. Teenage eating habits are influenced less by parents and family, and more by peers, media messages, and body image issue
