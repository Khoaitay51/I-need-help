Although adolescence spans a period of five to seven years, teens do most of their growing during an 18-24 month period called the growth spurt.. To support this rapid growth, teenagers need to consume lots of calories and other nutrients.     Calories   Teenagers need lots of calories to support rapid growth. Girls need approximately 2200 calories, while boys need 2500-2900 calories.     Protein   Protein is also necessary for physical growth, and should account for 15-20% of total calories.
