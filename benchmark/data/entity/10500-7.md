The eye infections are caused by various reasons such as viral or bacterial attack and the effects can range from simple discomfort to severe pain and swelling that may have serious consequences unless treated urgently by qualified eye specialists. Blepharitis eye infections are of two basic types – Anterior Blapharitis and Posterior Blapharitis depending on which portion of the eye the infection actually affects. The primary cause for this kind of eye infections is bacterial action and presence of dandruff on the eyebrows.
