Every table in the summer would include a communal plate of Greek salad. You meals most likely include a plate too (horiatiki salata = village salad, is best). Since the country is a major exporter of fruits and vegetables chances are good that your salad would be made with fresh, and very tasty ingredients. 1 The water in Greece is safe to drink out of the tap. 2  In our own visits to Greece we often drink the water out of the tap, but we just as often buy bottled water which comes in many sizes and is very inexpensive.
