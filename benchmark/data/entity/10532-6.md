Bulb ornamental plants are of the Liliaceae and Amaryllidaceae families and are cultivated in ornamental gardening: snowdrops, squills, tulips, narcissus, hyacinths, and lilies. Hyacinths, tulips, narcissus, and some other bulb types successfully multiply by forced planting, that is, in hothouses and rooms. Ornamental trees and shrubs include leafy and conifer types, evergreens, and deciduous plants. They are used in parks and squares and to line boulevards and streets. In separate plantings, varieties with spreading crowns (oak, ash, or plane trees) or umbellate crowns (silk trees) are used.
