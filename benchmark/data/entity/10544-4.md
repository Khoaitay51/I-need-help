Uniqua is highly energetic. She is often the character to come up with new ideas for adventures, and if she's not, Uniqua will become a large part of the journey. Compared to Tasha, she displays a more tomboy-like personality. Appearances. Uniqua is the only character to appear in every episode of The Backyardigans. Her first appearance was in Me and My Friends, the unaired live-action pilot. Her first appearance in an aired episode was in Pirate Treasure.
