Answer   Organisms all possess DNA as their genetic material. What differentiates them (and their DNA) is the sequence of base-pairs within the DNA. The base-pairs … are actually specific sequences of nucleotides (i.e. adenine, thymine, guanine and cytosine, labelled A, T, G, and C respectively) which encode genes. DNA = Deoxyribonucleic acid Deoxyribose is a sugar that differs from another sugar called ribose (ribo) by missing one oxygen atom (deoxy).
