Stems have four main functions which are: 1  Support for and the elevation of leaves, flowers and fruits. 2  The stems keep the leaves in the light and provide a place for the plant to keep its flowers and fruits. A stem is one of two main structural axes of a vascular plant, the other being the root. The stem is normally divided into nodes and internodes: 1  The nodes hold one or more leaves, as well as buds which can grow into branches (with leaves, conifer cones, or inflorescences (flowers)).
