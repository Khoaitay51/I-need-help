1 Pharmacogenomics: type of genetic testing that determines the influence of genetic variation on drug response. 2  When a person has a disease or health condition, pharmacogenomics can examine an individual’s genetic makeup to determine what medicine and what dosage would be the safest and most beneficial to the patient. 1 If both parents are tested, the test can provide information about a couple's risk of having a child with a genetic condition like cystic fibrosis. 2  Preimplantation genetic diagnosis: Genetic testing procedures that are performed on human embryos prior to the implantation as part of an in vitro fertilization procedure.
