Worms are in a very broad category that is not officially classified in any scientific sense. While most worms are limbless invertebrates, some worms are actually reptiles or amphibians. Many other animals called worms are simply insect larvae that have yet to metamorphose into their adult stage. 1 Nematoda – This phylum of worms includes roundworms, hookworms and threadworms. 2  Many roundworms are parasites and can cause a variety of problems for people and pets. 3  Threadworms live on the ground in damp soil, mossy areas or in decaying vegetation.
