The Nutrition of Oatmeal. Oatmeal has long been one of the widely used breakfast cereals. It is an excellent low calorie meal. One cup provides about 150 calories. It is a highly regarded cereal for its rich mineral content. Oats contain high levels of calcium, phosphorous, potassium and magnesium. They also contain iron, manganese and zinc as well as other trace minerals. The cereal is also very rich in dietary fiber. Oatmeal has various health benefits.
