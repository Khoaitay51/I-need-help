The body can produce glucose from protein and fat, however carbohydrates are the most convenient and quickest way for the body to produce energy. Some weight-loss diets exclude or reduce carbohydrate intake triggering the body to convert its supply of fat to glucose. In Summary. Carbohydrates contain the glucose that the body needs for energy. There are two main types of carbohydrates, simple and complex. The more refined the carbohydrate the more quickly it is converted to glucose and released into the bloodstream.
