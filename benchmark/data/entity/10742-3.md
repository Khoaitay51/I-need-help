Smooth muscle is also found in lymphatic vessels, the urinary bladder, uterus (termed uterine smooth muscle), male and female reproductive tracts, gastrointestinal tract, respiratory tract, arrector pili of skin, the ciliary muscle, and iris of the eye. 1 Also, smooth muscle may contain MHC that is not involved in contraction, and that can arise from multiple genes. 2  Myosin II also contains 4 light chains, resulting in 2 per head, weighing 20 (MLC 20) and 17 (MLC 17) kDa. 3  These bind the heavy chains in the neck region between the head and tail.
