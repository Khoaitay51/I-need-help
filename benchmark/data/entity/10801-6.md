The origins of the Manipuri alphabet, or Meetei Mayek as it is know in Manipuri, are shrouded in mystery as many historical documents were destroyed at the beginning of the 18th century during the reign of King Pamheiba. Between 1709 and the middle of the 20th century, the Manipuri language was written with the Bengali alphabet.
