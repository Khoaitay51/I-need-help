Used to write: Manipuri, or Meeteilon/Meitei, one of the official languages of the Indian state of Manipur in north-east India and has about 1.6 million speakers. It is a member of the Tibeto-Burman branch of the Sino-Tibetan language family and is also spoken in Bangladesh and Myanmar. Between 1709 and the middle of the 20th century, the Manipuri language was written with the Bengali alphabet.
