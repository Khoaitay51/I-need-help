Making slaked lime. Calcium oxide reacts with water to form calcium hydroxide, also called slaked lime. Here are the equations for this reaction: calcium oxide + water → calcium hydroxide. CaO + H 2 O → Ca(OH) 2. A lot of heat is produced in the reaction, which may even cause the water to boil. If limestone is heated strongly, it breaks down to form calcium oxide and carbon dioxide. Calcium oxide is also called quicklime. It is yellow when hot, but white when cold.
