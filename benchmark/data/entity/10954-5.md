The books features the three Grace children: Jared, Simon and Mallory, who finds a mysterious Field Guide hidden in the attic, written by their great-great-uncle Arthur Spiderwick, who studied Faeries — magical beings who hide themselves from Humans using a type of magic called glamour. The Spiderwick Chronicles are a fantasy book series of five books by Holly Black and Tony DiTerlizzi, published at Simon & Schuster Books for Young Readers.
