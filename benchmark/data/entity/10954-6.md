36,233 pages on this wiki. Mulgarath is a gigantic, evil ogre who appears in the Spiderwick Chronicles as the primary antagonist. His goal through out the series was to steal Arthur Spiderwick's Field Guide to gain control over his power. He was voiced and played by Nick Nolte who also played David Banner. Mulgarath is the main antagonist of the series despite only appearing in the fourth and fifth book. He is first mentioned in the fourth when the dwarf Lord Korting states that Mulgarath the Ogre is gathering an army of Goblins.
