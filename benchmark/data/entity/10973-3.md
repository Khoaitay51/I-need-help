Muscle Inflammation. Muscles play the most vital role in our movement. However, muscle inflammation, soreness, and pain can occur due to overstraining of the muscles and this in turn can impede our movements. Muscles work by contraction and relaxation. Overuse of these muscles can lead to muscle injury and swelling. For more information about muscle inflammation, causes, symptoms, and treatment, browse through the following lines. Causes, Symptoms And Treatment Of Inflammation Of The Muscle. Causes The major cause for temporary muscle inflammation is overuse of the muscles or continuous muscle use without proper resting.
