Inflammation of this. Skeletal muscles are prone to wear and tear and undergo physiological and pathological changes like other organs of the body. Sometimes these muscles become inflamed which is the typical protective response of muscles to injury. Inflammation of this muscle tissue is known as myositis. This is quite different from other muscle ailments like myopathy (disease of the muscles), rhabdomyolysis (degenerative disease of the muscles) and myalgia (muscle pain).
