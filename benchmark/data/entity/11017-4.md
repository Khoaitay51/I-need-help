Bell pepper. Bell pepper, also known as sweet pepper or a pepper (in the United Kingdom, Canada and Ireland) and capsicum /ˈkaepsɨkəm/ (ˈkæpsɨkəm In, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Australia singapore And New), zealand is a cultivar group of the Species capsicum. annuum While the bell pepper is a member of the Capsicum genus, it is the only Capsicum that does not produce capsaicin, a lipophilic chemical that can cause a strong burning sensation when it comes in contact with mucous membranes.
