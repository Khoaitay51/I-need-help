Shower material. Veritek IS Swanstone. It is a solid surface product and you can order them through most Lowes or Home Depots. Shower bases are not subject to the abuse that kitchen sinks are (boiling water, cleaning solutions etc.) and we have installed them for many years with almost no problems. We are putting in a prefab shower into our half bath. I've seen models from Sterling, Swanstone, and American Standard. Is one better than the other? Also, I've seen the material used described as Vikell, Veritek and Acrylic-capped ABS with fiberglass.
