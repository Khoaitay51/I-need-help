Swanstone Veritek is a compression-molded material manufactured exclusively by the Swan Corporation of St. Louis. The non-porous material is mold and mildew resistant, has no surface coating and with molded color throughout, is impervious to chipping and cracking. Swan Corporation provides a five-year limited warranty on bathtubs, bath and shower walls, and shower doors. A one-year limited warranty applies to utility sinks and basins manufactured of Veritek.
