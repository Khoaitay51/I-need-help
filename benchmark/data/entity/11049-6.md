Because of its mold and mildew resistance, Veritek is used in applications where water is present. Swanstone products manufactured with Veritek are bathtubs, bath and shower wall combination sets, shower doors, utility sinks and basins. Swan Corporation provides a five-year limited warranty on bathtubs, bath and shower walls, and shower doors. A one-year limited warranty applies to utility sinks and basins manufactured of Veritek.
