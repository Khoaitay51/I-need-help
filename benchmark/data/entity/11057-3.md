Diabetes insipidus is a condition that results from insufficient production of the antidiuretic hormone (ADH) , a hormone that helps the kidneys and body conserve the correct amount of water. Normally, the antidiuretic hormone controls the kidneys' output of urine. ADH is secreted to decrease the amount of urine output so that dehydration does not occur.
