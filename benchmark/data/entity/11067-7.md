Finding the right woodwind instrument is easy at Musician's Friend. We carry the entire family of saxophones, flutes, piccolos, clarinets and double reed instruments. Players can easily move between most woodwind instruments because the key/fingering systems are very similar, making woodwinds a versatile choice. Brass instruments are divided into two categories: valved brass–including trumpet, French horn, euphonium and tuba; and slide brass–like the trombone.
