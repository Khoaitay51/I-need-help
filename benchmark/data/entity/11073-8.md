Celiac disease, an autoimmune disorder, is one type of food intolerance. It causes digestive problems when you eat gluten, a protein in wheat, barley, and rye. People with celiac disease must follow a gluten-free diet to minimize symptoms and damage to the small intestine. Possible Serious Conditions. A gastroenterologist is a type of doctor that specializes in diagnosing and treating diseases involving the digestive system. If you continue to experience digestion problems, it’s time to make an appointment. More serious signs could mean an emergency medical problem.
