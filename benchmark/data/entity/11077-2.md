Kilik is a playable character in the Soulcalibur series of fighting games, and has appeared in every Soulcalibur video game, with the exception of Soulcalibur Legends. He was confirmed to return as a playable character in Soulcalibur 5 during the live event at IGN on Monday January 23, 2012
