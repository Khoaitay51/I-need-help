— Kilik. Kilik (キリク, Kiriku) is one of the main protagonists of Namco 's Soul series of fighting games. First appearing in Soulcalibur, he has returned as a starting character for all subsequent games in the series, with the exception of Soulcalibur Legends and Soulcalibur: Lost Swords. Soulcalibur IV Edit. 1  In the second stage of his Story Mode, Kilik fights Xianglian, who is actually an illusion created by Zasalamel. 2  In the fourth stage after he defeats Tira, he fights Maxi, who has become a host for Soul Edge.
