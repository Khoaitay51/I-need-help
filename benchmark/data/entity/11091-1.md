There are several different types of Ebola virus that effect humans. Currently they are Zaire, Sudan, and Ivory Coast Ebola virus, named for the respective regions in Africa in which the strain was found. Reston Ebola virus is a type of Ebola that only infects non-human primates. The virus causes Ebola hemorrhagic fever, which is a serious and usually fatal disease for those who contract it. It damages the endothelial cells that make up the lining of the blood vessels and creates difficulty in coagulation of the infected individual’s blood.
