1 The hormones of the pituitary gland help regulate the functions of other endocrine glands. 2  The pituitary gland has two parts—the anterior lobe and posterior lobe—that have two very separate functions. 3  The hypothalamus sends signals to the pituitary to release or inhibit pituitary hormone production. 1 Adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH): ACTH stimulates the adrenal glands to produce hormones. 2  Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH): FSH works with LH to ensure normal functioning of the ovaries and testes.
