Hummingbird moths are good hummingbird mimics. Dwight Sipler. Hummingbirds can be exciting to see, but more than one backyard birder has been disappointed to discover they may not be looking at a bird at all – hummingbird moths are fantastic hummingbird imposters. 1 Flocks: Hummingbirds are more aggressive than moths, and while some flocks may form at feeding areas, it is more common to see hummingbirds alone. 2  Hummingbird moths, however, are more likely to travel together and show no aggression toward other moths.
