The teardrop shape has a round or oval shape on half the facet pattern while the other half resembles a marquise with the sides tapering to a point. Some of the world's largest and most famous diamonds were cut in to the pear shaped brilliant cut. In the pear brilliant, as with marquise shapes and other elongated fancy shapes, there is an increased likelihood of having a bow tie.. This is a dark area in the shape of a man's bow tie that occurs when the angles of the cut are not optimum.
