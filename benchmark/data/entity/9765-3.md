Often called a teardrop diamond, the pear-shape diamond combines, at one end, the brilliance and facet similarities of an oval-cut diamond, and, at the other end, the strong shape of a marquise diamond. In essence, one end is rounded and the other is pointed. The pear-shape diamond weighs 67.89 carats and is a champagne color. It’s believed that the rough diamond from which it was cut weighed an astonishing 240 carats. It was first cut into a 75-carat pear shape and then re-cut into the 67.89-carat size to improve its brilliance.
