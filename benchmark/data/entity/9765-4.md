The pear shape is a combination of the round and marquise brilliant cuts and usually has the 58 facet brilliant pattern, but can be cut with different numbers of pavilion mains of 8, 7, 6, or 4 facets. In the pear brilliant, as with marquise shapes and other elongated fancy shapes, there is an increased likelihood of having a bow tie.. This is a dark area in the shape of a man's bow tie that occurs when the angles of the cut are not optimum.
