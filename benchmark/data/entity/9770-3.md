The oleander (Nerium oleander) grows as a rounded shrub, but it can also be trained as a small tree. These multibranched evergreens have dense, dark foliage that makes a thick screen; they’re often used to define borders in home landscapes or add privacy to the yard. Changing pH. Before planting oleanders, test your soil’s pH level. Most garden centers and nurseries sell at-home pH test kits that can give you a reasonably accurate measurement of your soil’s pH.
