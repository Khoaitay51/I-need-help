Oleander Care. When planted in soil with a proper pH, oleanders will grow from 3 to 20 feet tall and up to 10 feet wide. These adaptable evergreens are hardy in U.S. Department of Agriculture plant hardiness zones 9 to 11 and can thrive in a range of conditions, including drought, salt, and poor and sandy soil. Changing pH. Before planting oleanders, test your soil’s pH level. Most garden centers and nurseries sell at-home pH test kits that can give you a reasonably accurate measurement of your soil’s pH.
