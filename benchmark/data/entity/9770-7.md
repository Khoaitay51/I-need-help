All parts of the oleander are toxic if ingested, and skin contact can cause a rash. The beautiful, fragrant -- and all-over toxic -- oleander (Nerium oleander) is a shrub that survives tough conditions. For 10-10-10 fertilizer, you'd need to apply an entire 20-pound bag over 1,000 square feet to get 2 pounds of nitrogen (.10 X 20 = 2), or .4 pounds for a 20-square-foot area around a single shrub (20/50 = .4).
