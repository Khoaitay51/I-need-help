3. Choose tankmates, but pick the right ones! Although Bala sharks aren't as aggressive, there are fish unlikely to be successful with the Bala shark. 1  Tankmates can be: Larger cichlids, angelfish, parrot cichlids, mid sized tetra, Gouramis, Ram cichlids, Etc. Bala sharks grow to about, 10-14 in length but, when purchased could vary from the size of 1-5. When juvenile they should be kept in at least a 55 gallon aquarium (208.19 Liters) for a fully grown shoal of at least three Bala shark, 125 gallons (473.176 Liters) would be a reasonable minimum.
