Background of eye problems and Graves' disease. Eye problems developing with hyperthyroidism were described over 160 years ago by Dr. Robert Graves of Dublin. That is why it is called Graves' disease. It is now known to be an autoimmune disorder. The eye involvement, like the thyroid disorder, is mediated in part by white blood cells (lymphocytes) that accumulate in the targeted tissues.
