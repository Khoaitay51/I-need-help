Graves’ eye disease, like Graves’ hyperthyroidism and Hashimoto’s thyroiditis, is an autoimmune disorder. It is caused by the reaction of antibodies and certain white blood cells called lymphocytes, with proteins in eye muscle, the connective tissue and fat around the eyeball. Graves’ eye disease is an eye condition that occurs in about 50% of patients who currently have, or have had Graves’ hyperthyroidism if eyelid changes are included.
