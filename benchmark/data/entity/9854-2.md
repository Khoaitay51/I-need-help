Sweet dessert wines are also made from Pedro Ximenez or Moscatel grapes, and are sometimes blended with Palomino-based Sherries. The word Sherry is an anglicisation of Xeres (Jerez). Sherry was previously known as sack, from the Spanish saca, meaning extraction from the solera. 1 Oloroso ('scented' in Spanish) is a variety of sherry aged oxidatively for a longer time than a Fino or Amontillado, producing a darker and richer wine. 2  With alcohol levels between 18 and 20%, Olorosos are the most alcoholic sherries.
