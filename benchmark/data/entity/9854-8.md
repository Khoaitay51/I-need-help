Sherry is a fortified wine. During the fermentation of the grapes, a neutral grape spirit (alcohol) is added to the wine. This raises the alcohol level and stops the fermentation, similar to the production of Port and Madeira. 1 Pedro Ximenez: This type of Sherry is made exclusively from a grape called Pedro Ximenez. 2  The grapes are traditionally left to dry on mats in the hot sun, basically becoming raisins, concentrated and powerful. 3  The resulting wine is a rich, intense sweet wine which is best served as dessert or along with dessert courses.
