Best Answer: Bacteria, as mentioned in the previous answers, have no nucleus, so the DNA is located in the cytoplasm. However, the bacterial chromosome tends to be tethered to the plasma membrane. This area of the cytoplasm containing the chromosome is referred to as the nucleoid.
