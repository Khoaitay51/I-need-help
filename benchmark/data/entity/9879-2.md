Mango Lassi Recipe – a chilled beverage to beat the summer heat. It is made from mangoes, sugar and yogurt with pinch of cardamom powder. Mango lassi or aam ki lassi is thick, rich and creamy with lots of mango flavor. It is perfect for this summer. It is very refreshing and cooling beverage. You can have it as a breakfast instead of your breakfast smoothie. There are other variety of lassi that I make during summer. e.g. salted lassi, sweet lassi. How to make mango lassi (Step by Step Recipe with Photos): 1) Take chopped mangoes and sugar in a blender. Adjust the sugar quantity as per the sweetenss of the mangoes. Blend it till you get smooth puree. 2) Add cardamom powder. 3) and rose water. 4) Also add yogurt. 5) and few ice cubes.
