The black truffle or Tuber Melanosporum, also known as the Perigord truffle, grows mainly in France and Italy and is the variety most commonly cultivated in a truffle orchard or trufferie. With a unique scent, they our sniffed out by trained pigs and dogs. The ripe truffle, when cut, exhibits a dense black interior that is streaked with white veins. The truffle is firm and resembles the texture of a potato. The outside has a fairly tough, scaly skin that can withstand the conditions in which the truffle grows.
