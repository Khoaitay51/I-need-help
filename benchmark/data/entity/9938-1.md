(ii) Kingdom Protista: (Unicellular eukaryotes) It is a kingdom of unicellular eukaryotic organisms. These organisms are usually found in aquatic habitats. They show wide range of variations in cell structure, nutrition, reproduction etc. All other kingdoms include eukaryotic organisms. Unicellular eukaryotics are included in kingdom protista. All other kingdoms include multicellular eukaryotes. Remaining three kingdom are classified on the basis of mode of nutrition.
