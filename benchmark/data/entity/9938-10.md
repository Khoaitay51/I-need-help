The organisms which are included in this kingdom are unicellular plants, unicellular fungi and unicellular animals. Thus it is a heterogeneous group. So all types of mode of nutrition are observed. Algae and diatoms are autotrophs. Protozoans like amoeba and paramoecium are predators. All other kingdoms include eukaryotic organisms. Unicellular eukaryotics are included in kingdom protista. All other kingdoms include multicellular eukaryotes. Remaining three kingdom are classified on the basis of mode of nutrition.
