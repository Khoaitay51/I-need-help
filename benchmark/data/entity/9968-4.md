by Laurie. Many people think that a baby rabbits is called a bunny, but this is actually just an affectionate nickname for rabbits, and not the correct English name for rabbit babies. So, if you want to know what baby rabbits are called, it depends what century you are looking at. If you (or the rabbits) were living in the eleventh century, baby rabbits (coneys) were called rabbits, but today in the modern age, baby rabbits are called kits or kittens.
