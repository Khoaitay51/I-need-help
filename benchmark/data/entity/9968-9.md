A lesser known fact is that in ancient times rabbits where in fact called coneys. An adult rabbit was known as a coney, and a baby coney (baby rabbit) was called a rabbit! So, if you want to know what baby rabbits are called, it depends what century you are looking at. If you (or the rabbits) were living in the eleventh century, baby rabbits (coneys) were called rabbits, but today in the modern age, baby rabbits are called kits or kittens.
