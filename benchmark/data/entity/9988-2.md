Knowledge center. Prader-Willi syndrome, also known as PWS is an uncommon genetic disorder (present at birth) in which seven genes (or subsets) on chromosome 15 are deleted or unexpressed. Patients with PWS may have physical, mental and behavioral problems-the main one being an unrelenting feeling of hunger. There is no cure for Prader-Willi syndrome, however, several treatments are available which help to lessen the signs and symptoms of the condition, including infant/child nutrition, growth hormone therapy, sex hormone therapy, physical therapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, and developmental therapy.
