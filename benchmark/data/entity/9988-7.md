Causes. Prader-Willi syndrome is a genetic disorder, a condition caused by an error in one or more genes. Although the exact genes responsible for Prader-Willi syndrome haven't been identified, the problem lies in a particular region of chromosome 15. Prader-Willi syndrome disrupts the normal functions of a portion of the brain called the hypothalamus. The hypothalamus controls hunger and thirst and releases hormones that prompt the release of other substances responsible for growth and sexual development.
