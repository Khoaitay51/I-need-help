1 The lac operon consists of three structural genes, and a promoter, a terminator, regulator, and an operator. 2  The three structural genes are: lacZ, lacY, and lacA. The protein that is formed by the lacI gene is known as the lac repressor. The type of regulation that the lac operon undergoes is referred to as negative inducible, meaning that the gene is turned off by the regulatory factor (lac repressor) unless some molecule (lactose) is added.
