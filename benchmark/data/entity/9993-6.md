When glucose is present, the CRP does not bind to the promoter and expression of the lac operon remains low even if lactose is present. However, when glucose is absent, the CAP binds to the promoter region and promotes transcription. Glucose does not directly regulate the binding of CRP. A cAMP-CRP complex is entailed. Structure and Mechanism. The lac operon consists of three structural genes, Z, Y and A, which code for the enzymes necessary to catabolize lactose and a regulator gene, I, which codes for a regulator protein. The regulator protein regulates the operon.
