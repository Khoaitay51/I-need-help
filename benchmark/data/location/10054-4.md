Santa Catalina Island does have two cities if you will, although city is a relative term on Catalina. Neither Avalon nor Two Harbors are large. Avalon is actually the only incorporated one of the two towns, becoming a city in 1913, and has about 4,000 permanent citizens. Catalina Island (whose official, but rarely used, name is Santa Catalina Island) is one of a group of islands in California, lying off the southern coast of the state. It is a popular day trip and vacation destination for locals and tourists. Catalina Island and the surrounding islands are part of Los Angeles County
