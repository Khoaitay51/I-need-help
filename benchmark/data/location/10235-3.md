(Visit-Portugal History) Faro-Description Particularly attractive is the old part of the city surrounded still by the Roman walls which date back to the 9th. Inside a spacious open square that was once the site of the Roman Forum is a 13th Century Cathedral that faces the 18th Century Episcopal palace. The Parish Church was built in the 16th century on the site of a previous chapel. The attractive Manor House know as Pal cio de Estoi has been converted into a Pousada. Further to the north is the town of Santa Barb ra de Nexe with a Bronze Age hilltop fort nearby.
