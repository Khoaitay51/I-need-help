Faro Portugal, is a nice place to shop. In the center is a pedestian area with a variety of shops, but just outside Faro Portugal, you find a large shopping center, called Forum Algarve. Here you find some nice clothes and shoe shops, furniture and sport shops. Faro Portugal, has a variety of bars and restaurants at the Rua de Santo Antonio. While in town, treat yourself to the local cakes and desserts, and sweet 'bolos' bursting with figs and almonds. For sea views, book a table at the Ginasio Clube Naval, a wonderful little eatery overlooking the harbor of Faro Portugal.
