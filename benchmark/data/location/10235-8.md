Faro is a Portuguese city with a long history. Over 2000 years ago the Romans built a major port. Faro Portugal, became in 1756 the capital city of Portugal's most southern province, the Algarve. Today Faro Portugal is the administrative center of the district of Faro. Faro has an international airport where many tourist of various countries arrive to start their holiday in the Algarve. Faro Portugal, has a variety of bars and restaurants at the Rua de Santo Antonio. While in town, treat yourself to the local cakes and desserts, and sweet 'bolos' bursting with figs and almonds. For sea views, book a table at the Ginasio Clube Naval, a wonderful little eatery overlooking the harbor of Faro Portugal.
