The name is Plains Miwok and is constructed from moke, meaning fishnet, and-umne, a suffix meaning people of. The town of Mokelumne Hill was named for the river in about 1850. The confluence of the North and Middle Forks forms the Mokelumne River proper. The main stem flows west-southwest past Mokelumne Hill into Pardee Reservoir, formed by the 345-foot (105 m)-high Pardee Dam.
