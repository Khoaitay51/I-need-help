Things to do on holiday in Marmaris. An exciting way to explore the surrounding countryside of Marmaris is by Jeep. There are a number of tours and safaris available to see some of the unspoilt scenery hidden away from the main tourist resort. In just over 4 hours travelling from the UK, you can be entertained from dawn ‘til dusk or, if you prefer, dusk ‘til dawn on holiday in Marmaris. Fast becoming one of the most popular resorts in Turkey, there is plenty to do on holiday in Marmaris for divers and dancers alike.
