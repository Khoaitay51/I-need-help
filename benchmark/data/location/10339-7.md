A holiday to Marmaris is the place to head for a never-say-sleep fun resort. Nestling on the cusp of the Aegean and Mediterranean Seas, it has some of the best bars and clubs on Turkey’s south coast. Flight time from UK: Approx. 4 hours. Tourist Information: Further Marmaris tourist information can be found at www.gototurkey.co.uk. Visa & Health: Before you travel, visit www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/turkey for recommendations and advice on visas and health for your holiday to Marmaris.
