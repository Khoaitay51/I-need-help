The Robertson family has filmed four seasons of Duck Dynasty at locations throughout Ouachita Parish in the Monroe and West Monroe area (see details below). Due to the huge success of the series, Duck Dynasty began Season 2 in October, 2012, followed by Season 3 early in 2013. The show, produced for A&E Networks by Gurney Productions, ended 2012 as the most watched A&E telecast of all time with 6.5 million viewers.
