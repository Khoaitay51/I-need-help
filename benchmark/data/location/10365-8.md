Duck Dynasty is filmed in the Monroe area of northeast Louisiana, and airs on the A&E Network which premiered the show in March of 2012. Due to the huge success of the series, Duck Dynasty began Season 2 in October, 2012, followed by Season 3 early in 2013. The show, produced for A&E Networks by Gurney Productions, ended 2012 as the most watched A&E telecast of all time with 6.5 million viewers.
