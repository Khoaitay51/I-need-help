The new President of the Methodist Church in Ireland is Rev. Aian Ferguson, who was installed in Carlisle Road on Friday evening, and has taken as his theme: “One Lord-One Task”. He succeeds Rev. Roy Cooper, who returns to the Newtownards Circuit in Co Down. Rev. Ferguson and his wife Joan are both from Lisburn Road, and they have four children: Gillian, Peter, Stephen and Nicola. Right Rev. Ken Good and Rev. Peter Thompson from the Church of Ireland; Very Rev. Dr John Finlay and Mrs Ruby Miller from the Presbyterian Church; and Mrs Mary Crawford from the Religious Society of Friends, were the official observers.
