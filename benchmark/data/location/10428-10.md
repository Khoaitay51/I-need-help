On the west side of the swamp, at Billy's Island, logging equipment and other artifacts remain of a 1920s logging town of 600 residents. Most of the Okefenokee Swamp is included in the 403,000-acre (1,630 km 2) Okefenokee National Wildlife Refuge. A wildfire begun by a lightning strike near the center of the refuge on May 5, 2007, eventually merged with another wildfire which began near Waycross, Georgia, on April 16 when a tree fell on a power line. The Suwanee River originates as stream channels in the heart of Okefenokee Swamp and drains at least 90% of the swamp's watershed southwest towards the Gulf of Mexico.
