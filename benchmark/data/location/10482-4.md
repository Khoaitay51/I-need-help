In both 2000 and 2001, Tanjung Pelepas Port was recognized as the Best Emerging Container Terminal by Lloyd's List Maritime Asia. In 2002, the second biggest shipping company in the world, Evergreen Marine Corporation, moved its operations from Singapore to Tanjung Pelepas Port. Tanjung Pelepas Port offers a sheltered bay without tide restrictions. The terminal has depths ranging from 15 to 19 meters (49.2 to 62.3 feet). The 12.6-kilometer (20.3-mile) access channel and 720-meter (2362-foot) turning basin allow Tanjung Pelepas Port to accommodate the biggest cargo vessels.
