The Johor Port Authority (JPA) regulates and is responsible for all Tanjung Pelepas Port operations. The JPA also monitors Tanjung Pelepas Port performance to assure continued high-quality operations. Tanjung Pelepas Port offers a sheltered bay without tide restrictions. The terminal has depths ranging from 15 to 19 meters (49.2 to 62.3 feet). The 12.6-kilometer (20.3-mile) access channel and 720-meter (2362-foot) turning basin allow Tanjung Pelepas Port to accommodate the biggest cargo vessels.
