Arkansas-French variant of Quapaw, a Siouan people meaning downstream people.. Connecticut-From Mohican and other Algonquin words meaning long river place.. Delaware-Named for Lord De La Warr, early governor of Virginia; first applied to river, then to Indian tribe (Lenni-Lenape), and the state. Hawaii-Possibly derived from native word for homeland, Hawaiki or Owhyhee. Michigan-From Chippewa words mici gama meaning great water, after the lake of the same name. Minnesota-From Dakota Sioux word meaning cloudy water or sky-tinted water of the Minnesota River.
