Mississippi-Probably Chippewa; mici zibi, great river or gathering-in of all the waters.. Also: Algonquin word, Messipi.. Missouri-An Algonquin Indian term meaning river of the big canoes.. Nebraska-From Omaha or Otos Indian word meaning broad water or flat river, describing the Platte River. North & South Dakota-Dakota is Sioux for friend or ally. Michigan-From Chippewa words mici gama meaning great water, after the lake of the same name. Minnesota-From Dakota Sioux word meaning cloudy water or sky-tinted water of the Minnesota River.
