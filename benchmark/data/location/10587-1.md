Mckinney is part of Collin County. Mckinney has 62.21 square miles of land area and 0.67 square miles of water area. As of 2010, the total Mckinney population is 131,117, which has grown 141.16% since 2000. The population growth rate is much higher than the state average rate of 20.59% and is much higher than the national average rate of 9.71%.
