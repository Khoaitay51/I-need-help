Kazakhstan Map-Kazakhstan Located to the north western part of China besides the River Ural of Central Asia, Kazakhstan covers total land area of 2,717,300 sq. km and holds a population of 15,284,929 according to the population estimate of the year 2007. Kazakhstan, also known as Republic of Kazakhstan is the 9th largest country in terms of land area. It is situated in Central Asia and Europe and borders Russia, China, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan.
