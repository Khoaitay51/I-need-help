Majorca is near Spain and is very sunny most of the year. Majorca is a small island just off the south east coast of Spain. It is part of the Balearic Islands. Majorca is the largest island in the group, with Formentera as the smallest inhabited island in the group. Formentera is widely known because it permits nude sunbathing on most of its beaches. It is approximately 310 miles/ 200Km from the main city, Palma, to Madrid, capital
