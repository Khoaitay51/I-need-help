Your mouth, nose, throat, sinuses and lungs are lined with mucus membranes. These membranes contain mucus glands that produce--surprise--mucus. Mucus-producing membranes line specific passages in your body, like the respiratory and digestive tracts, for protection and support. The cilia transport the mucus layer toward your throat, where it could either get spit out or swallowed. Mucus defends your body. Mucus contains antibodies, enzymes, and proteins that work to help get rid of whatever's in the air you've inhaled that could make you sick.
