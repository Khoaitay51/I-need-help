1 Lavender plants grow in hardiness zones 5 through 10 and are planted about 15 inches (38.1 cm) apart. 2  The lavender blooms come in midsummer and range from lavender-hued gray to a vibrant royal purple. 3  There are also species with blooms in other colors: white, pink, and a yellow-green. Easy to grow and easy to appreciate, lavender is a welcome addition to any garden, with its beautiful flowers and wonderful aroma. All you need to grow and maintain this fragrant flowering herb is a suitable spot in the garden and a little horticultural know-how.
