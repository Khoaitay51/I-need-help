GEORGIE CIOPEI, son of Iustin and Maria Ciopei, born 15 June 1897 in Tereblecea, Bucovina, Austria-Hungary; died at the age of 4 months on 8 Nov. 1897 in Tereblecea. Georgie was born at house no. 330, and the midwife at his birth was Varvara Cigan of Tereblecea. Since Ecaterina was a Ciopei of house no. 6, and was of the same generation as Ioan Ciopei's children who were also from house no. 6, it is almost certain that Ecaterina was also one of Ioan's children. Ecaterina's husband was GRIGORI GAVRILOAIEI, son of Ioan Gavriloaiei, a peasant farmer
