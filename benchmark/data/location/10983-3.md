Konstantin Sergeyevich Stanislavsky (Russian: константи́н серге́евич станисла́вский; also transliterated as Constantin Stanislavski; 17 January [ O.S. 5 January] 1863 – 7 August 1938) was a Russian actor and theatre director. In February 1897 Stanislavsky joined Anton Chekhov, whom he had met on 15 February at a literary-musical evening, in an open public discussion on the creation of a popular theatre that was reported in the press.
