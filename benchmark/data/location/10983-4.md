3M. SIMION CIOPEI, son of Michail Ciopei of Tereblecea, born 9 Feb. 1847, probably in Tereblecea, died 18 Oct. 1910 at the age of 62 years 8 months in Tereblecea, Bucovina, Austria-Hungary. The cause of death was  de oftiga  (consumption or tuberculosis). Since Ecaterina was a Ciopei of house no. 6, and was of the same generation as Ioan Ciopei's children who were also from house no. 6, it is almost certain that Ecaterina was also one of Ioan's children. Ecaterina's husband was GRIGORI GAVRILOAIEI, son of Ioan Gavriloaiei, a peasant farmer of Tereblecea.
