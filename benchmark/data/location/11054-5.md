Address: Madgaon, Goa, India URL for more details/booking: http://www.goatourism.gov.in/. About 33 km south of Panjim, Madgaon is a major religious centre and commercial hub of Goa. Madgaon is the oldest and the second largest city in Goa. Madgaon is the capital of Salcete taluk which gets its name from the Sal River.
