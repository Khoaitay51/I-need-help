Place Details: About 33 km south of Panjim, Madgaon is a major religious centre and commercial hub of Goa. Madgaon is the oldest and the second largest city in Goa. Located on the banks of the Sal River, the city gets its name from the Sanskrit word ‘Mathagram’ which stands for a place that has a math. Madgaon is the capital of Salcete taluk which gets its name from the Sal River.
