SEATTLE — Washington Department of Transportation officials say they will close the Alaskan Way Viaduct when they start tunneling underneath it. The closure could last up to two weeks. About 110,000 cars and trucks drive the viaduct through downtown Seattle every day. WSDOT closed the viaduct for a week in 2011.
