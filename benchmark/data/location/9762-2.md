Your provider will use a CT scan to help guide a needle to the lymph node. Your provider will remove a small piece of tissue from the lymph nodes for testing. You will be in recovery for up to 6 hours to make sure that there is no bleeding. Avoid strenuous activity for the rest of the day. Lymph nodes are oval-shaped organs found throughout your body. The lymph nodes are like a filter, keeping bacteria and other harmful substances out of the bloodstream. They may also trap cancer cells. However, they can also develop a cancer called lymphoma.
