Right behind the central sulcus and the front portion of the parietal lobe is the post central gyrus, the secondary somatosensory cortical region. Separating this and the back part of parietal cortex is the post central sulcus. The back portion of parietal cortex has further divisions. They are: The structure of parietal lobe can be segregated into 3 different parts: 1  The Central Sulcus: The central sulcus divides the parietal lobe from the frontal lobe. 2  The Parieto-Occipital Sulcus: It divides the parietal and occipital lobes and. 3  The Lateral Sulcus: It separates parietal from the temporal lobe.
