The temporal lobe is located on the bottom of the brain below the lateral fissure. This lobe is also the location of the primary auditory cortex, which is important for interpreting the sounds and the language we hear. The occipital lobe is located at the back portion of the brain behind the parietal and temporal lobes. The frontal lobe is located at the front of the central sulcus where it receives information signals from other lobes of the brain. The parietal lobe processes sensory information for cognitive purposes and helps coordinate spatial relations so we can make sense of the world around us.
