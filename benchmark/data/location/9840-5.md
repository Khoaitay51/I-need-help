The difference between these three types of zebras is only about where do zebras live and the answer is that they live at different places. One type of zebra wants to live on mountains and is known as a mountain zebras. Second type lives on plain, grassy lands and is called as Burchell's zebras. Zebras are fond of dry places and can be found in deserts of Africa very easily. Not only in deserts, zebras also prefer to live in open areas having green grass and water around. Besides grasslands and deserts, zebras can also be heavily found on various hilly areas of Africa.
