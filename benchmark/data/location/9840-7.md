Also where do zebras live has a direct connection with their ages as well. Zebras are fond of dry places and can be found in deserts of Africa very easily. Not only in deserts, zebras also prefer to live in open areas having green grass and water around. Besides grasslands and deserts, zebras can also be heavily found on various hilly areas of Africa.
