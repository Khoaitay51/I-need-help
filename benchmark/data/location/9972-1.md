The Danube is one of the world’s greatest rivers. It is the second longest river in Europe after the Volga and the longest river in the European Union. It measures 2,857 km in length and up to 1.5 km in width, with depths between 1 to 8 metres and drains 817,000 km². The Sava is shared by Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia and Montenegro. The Siret flows from its source in Ukraine into Romania. The Siret River Basin is the third-largest catchment area of any Danube tributary, and is situated to the east of the Carpathians.
