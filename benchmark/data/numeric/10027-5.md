Average Salary: $42,000 annually. Registered nurses are a large part of the nurse workforce. RNs practice in a variety of specialties and work environments. RNs must have at least an associate's degree in nursing, and many RNs have a bachelor's degree. Salary Range: $70,000-80,000+ annually. Clinical nurse specialists hold at least a master's degree in nursing with a CNS track in one of several medical specialties, such as oncology, geriatrics, cardiology, etc.
