Applying Stain. Deck stain can be sprayed on but is most commonly applied with a brush. Temperature and weather are very important when applying stain. You do not want to apply stain when it is wet or too cold or hot. You want the deck to be clean and dry so that the stain can soak in thoroughly. The temperature should be between 50 and 90 degrees Fahrenheit in the air and on the surface of the deck. If it is too cold, the stain will not dry completely or will be damaged. You will want the temperature to be in the safe zone for at least twenty-four hours.
