Radon mitigation is any process or system used to reduce radon concentrations in the breathing zones of occupied buildings. The goal of a radon mitigation system is to reduce the indoor radon levels to below the EPA action level of 4 pCi/L. 1 Slab-On-Grade: Slabs built on grade can have many openings that allow radon to enter, just as in a basement. 2  Crawl Space: Homes with crawl spaces can also have elevated radon levels. 3  The vacuum effect can draw radon gas from a crawl space into the home.
