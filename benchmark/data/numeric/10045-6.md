Assuming you are referring to the proceeds from a death benefit on a life insurance policy, the process does not take long. Once the claim is submitted, it will be processed and a check can often be sent or deposited into a money-market-like find within 10-14 days. Usually it doesn't take long to receive a death benefit check. First a claim form must be completed and returned to the insurance company, along with a certified copy of the death certificate.
