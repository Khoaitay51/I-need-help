I have seen babies survive with the advances in medical technology as young as 26 weeks gestation. However on the flip side I've also seen a baby that at 35 weeks didn't have mature lungs and was never able to take a breath on his own. Babies that are born around 26 weeks and weigh only a pound may survive with extreme medical intervention, but usually have life long complications due to a premature birth. Usually babies born after the 36 -38 week mark have the best chance of thriving without medical complications. E&L · 7 years ago. Thumbs up.
