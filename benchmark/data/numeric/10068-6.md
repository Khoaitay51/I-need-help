General. As of May 2011, airline pilots made a mean $118,070 per year, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. About 10 percent earned the low of $57,420 annually and another 10 percent earned the high of more than $187,199 yearly. This was the highest mean for all transportation and material moving occupations in the country, which averaged $33,200 per year. Experience. According to FltOps as of 2012, the salaries of airline pilots varied by experience and rank. In the first year, a first officer starts at an average $36,283. He is more often known as the co-pilot and is second-in-command of the flight behind the captain or pilot.
