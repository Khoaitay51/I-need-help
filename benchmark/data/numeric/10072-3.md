Salary and Benefits-Officer Salary ​Cadet ​$4,442 - $5,694 ​Cadets earn a monthly salary while attending the CHP Academy. in addition, cadets receive free room and board. Effective the first month following their Academy reporting date, cadets receive full health and dental benefits for themselves and their dependents. Officers receive a stipend equal to 3.5% of their base pay to compensate for pre-and post-shift activities. In addition, all officers earn the following pay incentives: Physical performance Pay (PPP) - $65.00 per month; after the fifth year, $130.00 a month.
