Maintaining a normal platelet count range is very important. If you don’t know what are platelets, in simple terms, they are blood components which have a very important role in blood clotting, which is most essential in cases of bleeding. Platelets are also known as thrombocytes. A low platelet count is a condition that is known as thrombocytopenia. It happens when platelets drop to a level lower than the normal range. On the other hand, when the amount goes above platelets normal range, it is referred to a condition called thrombocytosis.
