Travel safety. Most airlines charge 10% of the adult fare for a child under two years old to fly without a seat of their own. If you want your baby to have their own seat, it may cost 50-70% of the adult fare, but you should always check with the individual airline. If you have booked your child a seat, you will also have to pre-book a travel cot or sky cot for them. These are usually only available on long-haul flights.
