Report Abuse. There is no particular age that a baby is allowed to fly. They can fly as young as 1 month with a Dr.'s approval. Just speak to your Dr. & see if he or she has any health or other concerns & if not feel free to fly when you wish. At that age I had a bottle ready for her for take off and landing (you could ask to sit in the back of the plane and breastfeed if you'd like) and held her the whole trip.
