Staff members with an academic title usually teach less than those without. As an Assistant Professor in Turkey you may expect a contracted worload of six hours of teaching to undergraduates per week and some other time (2-3 hours) to be dedicated to teaching to graduate students and thesis supervision. Higher Education in Turkey. Higher education is provided by 82 institutions: 54 are state universities, and 2 public Institutes of Technology; 24 are private universities, and there are 2 Vocational Schools of Higher Education.
