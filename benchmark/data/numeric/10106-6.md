Absolute Neutrophil Count Normal Range. The number of neutrophil granulocytes or polymorphonuclear cells or white blood cells present in your blood can be measured in terms of Absolute neutrophil count in it. The white blood cells that are measured through absolute neutrophil counts help your body in fighting with infections. The measurement of absolute neutrophil count is done by calculating the total number of white blood cells on the basis of combined percentage of bands immature neutrophil and mature neutrophils.
