In this medium-risk population, the treatment of 1000 patients with aspirin for four years may prevent 51 important cardiovascular events, an absolute benefit at least 10 times greater than that obtained in the American and British doctors' trials. Indirect comparisons of the effects of starting antiplatelet treatment either preoperatively or within 24 hours after the operation indicated that the two approaches yielded similar benefits 70. The effects of doses of aspirin in the range of 100 to 160 mg daily have been studied in three trials 71-73.
