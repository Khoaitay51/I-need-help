Upper GI Endoscopy = Esophago-Gastro-Duodenoscopy (EGD). Upper gastrointestinal (GI) endoscopy is a diagnostic procedure enabling your doctor to see inside your esophagus, stomach and duodenum (the first part of the small intestine), using a flexible viewing tube – gastroscope. The cost of upper endoscopy (without insurance) may range from $800 to $1,800 and depends on country, hospital, procedures performed during investigation and the cost of sedatives and anesthetics.
