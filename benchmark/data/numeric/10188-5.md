Peak flow measurement using a peak flow meter is particularly useful for individuals with asthma. During an asthma flare-up, the large airways in the lungs slowly begin to narrow. This slows the speed of air leaving the lungs. Peak flow may also be measured with a peak flow meter (PFM), a portable, hand-held device. Both devices take the measurement as an individual forcefully blows into the mouthpiece of the device. Spirometry is usually performed in a doctor’s office, clinic, or a hospital.
