Covering the Hard Costs of Invisalign. The cost of Invisalign will vary depending on the complexity of your tooth issues, the number of aligner trays it will take to achieve straight teeth and the coverage your dental insurance provides. For simpler cases, Invisalign may only cost a couple thousand dollars. 1 Time: You still have to wear Invisalign aligner trays for 22 to 24 hours a day for 6 to 18 months typically. 2  But you only need to schedule appointments every 4 to 6 weeks, compared to every 4 weeks with braces.
