Connect the smaller end of the USB cable into the Sansa Clip on the left-hand side of the player. Plug the larger end of the USB cable into the USB port on your computer. Do not use a low-power USB port as it may not be able to properly and fully charge the Sansa battery. Turn the computer on. If it does not, try a different USB port. Reset the device if the battery does not charge. Slide the Power button up and hold it in place for 15 seconds. Release the button. The Sansa Clip will power on and then off. Attempt to recharge the battery again. Replace the USB cord if the Sansa Clip still won't charge. If the device is charging, allow it to fully charge, which should take approximately three to four hours. Power the device on. If the battery has not charged or will not hold a charge, the battery is dead
