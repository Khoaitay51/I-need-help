A Sansa Clip is an MP3 player manufactured by SanDisk. An MP3 player stores and plays digital music files that can be transferred to the device through the use of a computer. If your Sansa Clip won't charge or won't hold a charge, troubleshooting can help you determine the problem. If it does not, try a different USB port. Reset the device if the battery does not charge. Slide the Power button up and hold it in place for 15 seconds. Release the button. The Sansa Clip will power on and then off. Attempt to recharge the battery again. Replace the USB cord if the Sansa Clip still won't charge. If the device is charging, allow it to fully charge, which should take approximately three to four hours. Power the device on.
