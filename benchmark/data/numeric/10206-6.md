With all else equal, RNs with BSN degrees receive higher salaries than those with ASN or ADN degrees. In 2011, ADNs earned about $64,000 on average while BSNs made $76,000. These rates are based on the Indeed website as the BLS does not calculate the pay difference between RNs with ASN and BSN degrees. RN versus BSN Salary Based on Experience. Generally, the salary rates go higher as a nurse gains experience. In the first few years in service, RNs with BSN degrees may earn about $57,280. On the other hand, RNs with an ASN or ADN degree may land entry-level positions that will provide them with $40,250 on average
