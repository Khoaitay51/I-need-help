It is best to plant your tomatoes indoors to start with, you can put them in small containers. The best time to start planting is eight to ten weeks before the last frost date in your area. The seeds should be planted about 1/8 of af an inch into the soil, use seed starting soil to plant your seeds. Gardening is a hobby for some people while for others it is how they feed their family sometime. The most popular thing to grow right in your back yard are tomatoes. Tomatoes are used for just about everything so there is a high demand for them. If you use them frequently then growing them is a very good thing to do.
