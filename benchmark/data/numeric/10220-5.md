Although the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons suggests athletes obtain 12 to 15 percent of their calories from protein, a 2009 review in “Nutrition and Metabolism” reports that individualized protein needs are based on an athlete’s body weight, not the percentage of his total calorie intake. General guidelines exist to help determine the percent of an athlete’s calories that should come from dietary fat. While protein and carbs provide 4 calories in each gram, fat contains 9 calories per gram.
