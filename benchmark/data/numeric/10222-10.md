A n adult female cod lays between 4 and 6 million eggs at a single spawning. The eggs are released into the water to become fertilized. They develop and grow without help from the parents. All but a handful of these millions of eggs will end up as food for other water creatures. Also, this stage lasts longer than the egg stage. In addition, they need a steady supply of food and will die if there isn't enough. Those that do survive, drift and grow until midsummer. At this point they are young fish about 2 - 2.5 cm (3/4 - 1 in.) in length.
