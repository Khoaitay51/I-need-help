Men's Nets. The standard net height for men is 7 feet, 11 5/8 inches. Men playing in events for ages 55 and over are allowed to lower the net to 7 feet, 9 5/8 inches. Events for men 70 years and older are permitted to use a net that is 7 feet, 6 inches. Co-ed events are played with the same nets used by men.
