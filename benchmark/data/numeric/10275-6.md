The salary of a preschool teacher varies considerably across states. The highest-paying state on average is New York, where preschool teachers earn a mean annual salary of $39,560. Other high-paying states include New Jersey at $37,370, Alaska at $35,120, Vermont at $33,670 and Michigan at $33,430. The 10th-percentile salary level, indicative of a the field's starting salary, was $8.53 an hour, or $17,750 annually. The top 10 percent of preschool teachers earned $84,320 or more annually.
