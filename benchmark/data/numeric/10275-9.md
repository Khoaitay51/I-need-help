Teaching the next generation can be rewarding in an intangible sense, but salary-wise, it’s not the most lucrative social service job. Elementary school teachers ($56,320) and middle school teachers ($56,630) both earned average salaries in 2013 that were higher than preschool teachers, who earned an average of $31,420. Instructing very young children isn’t as lucrative as teaching preteens or teenagers. A preschool teacher earned a median salary of $27,570 in 2013. The profession’s top salaries were more than $49,660 a year, while the lowest salaries were $18,420 or less.
