Making overhead and profit an incurred coverage item can put a huge burden on an insured that may not have the means to front the expense. Imagine a homeowner has a fire that destroys their home. The replacement cost of the home is $200,000 and after overhead and profit is added the total rebuild cost is then $240,000. However, the home is older and the insurance carrier depreciates the home by $50,000 making an actual cash value payment of $150,000.
