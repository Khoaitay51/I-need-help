The woofer driver is responsible for low, bass frequencies. Deep, pulsating notes come from the woofer. In technical terms, the range of a woofer is between 20 Hz, the lowest range of human hearing, and 1,000 Hz. However, some woofers that have to cover part of the mid-range can operate as high as 5,000 Hz. Subwoofers are a type of woofer that plays the very lowest frequencies. They operate from 20 Hz up to 120 Hz
