Jehovah's Witnesses got their modern day start in the late 1800's. Charles Taze Russell contrary to popular belief is not the founder of Jehovah's Witnesses. He along with other associates incorporated the Watchtower Bible and Tract Society in 1884 as the legal agency or corporation used by Jehovah's Witnesses. The religion of Jehovah's Witnesses is thousands of years old,at least 6,000 years. Abel and other faithful people before Jesus were called witnesses at Hebrews 11 and 12. Jesus Christ is called the Faithful and true Witness at Revelation 3:14.
