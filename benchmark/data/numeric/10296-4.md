Bone-In 1 1/2-Inch (4 cm). 20 to 30 minutes. The secret to a great pork chop is a hot fire and the right time. Preheat your grill or broiler to high before you start. If you are using a broiler, the meat should be 4 to 5 inches from the heat. Whether grilling or broiling, turn the pork chops over half way through the cooking time to get them cooked evenly.
