The key to successfully grilling thick-cut pork is the keep the temperature of the grill low and to grill them slowly. Trying to rush the cooking will lead to a burned pork chop that is raw in the middle. Get the grilling right and you will have a delicious entree entrée that the entire family will. enjoy The meat's internal temperature should reach 150 to 155 degrees Fahrenheit. If the temperature is less than 150, put the meat back onto the grill. Grill the pork chops one more minute for every 10 degrees the pork chop is under the optimal temperature.
