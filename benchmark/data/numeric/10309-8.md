6 pork chops, 3/4 inch thick. salt and pepper to taste. 1 cup BBQ sauce, you favorite recipe or brand. Directions. Prepare the grill for high direct heat, preheating until it is ready. Oil the grates. Trim off excess fat and pepper both sides of pork chops. Add to grill over direct high heat with lid closed. Cook for 2 minutes. Turn pork chops over and continue grilling, lid closed, for another 1 minute. Reduce grill to medium low with a gas grill or move chops to a cooler portion of the charcoal grill.
