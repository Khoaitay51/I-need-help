1. You can’t work more than 48 hours a week on average-normally averaged over 17 weeks. This law is sometimes called the ‘working time directive’ or ‘working time regulations’. You can choose to work more by opting out of the 48-hour week. If you’re under 18, you can’t work more than 8 hours a day or 40 hours a week. You may have to work more than 48 hours a week on average if you work in a job: 1  where 24-hour staffing is required. 2  in the armed forces, emergency services or police.
