June Weather In Juneau. The hottest June day in Juneau for the past 66 years was 86 degrees Fahrenheit, measured on June 8, 1969, and the normal high is 62. The lowest documented temperature for Juneau for the past 66 years in June was 31 most recently registered on June 4, 1971, while the normal low measured temperature is 46. National Weather Service historical Juneau June Weather data indicates that on June 3, 1972, the recorded high did not exceed 45 degrees Fahrenheit, the lowest high measured in Juneau in the past 66 years.
