Average price examples. As you can imagine there is no specific figure you can put on a window like this. A lot will depend on a number of different factors. These include the size of the window, what it will be made from (wood or uPVC) and the quality of the glass used all have an effect on the price. However it is possible to get a ballpark figure. Sash windows made in uPVC can start at around £300, although the cost can be more depending on the specific company you use.
