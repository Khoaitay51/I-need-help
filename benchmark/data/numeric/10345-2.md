As a body is completely reduced to just ashes in the cremation process, there are quite strict laws governing the cremation of a body. There are licensing and operational requirements for crematories, and state laws governing who can authorize a cremation and how long after a death a cremation can be performed. In many of the states where the cremation rate is higher, prices are more competitively priced and a basic cremation can be purchased for around $700 - $900. In Florida and Nevada the prices for a direct cremation can be as low as $450.
