Best Answer: Depends on the size of the aircraft. Large aircraft like a 747 or A340s need about 150 knots on rotation. Medium to smaller aircraft (like the 737, A320s) need only about 130 - 140 knots. Th jet I fly rotates anywhere between 1bout 105knots and about 119 knots (119-136mph). Its a relatively small jet though, so expect larger and heavier planes to be significantly higher.
