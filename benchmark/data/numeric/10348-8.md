Your jet aircraft usually stay around the same speeds though. Rotate speed is about 130-150 knots (150-170 mph). They then cruise anywhere from 250-400 knots (290-460 mph). Such a big possibility for the speeds because it all depends on the airplane. As fast as necessary to produce enough lift so the aircraft can safely fly. Some can take off with a forward speed of 40 miles per hour, others need speeds that are more than 150 miles per hour, & a few even more than that.
