The top 10 percent of all restaurant managers earned salaries of more than $81,400 per year, according to a May 2011 Bureau of Labor Statistics' report. These managers may be more experienced on average and work in areas that typically pay higher salaries. Salary & Benefits. Restaurant managers earned an average salary of $52,620 per year, according to May 2011 data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. This equates to approximately $25.30 per hour. The median salary for these workers was $48,110 per year or $23.13 per hour. Median salaries are those that appear in the middle of salary lists. The middle half of all restaurant managers made between $38,090 and $62,490 per year. The bottom 10 percent made less than $30,570 per year
