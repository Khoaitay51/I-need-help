Restaurant manager salary by years experience is as follows: restaurant manager with less than 1 year of experience earns $24,099 to $43,759 per year. Those with 1 to 4 years of experience earn $25,734 to $49,023 per year. Those working for 5 to 9 years receive from $29,420 to $53,028 per annum. Assistant restaurant manager salary ranges from $20,899 to $45,779 per year. The median takes home $33,036 per annum. Normally, assistant restaurant manager with at least 1 year of experience earns $8.10 to $14.04 per hour.
