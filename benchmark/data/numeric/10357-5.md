Restaurant manager salary in USA. Restaurant manager salary for the 10th percentile ranges around $24,000; twenty five percent receives $32,000; the median takes home $40,000; seventy five percent collects $48,000; and ninety percent receives around $56,000. Assistant restaurant manager salary ranges from $20,899 to $45,779 per year. The median takes home $33,036 per annum. Normally, assistant restaurant manager with at least 1 year of experience earns $8.10 to $14.04 per hour.
