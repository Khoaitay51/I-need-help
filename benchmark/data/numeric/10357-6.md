Restaurant manager’s salary is around $40,000 in the United States, but this significantly gets higher as he acquires more experience. In fact, a restaurant manager working for more than 5 years collects an annual income of around $29,420. Assistant restaurant manager salary ranges from $20,899 to $45,779 per year. The median takes home $33,036 per annum. Normally, assistant restaurant manager with at least 1 year of experience earns $8.10 to $14.04 per hour.
