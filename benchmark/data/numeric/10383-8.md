According to Rice University, an average, moderately active adult needs about 0.4 to 0.6 grams of protein per pound of body weight, which equates to a range of 60 to 90 grams daily for a 150-pound person. Athletes need more daily protein, however, especially those doing strength training. You can figure out how much whey to have daily by calculating your protein needs and then estimating the amount of protein you get every day in your regular diet. To do that, it may help to keep a food journal for several days and record everything you eat, then find the amount of protein in your meals and snacks
