The largest pools start at $40,000, but the price can soar much higher, depending on size and options. The cheapest pools are made using a vinyl liner stretched over a wood or metal frame, and cost between $20,000 and $30,000 on average, according to River Pools and Spas. Concrete or gunite pools are the most expensive, with a price tag typically between $20,000 and $45,000. Budget between $1,000 and $3,000 per year for maintenance and electricity for your in-ground pool, suggests BobVila.com.
