When the chops are golden brown on each side, remove them to a shallow backing pan. I like to stand mine up, so the stuffing side is at the top. Evenly pour the melted butter & oil over the tops of the chops. Bake for approximately 35 minutes, to 1 hour, depending on the size of your chops. Salt and pepper chops an … d brown each side in oil. Turn heat down, add 1/2 cup chicken broth, cover and simmer 40 min. Tender and delicious ... and the left over liquid can be used to make gravy.
