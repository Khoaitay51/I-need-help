To cook, put roast on rotisserie rod and insert into rotisserie. Cook about 20 minutes per pound, or until temperature on instant-read meat thermometer reaches 145 F. Let sit 15 minutes before cutting. 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar. 2 T fresh rosemary, finely chopped. 2 T garlic puree.
