National Average. Industrial pharmacists receive a base salary and bonus. The average base salary as of 2010 ranges from $87,399 to $124,949 per year. Average bonuses range from $4,913 to $25,435 per year. The total average compensation ranges from $87,999 to $152,318 per year. An industrial pharmacist participates in the research, development, testing and manufacturing of drugs and often oversees the manufacturing process. In November 2010, PayScale reported average salaries based on 36 individuals working in this occupation.
