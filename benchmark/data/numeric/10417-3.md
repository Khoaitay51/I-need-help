Report Abuse. 18 to 21 days from incubation period. Sometimes the Cockatiel won't start incubating the eggs till she has two or three. So give a few more days. And don't worry if the parents are sitting on the eggs they know what to do. Leave them alone. And don't go into the nest. Some hens may wait until the 3rd egg to begin the incubation process, so all eggs will hatch close to the same time. Generally though, the hen will start incubating the eggs by the time she lays the 2nd egg.
