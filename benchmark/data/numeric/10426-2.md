Cancellations are for a JetBlue flight credit only, valid for a JetBlue flight within 1 year. If you do not change/cancel before scheduled departure and you no show all money for reservation is forfeited. To change or cancel your reservation, visit jetblue.com or call 1-800-JETBLUE (538-2583) in the United States. Fares also do not include applicable fees for baggage fees or optional services. Baggage and Optional Services. For Blue fares, the first checked bag fee is $20 when purchased during web check-in or at a kiosk, or $25 at the check-in counter and the second checked bag is $35. For Blue Plus fares, one checked bag is included and the second checked bag fee is $35. For Blue Flex fares, two checked bags are included
