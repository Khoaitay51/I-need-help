JetBlue announced an increase in change fees today, though it’s not a change that really makes my blood boil. Their change fees used to be $50-100 depending on how expensive the ticket was, though now they’re as follows: Changes and cancellations made 60 days or more prior to departure date: 1  $75 per person fee. Also, I can appreciate the fact that they have different change fees for different fare types within 60 days of departure. Now, as you’ll see the fees are structured in such a way that not a whole lot of value will be left over after the fee, but it’s still better than a flat $200 fee, or whatever.
