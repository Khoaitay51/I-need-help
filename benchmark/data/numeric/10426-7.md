Fares may not be available on all days or on all flights. Certain fares may only be available for new bookings only and/or must be purchased at jetblue.com. A $25 fee per person applies to fares purchased by telephone or at an airport or city ticket office. Fares also do not include applicable fees for baggage fees or optional services. Baggage and Optional Services. For Blue fares, the first checked bag fee is $20 when purchased during web check-in or at a kiosk, or $25 at the check-in counter and the second checked bag is $35. For Blue Plus fares, one checked bag is included and the second checked bag fee is $35. For Blue Flex fares, two checked bags are included
