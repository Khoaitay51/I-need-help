Prior to the change, JetBlue’s fees for changing a ticket were $50 or $100, depending on the cost of the initial flight. So JetBlue is undeniably raising fees. Still, CrankyFlier isn’t the only traveler greeting the fee hike with understanding, if not approval. First, any passenger who changes a JetBlue ticket 60 days or more in advance of departure is charged a flat $75. Secondly, passengers who are changing itineraries within 60 days of departure are assessed fees based on how much the original ticket cost.
