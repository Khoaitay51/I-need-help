Photo courtesy of CarMD. Your car is getting more expensive to fix, at least according to the 2013 CarMD Vehicle Health Index. The study found that for the first time in six years, the average repair cost rose 10 percent to $367.84 on 1996 to 2012 model year vehicles. Replacement part prices went up six percent in 2012, while labor charges rose 17 percent. CarMD reports that replacing the transmission assembly and reprogramming the electronic control module cost an average of $5,474. “Inspect engine oil for correct level and viscosity,” is the least expensive repair, which simply means it’s time to check your oil.
