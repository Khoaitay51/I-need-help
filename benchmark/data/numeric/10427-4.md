The cost, on average, to repair a windshield breaks down as follow by type of vehicle: 1  Passenger vehicle windshield replacement $157 to $324. 2  Sports utility vehicle windshield replacement $164 to $290. 3  Pickup truck windshield replacement $164 to $310. This repair will cost between $20 and $60. A mobile service is around $55. The cost to repair a chip or crack is on a per incident basis-that is you will pay for every crack or chip. The size of the crack will also determine the cost.
