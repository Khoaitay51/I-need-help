Authors of this review recommend you consume no more than 2.5 grams of protein per kilogram, or about 1.1 gram of protein per pound of body weight each day. Therefore, the maximum safe amount of protein for a 50-year-old, 135-pound woman is 149 grams of protein per day. A 50-year-old woman should aim to consume a minimum of 46 grams of protein per day. This amount of protein is the recommended dietary allowance, or RDA, for non-pregnant, non-nursing women, according to the Institute of Medicine
