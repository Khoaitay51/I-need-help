It is situated across the suburbs of Santacruz, Vile Parle and Sahar village in Andheri with the PIN code 400099. Mumbai International Airport Limited, a consortium of GVK Industries Ltd, Airports Company South Africa and Bidvest, was appointed to carry out the modernisation of Mumbai Airport in February 2006. The airport consists of two passenger terminals: Terminal 1 at Santacruz for domestic flights and Terminal 2 at Sahar for international flights. While both terminals use the same airside facilities, they are physically separated on the cityside, requiring a 15–20-minute (airside) drive between them.
