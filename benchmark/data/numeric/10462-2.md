Airport Location. The international terminal is located at Sahar in Andheri East while the domestic terminal is in Santa Cruz, 30 kilometers (19 miles) and 24 kilometers (15 miles) north of the city respectively. Mumbai airport has the following terminals: 1  Terminal 1A-Domestic departures check-in and arrivals (Air India and Alliance Air). 2  Terminal 1B-Domestic departures check-in and arrivals (all other domestic airlines, specifically Jet, IndiGo, Go Air, and Spice Jet).
