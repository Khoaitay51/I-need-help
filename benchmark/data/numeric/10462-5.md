Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji International airport is the unique airport that serves Mumbai, but this airport was created by the fusion of two old airports and some people of Mumbai refer to it as two separate airports. Inter terminal shuttle bus: There is a free shuttle bus that connects both terminals (Domestic and International). The shuttle bus operates 24hours day every 20-30minutes, and the ride time depends on traffic and is variable from 20 to 40 minutes approx.
