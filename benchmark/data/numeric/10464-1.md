OKLAHOMA CITY-Oklahoma drivers will now pay more to renew their license. Renewal fees increase $12 starting Friday. The license renewal fee $was 21.50 and is now $33.50. ID cards jump from $10 to $20 as well. Replacement licenses will are up from $10 to $20. It's all part of Senate Bill 652, which will allow the Department of Public Services to hire 22 new license examiners.
