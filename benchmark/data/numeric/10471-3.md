The incubation period for HPV is weeks or months. Human papillomavirus lives on the skin. Cancer can form only in advanced stages and not in every case. The disease can be transmitted from person to person even when the infected person doesn't have any signs of human papillomavirus. These symptoms are usually either warts on the genitals or abnormal cancer-causing skin growths on the penis or cervical region. It is difficult to determine a precise incubation period because this varies significantly, even among the same virus types.
