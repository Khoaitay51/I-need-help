The irregularity of the HPV incubation period is challenging. People exposed to the virus might need to wait for several years before knowing they are or aren’t contagious. Alternately, when patients show signs of one of these viruses, they may have no way of knowing when they contracted it. These symptoms are usually either warts on the genitals or abnormal cancer-causing skin growths on the penis or cervical region. It is difficult to determine a precise incubation period because this varies significantly, even among the same virus types.
