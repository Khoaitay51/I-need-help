Copies of tax returns, all attachments and W-2 forms are generally available for seven years from the IRS and can be obtained by filing Form 4506, Request for Copy or Transcript of Tax Form. You'll also have to fork over a nominal fee for each return requested. © 2013 CBS Interactive Inc..
