The I-130 petition filing fee ($420 as of early 2014) is subject to change, as USCIS raises its fees on a fairly regular basis. You should always check the USCIS Web page for Form I-130 to confirm the fee before you file. If you are sponsoring more than one family member who qualifies as an “immediate relative,” you will have to file a separate I-130 petition and filing fee for each individual. An “immediate relative” is the spouse or unmarried child under the age of 21 of a U.S. citizen.
