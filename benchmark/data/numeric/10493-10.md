Effective June 1, 2014, the total fees you must pay to file a bankruptcy petition are: 1  $335 for Chapter 7. 2  $310 for Chapter 13, and. 3  $275 for Chapter 12. When you file for bankruptcy, you must pay a filing fee and costs associated with credit counseling. If you can’t afford the filing fee, you might qualify to pay in installments or for a waiver of the entire fee.
