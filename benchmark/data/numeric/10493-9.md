Most attorneys will charge you a flat fee for preparing an I-130 petition. An average flat fee for the entire I-130 process can be anywhere from $500 to $1,500. This amount may be more if you are sponsoring more than one relative or if your relative is filing for adjustment of status at the same time and wishes to have the attorney assist in that process. If you are sponsoring more than one family member who qualifies as an “immediate relative,” you will have to file a separate I-130 petition and filing fee for each individual. An “immediate relative” is the spouse or unmarried child under the age of 21 of a U.S. citizen.
