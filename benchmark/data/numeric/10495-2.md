Salary Outlook. Civil engineers earned a median salary of $80,770 in 2013, according to the BLS. The highest-paid 10 percent in the profession made $126,190, while the lowest-paid earned $51,810 in 2013. The most highly compensated positions are within the industries of oil and gas extraction, and the highest-paid positions are located in Midland, Texas; Hinesville, Georgia; and Houston. Best Paying Cities for Civil Engineers. The highest paid in the civil engineer profession work in the metropolitan areas of Houston, Gary, Indiana, and Waco, Texas. The Midland, Texas area also pays well, as does the city of Hinesville, Georgia.
