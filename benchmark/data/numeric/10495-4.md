Average Salary. The average annual wage for civil engineers working nationwide was $82,710 as of May 2011, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The BLS reported 254,130 civil engineers working nationwide, with the top 10 percent earning six figures, at $119,620 or more. The top-paying industry for civil engineers was commercial and industrial machinery maintenance, which paid $138,560, on average, according to the BLS. The second-best industry in terms of pay was specialty design, which paid $106,150.
