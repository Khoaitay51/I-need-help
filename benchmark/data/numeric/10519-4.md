See your GP if your flatulence is particularly troublesome – for example, if you're frequently passing smelly gas. You should also visit your GP if you experience additional symptoms, such as: 1  persistent abdominal pain and bloating. 2  recurring episodes of diarrhoea or constipation. 3  unexplained weight loss. 4  bowel incontinence. Flatulence is a normal biological process and is something everyone experiences regularly. Some people pass wind only a few times a day, others a lot more, but the average is said to be about 5 to 15 times a day.
