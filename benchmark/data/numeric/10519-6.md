Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) is the most common functional gastrointestinal (GI) disorder. The symptoms come from the colon and include abdominal pain and bloating, as well as disturbances in defecation (the process of having a bowel movement). Symptoms of irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) are abdominal pain, cramping and bloating. In this video, Christine Bouchard, MD, a colorectal surgeon at Chippenham & Johnston-Willis Hospitals, says that both constipation and diarrhea are also symptoms.
