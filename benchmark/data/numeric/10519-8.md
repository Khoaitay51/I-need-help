Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), also known as spastic colon or functional bowel syndrome, is a very common condition, affecting as many as 10 or even 20 percent of adults in the Western world. The goal is to eat 12 grams of fiber per day. Most patients find it difficult to consume this much fiber in food and turn to commercial preparations such as Metamucil®, Citrocel® or Fibercon®) as a fiber supplement. These are usually taken in relatively small quantities, (say, a teaspoon), once to three times daily.
