The Flemish Giant is also called the king of rabbits because of his large size, longevity and personality. These rabbits can reach immense size very quickly. By seven or eight weeks of age, they can already weigh four pounds or more. According to the American Rabbit Breeders Association (ARBA), there is no maximum weight for a Flemish Giant. The breed is a semi-arch variety of rabbit, which means that the arch in the animal's back starts at the base of the shoulders and curves up and over toward the tail.
