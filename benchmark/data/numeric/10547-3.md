Documentation Fees: Dealerships charge car buyers a documentation fee, or doc fee, to cover the cost of preparing and filing the sales contract and other paperwork. In some states, the doc fee is limited by state law. So, if your new car costs $25,000 and you are getting $10,000 for your trade-in, you will only be taxed on the difference, or $15,000. If sales tax in your state is 10 percent, this will save you $1,000.
