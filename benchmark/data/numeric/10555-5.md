As you can imagine, it costs a lot of money to fix a head gasket leak because the mechanic is going to have to remove the head off the top of the engine, which will take a lot of labor hours. A repair like this can easily cost between $1,000 and $2,000. Another common source of a leak is the radiator. Leaks can form on several different places on the radiator, from cracks in the tanks to tubes that leak due to damage or corrosion. Average cost to replace a radiator is $400 to $600. Radiator caps can fail and leak, but that’s definitely a do-it-yourself repair
