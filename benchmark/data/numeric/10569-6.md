The cost of NeoGraft® treatment begins at $10,000, although your cost may differ slightly depending on the size of your treatment area, and whether you choose to undergo follow-up sessions to increase the density or fullness of your hair. NeoGraft® is an FDA-approved hair restoration system that utilizes follicular-unit extraction (FUE) technology. During FUE, Dr. Greenberg uses a pneumatic pressure wand to swiftly and painlessly remove hairs and their follicles from your scalp using suction.
