According to the 2006 census, the average salary for a paralegal in Canada was $35,255, with the highest average salaries earned in Edmonton, Alberta, and the lowest in Saint John, New Brunswick. The British Columbia Paralegal Association (BCPA) reports that the salary range in British Columbia for a paralegal who has been employed for at least one full-time year ranges from $30,000-$90,000.
