1 boneless leg of lamb (about 4 to 5 lbs) 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary 2 tablespoons fresh thyme 2 tablespoons fresh oregano 4 cloves garlic Olive oil Salt & pepper. Preheat the oven to 450ºF. While the oven is heating, finely chop the herbs and garlic or use a food processor like we did. Bake 350 degrees F for 2 1/2 hours. Remove roast from roast pan into a platter deep enough to catch about 1-2 cups of juices.Do not cover or roast crust won’t be crispy. Rest meat 20 minutes drain juices back to roast pan. Add 3 cups water to pan.
