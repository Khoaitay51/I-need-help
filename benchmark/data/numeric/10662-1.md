At a cooking temperature between 300 and 350 degrees F it takes a turkey around 20 minutes per pound to cook. A 15 pound turkey should take around 5 hours to cook. When the internal temperature of the turkey reaches 160 to 165 degree F it is time to get it off the heat. This will determine the amount of fuel needed and for how long the grill will need to burn. Just as a general guideline and calculated on the safe side, a 12 pound/5.5 kg turkey will take 4 hours and a 15 pound/7 kg turkey will take 5 hours. I do not recommend cooking a turkey larger than 18 pounds/8 kg on a rotisserie.
