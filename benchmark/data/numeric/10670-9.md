Application to PA school is highly competitive. Look into PA programs you want to apply to as early as your freshman year in college. You’ll typically need to complete at least two years of college coursework in basic and behavioral sciences before applying to a PA program, which is very similar to premedical studies. The majority of PA programs have the following prerequisites: A physician assistant (or PA) is a nationally certified and state-licensed medical professional. PAs practice medicine on healthcare teams with physicians and other providers.
