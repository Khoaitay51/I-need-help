20 drops of water makes about 1 milliliter. And a teaspoon can hold about five milliliters: 1 teaspoon of liquid is about 5 milliliters. Milliliters are often written as ml (for short), so 100 ml means 100 milliliters. Here we have 150 ml of milk in a measuring cup. It doesn't say 150 ... it says 50 ... but it is half-way between 100 and 200 so you can figure out it is 150 ml.
