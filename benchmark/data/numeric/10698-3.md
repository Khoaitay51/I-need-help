All dogs should start on regular heartworm prevention medicine when they are puppies. Most heartworm prevention medicines are safe for puppies as young as 6-8 weeks old. If you give your dog his heartworm “prevention” on April 1, and he gets bitten by a mosquito on April 2, he can get infected with baby heartworms.
