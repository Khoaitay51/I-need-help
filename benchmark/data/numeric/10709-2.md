The table below details the hourly wages for Dental Hygienists and Dental Therapists. The Canadian national occupation classification code (NOC) for this role is 3222. Job titles and exact duties vary in this occupation and examples of some job titles are: dental hygienist, dental nurse and dental therapist. A typical full-time annual salary for this occupation is in the region of $60,000 – $70,000. According to the latest figures, the highest hourly average (median) wages are earned in Edmonton and Calgary, Alberta at $50 per hour and the lowest average (median) wages are earned in Montreal, Quebec at $30 per hour
