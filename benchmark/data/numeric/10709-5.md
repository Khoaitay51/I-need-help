Money: According to the Canadian Dental Hygienists Association, which conducts salary surveys for the profession (the last one was completed in 2013), the average hourly rate for dental hygienists across Canada is about $40.18. Salaries differ according to experience and location as well. Respondents to the CDHA survey who were under the age of 30 had an average hourly rate of $36.57, while more experienced dental hygienists made an average of $42.96 per hour.
