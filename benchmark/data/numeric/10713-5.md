The degree obtained by physiotherapist is another factor that affects their salary. Those who have a bachelor’s degree in Kinesiology and Physical Therapy have an annual salary of $54,080 to $71,239 and $50,684 to $89,104, respectively. New physiotherapist earns $49,290 to $70,514. With more experience comes a bigger pay, with one to four years of experience rewarded with an increase to an annual salary of $50,925 to $77,048.
