By under roasting you save water weight and by saving water weight coffee roasting companies can save thousands and thousands of dollars each year. It’s more expensive to roast our way because we lose up to 18% of the coffee’s weight during the roasting process compared to 14%-15%. The goal here is to gently bring each coffee up to it’s full potential, through personal attention to the characteristics of each coffee.
