Green bean lots are graded in size to prevent uneven roasting. Arabica beans in the large-size range are roughly 10.3 mm long, 7.3 mm wide and 4 mm high. In the smallest commercial-size range, Arabica bean dimensions are roughly three quarters as large. Length/width ratios for Robustas are smaller than for Arabicas. A Q, A D and A P vary with the type of roaster used, its associated quench time (15 to 30 s), preheat and charge time (30 to 90 s) and bean discharge time (5 to 40 s) and with bean-charge-and discharge-port size.
