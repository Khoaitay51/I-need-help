I am sure everyone has heard the “magic number”: 60mg is the nicotine lethal dose in adults. τ his is a very low level, which would categorize nicotine as one of the most toxic substances available. I always wondered how the lethal dose level was defined, because everyone mentioned that the lethal dose was just theoretical and was never really tested. In animals the lethal dose may be from 3mg/kg in mice to 50mg/kg in rats, while a 60mg dose means a 0.8mg/kg in humans.
