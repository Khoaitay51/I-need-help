It’s better to keep flowerhorn fish alone as a rare show fish. It is rather territory-dependent fish with aggressive temper and rather bad behaved as for its tank mates (except for big tanks of about 175 gallons capacity). If the fish is kept alone then minimal tank capacity should be 70 gallons, but the larger the better. If a couple of fish is kept in a tank, its capacity should be 100-10 gallons, if flowerhorn tank mates are other cichlids – 175-200 gal capacity tank is required.
