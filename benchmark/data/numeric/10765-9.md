Water Quantity. Although several factors may affect the amount of water a dog should drink daily, you can estimate a dog’s water need as 1 ounce of water per pound of body weight. Other guidelines approximate water intake to equate to two and half times the amount of food intake. The amount of water a dog should drink in a day varies by body weight and factors such as weather and the dog's health and activity level. Always provide easily accessible fresh water, in clean bowls, for your pets
