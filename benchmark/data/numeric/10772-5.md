Bankrate recommends keeping copies of credit card bills for 45 days to 7 years. This will help to account for any major expenses as well as any claimed tax deductions for any period of time in which the IRS could audit. With the implementation of electronic statements used by most credit card companies, consumers can call up any credit card bill at any point in time, should they need to review it. This could negate the need to keep paper copies of bills in the future.
