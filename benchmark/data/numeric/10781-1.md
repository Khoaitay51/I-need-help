What are the extra costs? 1  If the notary has to come to your location, a delivery charge will more than likely apply. 2  Like the document fees, many states have laws that forbid the notary from charging too much “per mile.”. 3  If copies have to be made, this can be anywhere from $0.10 to $0.30 per copy.
