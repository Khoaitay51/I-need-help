We tried PETCO’S dog training a couple years ago for $90.00 in Virginia. But it really wasn’t helpful for us at all. Our Jack Russle terrier mix (from pound) was attacked as a puppy and suffered other dog aggression/ fear. Teresa V. Before you go to Petco and do training there I would look around where you live and see if there are any other options. I am sure the dogs get some training at Petco but I have heard from a lot of people that they don’t do a very good job there and that the dogs that they brought there didn’t learn anything.
