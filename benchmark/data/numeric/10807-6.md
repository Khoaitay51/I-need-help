Dog Training Cost. 1  Group classes at a local community center, dog daycare or pet store usually cost from $40 to $125 or more for four to eight weekly one-hour sessions. 1 Training can be done at home using a book. 2  Amazon has a selection of dog training books starting under $10. 3  Some shelters offer low-cost training, starting at about $35 and up for several weeks of classes.
