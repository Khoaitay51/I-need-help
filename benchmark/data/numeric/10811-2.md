You can speed up the ripening process by putting your avocados in a brown paper bag — placing an apple in the bag will help hurry things along even more. This will usually cut down the ripening time to 1 to 3 days. (3) Store fully ripe avocados in the refrigerator. If you've got fully ripe avocados that you do not want to use right away, place them in the fridge in a plastic bag.
