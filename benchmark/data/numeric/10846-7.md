Dominican Republic: Salary dispute escalates (12 March 2013). Faced with the government’s failure to live up to its promises to teachers, EI’s affiliate, Asociacion Asociación dominicana De profesores Y (PROFESORAS), adp is preparing to step up its fight for decent working conditions For dominican. teachers Instead, it has proposed a wholly inadequate increase of 20 per cent. Salary strains. The basic salary for teachers in the Dominican Republic is US$219 per month. The cost of the basic monthly shopping basket, however, as defined by the Central Bank for the poorest in the country, is US$292.68.
