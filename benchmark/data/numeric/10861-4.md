Vaccination is the best way to prevent pneumococcal infection. Pneumococcal Vaccine Schedule. Children should receive 4 doses of PCV13. The first dose should be given at 2 months, the second dose at 4 months, the third dose at 6 months, and the fourth dose at 12-15 months. Adults age 65 years and older should receive the two types of pneumococcal vaccines (PCV13 and PPSV23). You should receive a dose of PCV13 first, followed by a dose of the PPSV23, ideally 6 to 12 months later
