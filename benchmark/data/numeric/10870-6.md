While the USPS ® rate for a First-Class letter is 49-cents ($0.49), metered clients receive a $0.485 rate. Note: This rate is effective as of May 31, 2015. The USPS discount is on all First-Class letters up to 3.5 ounces for metered mail. The $0.485 cent rate is automatically applied when you use a Postage Meter, PC Postage or Permit Imprint.
