Today, premium volume is $14.3 billion and growing. Yet we carry the goodwill of GEICO on our books at only $1.4 billion, an amount that will remain unchanged no matter how much the value of GEICO increases. In 1996, Berkshire purchased the 50 percent of GEICO that it did not already own for $2.3 billion implying a value of $4.6 billion for 100 percent of GEICO. At that time, GEICO had tangible net worth of $1.9 billion which means that Berkshire valued GEICO’s goodwill at $2.7 billion.
