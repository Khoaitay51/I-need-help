Best Answer: I agree with everyone else that it depends on the type of insurance you are selling. For instance, I know of an agent that owns her own business and makes upwards of $700-$900k gross per year. If you are starting out, I would not expect to make any more than $35k your first couple of years. Another example is one of my best friends that started his own independedn agency and made a whopping $9,000 his first year because of overhead etc. The insurance industry varies too much to give a solid dollar figure.
