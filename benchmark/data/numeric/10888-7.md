Stuffed pork tenderloin To butterfly the tenderloin, trim the silver skin with a thin sharp knife. Grilling pork tenderloin Always cook the tenderloin over indirect heat. Depending on its size, it usually takes 20 to 30 minutes over medium-high heat. Flip the tenderloin every 4 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 135ºF to 140ºF.
