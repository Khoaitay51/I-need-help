The optimum space for chickens is 3 to 4 square feet per bird. Chickens like to move around so the more space you provide the happier the hens. If you keep too many in a small enclosure they will pick at each other and fight. As a rule of thumb, the more room your chickens have then happier and more healthier they will be. Another factor to consider is the actual size of the bird. For exam … ple Bantams, which are smaller, don't need as much space, as say a larger breed of chicken.
