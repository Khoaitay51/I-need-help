The short answer would be that six chickens would need a coop that’s at least 18 square foot and a run which is at least 90 square foot. So in total that would mean you’d need just under 110 square foot to keep six chickens. This is where your chickens will sleep and you need to make sure that each chicken has 10 inches of perching space. So in our example you would need a roosting pole which is 60 inches long (5 foot).
