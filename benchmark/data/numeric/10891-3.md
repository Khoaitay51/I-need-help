If you can be claimed as a dependent by another taxpayer, your standard deduction for 2014 is limited to the greater of: (1) $1,000, or (2) your earned income plus $350 (but the total cannot be more than the basic standard deduction for your filing status). In general, the standard deduction is adjusted each year for inflation and varies according to your filing status, whether you are 65 or older and/or blind, and whether another taxpayer can claim you as a dependent. The standard deduction is not available to certain taxpayers.
