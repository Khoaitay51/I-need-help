Punta del Este: Annual Weather Averages. January is the hottest month in Punta del Este with an average temperature of 23°C (73°F) and the coldest is July at 10°C (51°F) with the most daily sunshine hours at 11 in December. The wettest month is August with an average of 94mm of rain. The best month to swim in the sea is in February when the average sea temperature is 23°C (73°F).
