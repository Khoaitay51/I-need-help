Nefertiti Biography. Born: c. 1390 B.C.E. Thebes, Egypt Died: c. 1360 B.C.E. Egypt Egyptian queen. Nefertiti was an Egyptian queen and wife of King Akhenaten who remains a mystery to scholars today. A bust (sculpture of a person's head and shoulders) of her discovered in 1913 is one of the most widely recognized symbols of ancient Egypt. Few facts known. Nefertiti was born around 1390 B.C.E. As queen. When Nefertiti was fifteen years old, she married Amenhotep IV, who was a year older and became king upon his father's death. They had six daughters and, according to some, one son. During the first five years of Amenhotep's reign, Nefertiti enjoyed a high profile.
