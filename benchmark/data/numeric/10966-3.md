Helping hand: Maternity pay means working mums can take time off to care for their newborn-but it's not much and it is taxed. You are entitled to up to 39 weeks of statutory maternity pay. The first six weeks of this is paid at 90% of your salary, after which you get £128.73 per week. Other conditions are that you were employed or self-employed for at least 26 of the 66 weeks before the week your baby was due and you earned an average of £30 over any 13 of those 66 weeks. The standard rate of Maternity Allowance is £128.73 or 90% of your average weekly earnings, whichever is less.
