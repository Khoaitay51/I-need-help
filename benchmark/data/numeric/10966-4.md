Statutory Maternity Pay (. ) is paid for up to 39 weeks. You get: 1  90% of your average weekly earnings (before tax) for the first 6 weeks. 2  £139.58 or 90% of your average weekly earnings (whichever is lower) for the next 33 weeks. is paid in the same way as your wages (eg monthly or weekly). Tax and National Insurance will be deducted. Use the maternity pay calculator to work out how much you could get. If you take Shared Parental Leave you’ll get Statutory Shared Parental Pay (ShPP). ShPP is £139.58 a week or 90% of your average weekly earnings, whichever is lower
