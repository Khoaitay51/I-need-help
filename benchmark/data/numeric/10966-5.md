Current United States maternity leave policy is directed by the Family and Medical Leave Act of 1993 (FMLA) which includes a provision mandating 12 weeks of unpaid leave annually for mothers of newborn or newly adopted children. The US doesn't even compare to countries like Albania, which has 12 months paid leave. Some other countries we recognize that offer paid maternity leave are Mexico with 12 weeks, United Kingdom with 40 weeks, Canada with 1 year, and China with 14 weeks.
