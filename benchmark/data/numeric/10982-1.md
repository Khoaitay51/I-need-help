The speed of light (and thus radio waves) is 3x10^8 m per second. So if the planets are 401million km apart, that makes it 1337 seconds, or just over 20 minutes. And if the planets are closest together, light would take 183 seconds, slightly more than 3 minutes. At its closest Mars is about 35million miles away. At its most distant it is about 230 million miles away. The speed of radio waves is 186000 miles per second so at its furthest the radio waves will take about 20minutes to get there.
