How long will it take for a radio wave to travel 100km if the speed of radio waves is 3x108metres per second? If the speed of radio waves, also known as the speed of light, is 3x108 metres per second, then it is also 3.333 nanoseconds per meter. (Simply invert) As such, the wave would … take 333.3 microseconds to traverse 100 kilometres. How long does it take a radio message to travel to the moon from the earth? It's roughly 1-1.5 seconds. Light (and radio) travel at 186,000 miles per second, and the moon is usually about 230,000 miles away.
