At the furthest, Venus and Earth are 257 million kilometers from each other. At the speed of light, that distance can be traversed in slightly over 14 minutes. Venus has an orbit of about 108 million kilometers (67 million miles) from the Sun. The closest approach of about 39.5 million kilometers (23.6 million miles) would be with Venus at its farthest from the Sun and Earth at its closest.
