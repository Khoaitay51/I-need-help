The Russian Provisional Government was overthrown on October 25, 1917. This date is according to the Julian calendar in use in Russia at that time. Under the Gregorian calenda … r, which the western nations used, it was November 7, 1917. The overthrow was known as the October or Bolshevik or Communist Revolution. The provisional government lost the support of many Russians  because it did not allocate land to the poor peasants, and the  decision to keep Russia in World War One.
