A study by MailSport found that (spoiler alert) it pays to be in the Premier League. The study examined salaries from 34 leagues from around the world and found that the average EPL salary is about £700 ,000 ($1,000,000+) more than the next-highest league. Players in the EPL make £2 ,273,277 per year ($3,562,111)
