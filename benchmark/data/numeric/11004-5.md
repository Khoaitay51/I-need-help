Since salaried employees earn a specified annual amount, it is likely that their gross pay for each pay period is the same recurring amount. For example, if a manager's salary is $48,000 per year and salaries are paid semi-monthly, the manager's gross pay will be $2,000 for each of the 24 pay periods. The pay for these employees is generally stated as a gross, hourly rate, such as $13.52 per hour.. Again, the gross amount refers to the pay an employee would receive before withholdings are made for such things as taxes, contributions, and savings plans.
