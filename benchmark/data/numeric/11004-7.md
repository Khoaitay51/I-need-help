The Department of Labor enforces the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) , which sets basic minimum wage and overtime pay standards. These standards are enforced by the Department's Wage and Hour Division. Minimum Wage. 1  Current Minimum Wage: $7.25 per hour. 2  Applies to workers covered by the FLSA. 3  Effective as of July 24, 2009. 4  Overtime Pay: Not less than one and one-half times regular rate of pay. 5  Required after 40 hours of work in a workweek.
