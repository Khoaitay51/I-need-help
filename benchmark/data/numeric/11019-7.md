Slab Thicknesses and Weight. Granite slabs are typically either 3/4 inch or 1 1/4 inches thick. The thicker slabs usually weigh approximately 19 pounds per square foot, while the thinner slabs weigh less than 13 pounds per square foot. The typical surface area of a slab is about 45 square feet, but because there will be some waste during the process of cutting the slab into a counter top, the usable yield of a slab will be much less than that. Consequently, large kitchen counter projects will often require more than one slab.
