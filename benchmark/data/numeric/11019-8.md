Slab size. When you choose your stone, you'll want to know how many slabs you need and what thickness of stone to buy. An average granite slab size is about 9 to 10 feet wide and 5 to 6 feet tall (slabs are displayed vertically). The size of the slabs can affect how many seams end up in your kitchen counters and where they must be placed. Patterned granite can be fragile. The thickness of granite is usually 2CM (3/4) or 3CM (1 1/4).
