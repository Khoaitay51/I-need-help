TJX Cos. Inc., owner of the T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods stores, said Wednesday that it would raise its minimum wage to $9 an hour in June and to $10 next year. TJX has 21 locations — most of them Marshalls and T.J. Maxx stores — in the St. Louis area. The announcement comes less than a week after Wal-Mart, the world’s largest retailer, announced that it was giving half a million U.S. workers a raise, bringing its minimum hourly pay to $9 in 2015 and $10 next year.
