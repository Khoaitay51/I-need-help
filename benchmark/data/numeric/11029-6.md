For comparison's sake, a simple paling fence might cost around $65 per linear metre if installed by a professional. Many homeowners who have compared the cost of materials versus supply and installation of Colorbond fencing report that installation costs about $1500 more than the cost of materials alone. Your cost difference can vary considerably from that figure, though. Before you commit to DIY installation, be sure you are able to do the job. If you buy your Colorbond fencing and then decide to have it installed, you will probably pay around $50 per hour for installation. Your materials, too, will probably cost more than they would if you chose a Colorbond fencing supplier/installer.
