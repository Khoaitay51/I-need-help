Glass Fence Price Adelaide. Glass fencing prices are going to be a lot more expensive than other fencing types. For frameless glass prices, the cost can start at $110 a lineal metre and continue upwards depending on how much work is involved and the size of the fencing job. The cost of good neighbour fences are around $80 per metre. This can vary a little bit either way, but usually no more than $10. Competition amongst fence builders is quite high, so prices are fairly tight between fencing contractors.
