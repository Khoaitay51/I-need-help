Our Financial Aid office has a budget of $110 million for student aid in the 2014-2015 academic year. The average University of Chicago aid applicant receives $37,500 in scholarships each year. Aid goes to a wide diversity of students. For the incoming class of 2015, 93% of lower-and middle-income families who applied for financial aid received support from the university. In total, UChicago pledges to meet 100% of demonstrated need for every undergraduate on campus. Family income below $80K.
