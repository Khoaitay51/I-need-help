54 percent of students received aid in the form of Pell Grants from the U.S. Federal Government. The average Pell Grant awarded for 2013/2014 was $4,547. To apply for a Pell Grant to attend University of Illinois at Chicago, the first step is to fill out the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). The net out-of-pocket total cost you end up paying or financing though student loans is known as the net price. The reported UIC net price for in-state students $11,645* for the 2013/2014 academic year.
