Deep ocean water makes up about 90% of the volume of the oceans. Deep ocean water has a very low temperature, typically from 0-3 °C (32-38 °F), and a salinity of about 3.5% or as oceanographers state as 35 ppt (parts per thousand). When deep ocean water is brought to the surface, it can be used for a variety of things. Its most useful property is its temperature. At the surface of the Earth, most water and air is well above 3 °C.
