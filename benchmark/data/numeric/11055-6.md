On June 28, 1919, Germany signed the Treaty of Versailles, officially ending its involvement in World War I. Germany was unable to negotiate any changes with the Allied Powers, who threatened to resume hostilities if the treaty was not promptly signed. (Macrohistory and World Report). The treaty was signed on June 28, 1919, in the Hall of Mirrors in Versailles, France. Two German representatives were the first to sign the Treaty of Versailles, followed by representatives of many of the Allied Powers. (MSN Encarta, EyeWitness to History)
