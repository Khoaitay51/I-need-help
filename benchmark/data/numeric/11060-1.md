A room that is up to 75 square feet should have a fan size of 29 to 36 inches. Room dimensions of 76 to 144 square feet need a fan size of 36 to 42 inches. Rooms that are 144 to 225 square feet will require a 44 inch fan. A room with square footage of 225 to 400 square feet will need a fan that is 50 to 54 inches. A ceiling fan is a money-saving investment as well as a way to cool a room. Many fans have a reverse switch that is used during cooler days to push the warm air down from the ceiling and circulate the warm air.
