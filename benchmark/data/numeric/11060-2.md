Ceiling Fans. If you plan on installing a light fixture with a ceiling fan attached, the size of the room will also be a determining factor in choosing a fan with the appropriate blade dimensions. Ceiling fan blades range from 29 inches to 54 inches. The American Lighting Association recommends the following guidelines for room and ceiling fan size: a room with up to 75 square feet can accommodate fans in the 29 to 36-inch range. So if your room is 10 feet by 12 feet, the ceiling fixture should be 22 inches wide. However, if you are installing a chandelier or pendant over a dining room table, you must measure the width of the table.
