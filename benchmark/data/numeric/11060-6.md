Fans should be at least 7 feet from the floor and 18 inches from any wall. If a room has a ceiling height higher than 8 feet, then it is preferable to mount the fan 8 to 9 feet from the floor to allow for optimal air circulation. A standard mount comes with a rod that is 3 to 5 inches in length. A ceiling fan is a money-saving investment as well as a way to cool a room. Many fans have a reverse switch that is used during cooler days to push the warm air down from the ceiling and circulate the warm air.
