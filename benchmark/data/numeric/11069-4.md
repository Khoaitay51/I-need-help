Preheat the oven to 500 degrees, rub roast with seasonings you like (salt, pepper, garlic powder, etc). Roast at 500 degrees for 15 minutes, then reduce heat to 350 degrees and cook about 20 minutes per pound for medium. Let roast sit (cover with a tent of foil) for 15 minutes before carving. You don't have to make a rack-I cook mine in a cast iron pan and sit it right on the pan. Put oven on at 500, place roast in pan and rub oil on roast, add seasonings. Oil will keep the roast juicy. Bake for 15 minutes, then reduce temperature to 350 for 20 minutes per pound.
