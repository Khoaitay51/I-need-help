According to wedding statistics released in 2014 by The Knot, the price brides are willing to pay for their gowns has gone up. The 2013 national average spent was $1,281, and the year before it was $1,211. Apart from venue, photographer, and planner, the wedding dress was one of the costliest items of the whole event. I think roughly 5-10% of the cost of the wedding. So if you're spending $20,000 total, then the dress should cost $1000-$2000 range. For me, my budget for dress, shoes, jewelry, veil, + alterations is $2000 and I'm okay with that..
