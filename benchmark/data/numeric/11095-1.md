There you will find out how to actually lay brick and blocks onto a solid foundation. The basic rules governing the building of a garden retaining walls are simple. Firstly the foundation you place should be a minimum of 150mm (6 inches) thick. When you have measured how deep the garden will be you simply divide it by 4 to get the required thickness of wall. If your retaining wall is holding back a 1m depth of garden then it needs to be 225mm thick. The minimum thickness of a garden retaining wall should be the width of a standard brick @ 112mm.
