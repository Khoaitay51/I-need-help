For a complete Turn-Key Home, our current average building costs in Summit County can run between $250-$325 per square foot dependent on the finishes you choose, complexity of roof system, and development cost of the lot including permits, utilities, driveway access, retaining walls and excavation. The homeowner is responsible for the cost of the freight to the building site. A 2500 sf home should be 2-3 trucks. Most recent freight bill from Rocky Mountain Log Homes from Hamilton MT was approx. $2800 per truck for freight. Freight approx. $5000 per truck from Canada.
