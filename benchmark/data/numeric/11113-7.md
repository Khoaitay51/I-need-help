A kilometre (American spelling: kilometer, symbol: km) is a unit of length equal to 1000 metres (from the Greek words khilia = thousand and metro = count/measure). It is approximately equal to 0.621 miles, 1094 yards or 3281 feet. 1 metre is equal to 0.000621371192237 miles, or 0.001 km. Note that rounding errors may occur, so always check the results.
