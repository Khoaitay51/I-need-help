PayScale.com reported in January 2014 that forensic scientists with less than a year of experience made $42,500-$66,500. Those with 1-4 years of experience made $30,913-$77,083. Salaries were $34,905-$79,227 for those with 5-9 years of experience and $45,171-$96,190 for those with 10-19 years of experience. As of May 2012, the BLS reported that the median salary for forensic science technicians was $52,840 annually, and the average salary was $55,730.
