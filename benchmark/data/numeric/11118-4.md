Over time, a forensic scientist can build experience which increases ones pay. Some forensic scientists who have been in the business for 5 years see their salaries double from $1500 to $3000 per month just because of the experience factor. Other forensic scientists eventually earn salaries that are between $35,000 and $50,000 per year. Determining ones pay usually involves several different factors. Location, type of facility, experience and certification all play parts in ones annual income. Another factor is what type of forensic scientist you are. Toxicologists can earn starting salaries up to $55,000 while a Forensic Quality Manager can earn up to $93,000.
