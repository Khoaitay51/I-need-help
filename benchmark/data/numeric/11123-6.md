Unfortunately unpaid tax liens remain on credit reports indefinitely, but my understanding is that if you discharge the debt it should be off your reports since it is more than 7 years old. Certain taxes and student loans may be discharged in bankruptcy. And the IRS can file a lien quickly, in as little as ten days after you are notified that you owe taxes. So if you get a notice that you owe Uncle Sam money, resolve it quickly before you end up with serious damage to your credit report! If tax liens have already been filed, resolve them as quickly as you can.
