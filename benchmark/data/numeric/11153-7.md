250 is a great price for both front brake pads and rotors. I have a different model of car, (honda accord) but I paid 350 when I had mine replaced a couple of months ago. The rotors are the most expensive parts I think. Parts are around $90 (low end) rotors / pads labor should run another $90 Parts markup varies but 225 -250 sounds about right for a retail repair. Frankly if you bring in your own parts the warranty issues are on you, pads wear out in three months (oh yeah raymold, valuecraft etc pads can wear that fast) its all on you.
