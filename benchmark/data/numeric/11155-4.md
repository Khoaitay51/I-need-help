For those women, a breast MRI is especially likely to be more accurate than a mammogram, and they are safer than mammograms. Breast MRIs are more expensive than mammograms, costing an average of $2,000 (compared to about $100 for a mammogram). Unfortunately, younger women tend to have denser breasts, which often look white on a mammogram. Since cancer also shows up as white, mammograms are less accurate for younger women (and other women with dense breasts).
