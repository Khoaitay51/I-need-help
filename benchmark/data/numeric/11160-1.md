The amount of carbohydrates a woman should consume each day depends on her total daily calorie intake. Despite carbohydrates’ bad reputation in many commercial weight loss diets, carbohydrates are your body’s main fuel source. Overweight and obese women trying to lose weight often have success consuming 1,000 to 1,600 calories per day, according to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. According to the Institute of Medicine, adult women should consume 45 to 65 percent of their daily calories from carbohydrates.
