If you are an active woman between the ages of 19 and 30, you should consume 2,400 calories and 270 to 390 grams of carbohydrates per day. Women between the ages of 31 and 50 need slightly less, requiring 2,200 calories and 247.5 and 357.5 grams of carbohydrates each day. Moderately Active. Moderately active women between the ages of 19 and 30 should try to eat 2,000 to 2,200 calories per day. As carbohydrates should account for 900 to 1,430 of these calories, you should eat 225 to 357.5 grams of carbs each day.
