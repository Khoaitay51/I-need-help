With Marconi having invented Radio, John Logie Baird pioneered visual communication... it was the next step up. John Logie Baird (August 13, 1888 - June 14, 1946) was a Scottish engineer and inventor of the world's first working television system in Hastings, England in 1923. John Logie Baird, a Scotsman, invented the first fully working TV system in the United Kingdom. It was first demonstrated in 1925 in London and was the first system to be used … for public scheduled broadcasts in 1929. The system was electro-mechanical and used a rotating disc to deliver a picture.
