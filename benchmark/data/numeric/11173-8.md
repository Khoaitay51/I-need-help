With Marconi having invented Radio, John Logie Baird pioneered visual communication... it was the next step up. John Logie Baird (August 13, 1888 - June 14, 1946) was a Scottish engineer and inventor of the world's first working television system in Hastings, England in 1923. Television was not invented in Australia,1956 Television was first introduced in Australia.   ______   The first television broadcast in Australia was by TCN-9 in Sydney … on the 16th of September, 1956, with announcer, Bruce Gyngell, introducing TV to the audience.
