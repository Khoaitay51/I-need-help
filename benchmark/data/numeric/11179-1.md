Per diem is a fixed amount of daily reimbursement paid to employees for lodging, meals and incidental expenses incurred while traveling on business, paid in lieu of actual travel expenses. Per diem rates vary by destination, and in some cases, by when you travel, based on the area’s cost of living. The M&IE per diem is shown on the right side of the table; these rates remain static during the entire fiscal year. Note that a standard rate of $46/day for meals and incidental expenses, and $83/day for lodging, applies to any location that doesn’t have a specified rate in the GSA tables.
