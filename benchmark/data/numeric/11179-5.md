The per diem rate for meals in major cities, such as Chicago, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, New York and the District of Columbia, qualify for the maximum tier of $71 per day. The M&IE per diem is shown on the right side of the table; these rates remain static during the entire fiscal year. Note that a standard rate of $46/day for meals and incidental expenses, and $83/day for lodging, applies to any location that doesn’t have a specified rate in the GSA tables.
