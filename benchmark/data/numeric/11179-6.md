Six Per Diem Tiers. As of March 2013, the six meals and incidental expenses per diem tiers are $46, $51, $56, $61, $66 or $71 per day. Major U.S. cities such as Chicago, New York and Los Angeles fall within the maximum per diem tier of $71 per day. Per Diem Breakdown. The GSA breaks down business trip per diem rates into two categories: The first is lodging and the second is meals and incidental expenses.. The incidental part of the meals and incidental expenses per diem is $5 per day no matter where you travel.
