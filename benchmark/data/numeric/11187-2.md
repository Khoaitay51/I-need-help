According to 2014 estimates from Homewyse, midgrade ceiling drywall cost between $0.38 to $0.45 per square foot, and labor costs for ceiling drywall replacement ran about $72 to $75 per hour for what is often a job that takes more than eight hours and involves cutting old drywall out and replacing it with new panels. Impacts and settling may lead to a cracked ceiling, which calls for stable patching to restore the consistency of the ceiling's surface and prevent further chipping or damage. In 2014, the construction estimate aggregator Redbeacon placed the typical price of patching a ceiling at $125 to $325.
