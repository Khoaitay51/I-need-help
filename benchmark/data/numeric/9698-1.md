In order to obtain license as an LPN, you need to complete an accredited LPN program and take the licensure examination for LPNs. Working as an LPN is one of the first steps if you want to work in the future as a registered nurse. If there is no way that you can attend classes for an LPN program during the weekends or evenings, you can still pursue a career as an LPN through online LPN programs. These are online classes provided by nursing schools as an alternative to the conventional LPN programs.
