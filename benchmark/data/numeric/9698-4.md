Some community colleges and nursing schools also offer classes in the evenings for LPN programs. This LPN program length is ideal for those who are working during the day in their full-time jobs. LPN students may also opt to study during the weekend. If there is no way that you can attend classes for an LPN program during the weekends or evenings, you can still pursue a career as an LPN through online LPN programs. These are online classes provided by nursing schools as an alternative to the conventional LPN programs.
