Ground beef is safe indefinitely if kept frozen, but will lose quality over time. It is best if used within 4 months. Mark your packages with the date they were placed in the freezer so you can keep track of storage times.. (http://www.fsis.usda.gov/Fact_Sheets/ground_beef_and_food_safety/index.asp). Beef can be frozen for 1 year, and even longer if stored in a deep freeze (0 degrees or lower) A deep freeze is a freezer with no automatic defroster.
