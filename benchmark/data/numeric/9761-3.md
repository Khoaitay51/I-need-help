Note that the initial cost of pet insurance will dramatically increase with the age of the pet. So what started out at $25/month for my cat at the age of 1, ended up $70/mo at the age of 15. She lived until 19. If you think a reasonable expectation of vet bills for a dog (not a cat) is $300 a year…you better not get a dog. Pet insurance is 100% worth the cost in the first 8 years of a large dog’s life. The type of pet matters.
