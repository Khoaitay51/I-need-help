An average surface fire on the forest floor might have flames reaching 1 metre in height and can reach temperatures of 800°C (1,472°F) or more. Under extreme conditions a fire can give off 10,000 kilowatts or more per metre of fire front. This would mean flame heights of 50 metres or more and flame temperatures exceeding 1200°C (2,192°F). The flash point, or the temperature at which wood will burst into flame, is 572°F, according to HowStuffWorks. Photo by Bill Gabbert.
