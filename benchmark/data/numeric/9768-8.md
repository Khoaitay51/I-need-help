The dinosaurs didn't suddenly appear on the earth. Animals evolved over millions of years to the point where they took the forms we are familiar with now as dinosaurs. The fossil record is clear in this. Smaller, less sophisticated creatures gave way to larger and more complex critters. Some 230 million years ago is the general time that paleontologists consider the dinosaur first appeared. Dinosaurs first appeared during the Triassic period, around 280 MYA (Million Years Ago), and died out around 65.5 MYA. They evolved from Archosaurs, or Ruling Reptiles.
