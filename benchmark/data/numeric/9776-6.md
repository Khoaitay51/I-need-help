Washi tape is reusable, biodegradable, tree free and made out of highly renewable resources. The adhesive can be silicon, rubber or acrylic depending on the brand. Just like masking tape it does come off...unlike duct tape it does not leave a sticky mess. The coming off obviously depends on what you stick it on...if it's paper you're going to have to go really slow...if it's glass it will come off just fine. Washi comes from wa meaning Japanese and shi meaning paper, and the term is used to describe paper made in the traditional manner, regardless of where it’s manufactured. When it comes to washi tape being eco friendly we consider the alternatives...which is duct (duck) tape.
