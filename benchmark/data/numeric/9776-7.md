The need for a convenient, easy-to-use format for magnetic tape was recognized early on. A number of attempts were made, including RCA Victor’s RCA Tape Cartridge in 1958 and the Muntz Stereo-Pak in 1952. RCA’s cassette was similar to the cassette tape we know, but in a larger package and with a 0.25-inch tape, like reel-to-reel. This made for a cassette that was 5 x 7.50 inches, and 0.50-inches thick. Unfortunately for RCA, the lack of prerecorded music available on the market made their product a failure.
