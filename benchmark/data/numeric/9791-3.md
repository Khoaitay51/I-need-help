Biometric Fee. In addition to the basic fee, applicants under 75 years of age must pay a biometric fee, which covers the cost of fingerprinting and other expenses. This fee is $85 as of 2014. Adding $85 to the filing fee, your total due comes to $680. Applicants aged 75 or older pay only the filing fee of $595. Filing Fee. Most applicants for U.S. citizenship pay $595 when filing Form N-400, the application for naturalization. Exceptions include those who qualify as members or veterans of the armed forces.
