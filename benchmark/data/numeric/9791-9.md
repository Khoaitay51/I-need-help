Step Three: File USCIS Form N-400. Once you have established your eligibility, you need to file some paperwork with U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). The N-400 is the form to get the process started. As of 2015, it cost $595 to file the application for naturalization, plus an $85 biometrics fee. You will need to attach a copy of your green card. Once your application has been accepted, you will be mailed a date for your fingerprinting and biometrics. See our tips for filing the N-400 to find out what to expect.
