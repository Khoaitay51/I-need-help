Tags: , camellia, grow, plant, plant shrub, planting, planting shrubs. Camellia japonica “Red Red Rose”. As a foundation shrub, or a specimen plant, camellias (Camellia sp. ) offer striking green foliage, elegant shaping, and brightly-colored blooms that make them one of the mainstays of the year-round garden. Planting Tips. 1  Camellias need to be planted a little high, so that the top of the root ball is level with the surface of the soil. 2  This helps water drain away from the trunk. 3  Camellia roots are shallow, so avoid planting them under shallow-rooted shade trees such as birch and maple.
