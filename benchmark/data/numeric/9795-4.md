1 Japonica camellias (Camellia japonica) bloom in winter or early spring. 2  They have large leaves and flowers and are the species most familiar to home gardeners. 3  Sasanqua camellias (Camellia sasanqua) have smaller, darker leaves along with smaller blossoms, and they bloom in the fall. Planting Tips. 1  Camellias need to be planted a little high, so that the top of the root ball is level with the surface of the soil. 2  This helps water drain away from the trunk. 3  Camellia roots are shallow, so avoid planting them under shallow-rooted shade trees such as birch and maple.
