Camellia shrubs have several different growth habits and sizes. Camellias (Camellia sp.) are evergreen shrubs with exquisite single or semidouble flowers that generally bloom from winter to spring, depending on the variety. They require acidic soil and partial shade but are otherwise dependable plants once established. Spreading Camellias. A few camellia cultivars have an unusually low, spreading and somewhat dwarf growth habit. One of these, a japonica type called Shishigashira, develops into a spreading shrub about 6 feet high and equally wide. It has semidouble, deep pink blossoms and foliage that tends to be a bronze-red color.
