Like most camellias, these plants are quite drought-tolerant but do best with regular watering and need at least 1 inch of water weekly while flowering. Spreading Camellias. A few camellia cultivars have an unusually low, spreading and somewhat dwarf growth habit. One of these, a japonica type called Shishigashira, develops into a spreading shrub about 6 feet high and equally wide. It has semidouble, deep pink blossoms and foliage that tends to be a bronze-red color.
