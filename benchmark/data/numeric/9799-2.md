Title Fee. Motor Vehicle. For model year prior to 1983, fee depends on the weight of the vehicle; after 1983, fee depends on the price of the vehicle; fees can vary from $33 to $148, fees decline by 10% each year until the fifth renewal. Title Fee. Motor Vehicle. Based on vehicle weight + $1.50 charge as part of the registration fee every two years. $51.50. Motorcycle. Prorated by date + $1.50 charge as part of the registration fee.
