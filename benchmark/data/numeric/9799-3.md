When you move to Florida and become a resident you have 10 days to register your out of state vehicle. You are considered a Florida resident when you place your children in public schools, become employed, or otherwise establish Florida residency. To register your out of state car you must complete an application for certificate of title this form starts the process of transferring the title of your vehicle from your current state to Florida. To complete this form you will need: 1  The Vehicle Identification Number.
