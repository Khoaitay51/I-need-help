To register any vehicle in Florida that does have a Florida registration there is an initial registration fee of $100. In addition you will pay a standard registration fee based on the vehicles weight as follows: 1  $27.60 for vehicles under 2500 pounds. 2  $35.60 for vehicles between 2500 and 3499 pounds. To register your out of state car you must complete an application for certificate of title this form starts the process of transferring the title of your vehicle from your current state to Florida. To complete this form you will need: 1  The Vehicle Identification Number.
