Natural-gas options. Installing a gas conversion burner on your existing oil furnace will cost around $1,000, but will have little or no impact on the efficiency of the unit. A gas conversion burner also may be leased from the gas company for $4.20 per month. If it was built before 1970, you may want to replace the furnace entirely rather than retrofit it with a gas conversion or flame-retention burner. Replacing a pre-1970 furnace with a new oil-fired unit with a combustion efficiency of 80 percent to 85 percent can reduce your consumption by as much as 30 percent.
