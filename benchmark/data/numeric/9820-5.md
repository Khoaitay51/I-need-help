Print. The mediastinum is the area that separates the lungs. It is surrounded by the breastbone in front and the spine in back, with the lungs on either side. It encompasses the heart, aorta, esophagus, thymus (a gland in the back of the neck) and trachea (windpipe). Middle mediastinal tumors include: 1  Bronchogenic cysts-a disease in the respiratory system. 2  Lymphadenopathy mediastinal-disease in the lymph nodes. 3  Pericardial cysts which include growths that start in the lining of the heart.
