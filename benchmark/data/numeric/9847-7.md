The effect of different temperature on the cell respiration. Introduction：. Cell respiration is the controlled release of energy from organic compounds in cells to form ATP,and then release waste products. The process of cell respiration can take place in the presence or absence of oxygen. If we lower the temperature at 7℃ or raise it above 60 ℃ , the rate of cell respiration would slow down,because at the very low temperature(7℃) enzyme will be inactive and at a very high temperature (above 60 ℃), the enzyme would denature and the structure of it changes permanently.
