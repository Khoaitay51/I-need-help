Mourning dove chicks fledge within 15 days of hatching, so they will be gone before 2 weeks. Just remember to remove the nest as soon as they are all gone. If they are mourning doves, they are protected by federal law. It's illegal to tamper with the nest, move it, or take any of the birds. Why does it need to be removed? They usually leave the nest when they are about 14-17 days old. Hopefully the bird will be gone in 2 weeks since it must already be at least 4-5 days old if is has some feathers.
