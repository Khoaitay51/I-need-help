Hourly Wages. The average hourly wage for bookkeepers is $16.71, according to 2009 data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. This wage translates to an annual salary of $34,750 for those working full-time. Another key task for the bookkeeper is to provide financial reports for business managers or owners.
