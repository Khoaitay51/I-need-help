Hourly Wages. According to the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics, the median pay for bookkeepers is $16.36 per hour, or $34,030 per year, as of 2010. O*Net OnLine's more recent estimates are slightly higher hourly at $16.70 as of 2011. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reports that the lowest hourly wage for bookkeepers as of 2010 is approximately $10.22 per hour, with the highest earners drawing hourly wages of $24.75 per hour.
