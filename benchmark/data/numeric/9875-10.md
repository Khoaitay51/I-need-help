The Hendo Hoverboard requires a certain kind of surface in order to work, so the Hendo team will also be creating a skatepark specifically for Hendo owners. As of right now, the board is still in its early stages, but when it’s available, you’ll have to be willing to spend $10,000 on one. Hoverboards are something we’ve all wanted for so long. Our hopes and dreams were crushed time and time again as viral videos showcasing what we thought were hoverboards were uncovered as fakes. All of that is about to end now, thanks to the Hendo Hoverboard.
