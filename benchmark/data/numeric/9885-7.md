Concrete/Gunite Concrete pools typically cost about the same as a fiberglass pool initially. When one analyzes long-term costs though, they will find a huge discrepancy. For example, concrete pools typically have to be replastered every 8-12 years. This repair usually costs between $6,000-$10,000.
