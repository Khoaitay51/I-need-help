Her parents were Henry Grey, the marquees of Dorset and Frances Brandon, the daughter of Henry VIII's sister Mary. Jane's father later became the Duke of Suffolk. Jane had two younger sisters, Katherine, born in 1540 and Mary, born in 1545. Edward's birth also likely sparked the hope of Jane's parents, that she might one day be his bride and Queen of England. Jane's baptism took place within forty-eight hours, as was the custom, in the parish church at Bradgate.
