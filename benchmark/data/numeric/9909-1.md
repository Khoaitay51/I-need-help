Cars and minivans cost less for brakes than SUVs and trucks in most cases. For a standard brake job where they just replace the pads and turn the rotors and drums, you can get that done when Midas is having a special for $79.99 per axle (so you're looking at $160.00 plus tax). If you take it to a shop to do the work and you don't know much about cars, expect the bill to be at least double what you think it will cost. once they get your car they will sell you everything under the sun to make sure you have good, safe brakes. Expect at least $400.
