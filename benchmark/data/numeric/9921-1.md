Germinate seeds at 70-75F, then plant in 2-3 inch pots. Start seeds in early January.Plants require 2-3 months to reach transplantable size. Planting and Spacing: Artichoke should be planted 3-4 weeks before the frost-free date for your area.Artichoke requires cool temperatures to initiate the flower stalk. If planted too late, plants will not flower.Space plants 18 inches apart in the row, with rows 2-3 feet apart.
