Artichoke plants are perennial and if they are in the right climate conditions — in zones 8 to 11 — they should produce flower buds for several years before needing to be replaced. From being sown, artichokes take about 150 up to 180 days to mature. However, just recently, a new variety of artichoke has been cultivated. That would depend on where you’re at and the variety of artichoke you are trying to grow. Typically, artichokes grown from seeds do not produce any buds during their first year. Or when they do, they are very small and of poor quality.
