Most Mailboxes USA or Etc. have notaries on call as well. Cost is specific by state. In CA, it's $10 per signature witnessed (so if two people are signing or two documents are being signed by one person, it'd be $20 for example). Bring the document(s) with you when you appear before the notary. You will be required to provide a valid ID and possibly a thumb print. The people signing the document must go down to the notary and show id to prove who you are and sign in front of them. Its about 10 to 12 dollars per signature. You can get it done at all kinko's and UPS stores.
