According to the list compiled by Glassdoor and based on 58 retail companies in the U.S. here are the 10 highest-paying hourly jobs in fashion retail. The study consisted on. employees who work on stores floor that sell apparel, accessories or beauty products, such as cashiers and sales associates, excluding managers and supervisors. Bloomingdale's topped the list, with an average hourly salary of $12.36. Pacific Sunwear was at the bottom, at $7.62 an hour. And Abercrombie & Fitch was second-to-last, with an average hourly wage of $7.69. #10 Belk, Sales Associate, $9.49 an hour. #9 Zara, Sales Associate, $9.66 an hour.
