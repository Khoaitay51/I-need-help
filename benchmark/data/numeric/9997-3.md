The annual total list price cost to go to Yale was $63,250 for the 2014/2015 academic year. The cost is the same for all students regardless of Connecticut residence status as there is no in-state discount. What will it cost to send your child to Yale University in the future? In the past five years, this school's published in-state tuition and fee total rose from $33,030 to $40,500. This amounts to an average annual price increase of 4.5%.
