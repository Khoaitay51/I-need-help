Cinema tickets cost between EUR 7 to EUR 12 depending on what time and where you see the film. Student discounts are usually available during the week on production of a student card, but most cinemas do not offer student rates at the weekend and there is no discount at the expensive soft-drink and snack counters! On average, a pint of beer in Dublin costs about EUR 4.50 and a glass of wine costs about EUR 5. However, prices can vary quite significantly between bars. If cigarettes are part of your lifestyle, you are likely to find them expensive in Ireland at around EUR 10.00 for a packet of 20.
