Every year estimates are published which give an indication of how much it costs to be a student for one academic year (nine months) in Ireland. Recent figures for Dublin have ranged between EUR 10,000 and EUR 15,000, largely depending on the type of accommodation chosen. On average, a pint of beer in Dublin costs about EUR 4.50 and a glass of wine costs about EUR 5. However, prices can vary quite significantly between bars. If cigarettes are part of your lifestyle, you are likely to find them expensive in Ireland at around EUR 10.00 for a packet of 20.
