The Manhattan Project. World War II started on September 1 1939, when Germany attacked Poland. By 1941, the Germans were ahead in the race for the atomic bomb. They had a heavy-water plant, high-grade uranium compounds, capable scientists and engineers, and the greatest chemical engineering industry in the world. The explosion created a crater which measured nearly 2,400 feet across and was equivalent to about 20,000 tons of TNT. The Manhattan Project produced three bombs: the first bomb was known as Gadget and was used as a test model.
