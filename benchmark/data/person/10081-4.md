List of Olympic athletes born in Alaska, listed alphabetically with photos when available. These famous Olympians from Alaska have achieved some of the highest honors in sports history just by qualifying for the Olympics, as most athletes will never be chosen to represent their countries. These are the most prominent people from Alaska who have competed in the Olympics, although not all of them have taken home an Olympic medal. While these popular Alaska Olympians were born in Alaska, they didn't necessarily compete for their originating country. Athletes include everyone from Scott Gomez to Jeff Pain.
