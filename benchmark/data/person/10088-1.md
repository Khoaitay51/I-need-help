Jake brainstorms with Percy Jackson, Annabeth Chase, Chiron, and the other campers in order to figure out how to defend Manhattan and the Empire State Building, which is the entrance to Olympus in the Battle of Manhattan. As a child of Hephaestus, he has the following abilities: 1  He is an expert builder. 2  Technokinesis: As the son of Hephaestus, he can manipulate machines. 3  He is an expert mechanic. 4  He can sense machines, mechanics, or metal. 5  He can identify machinery type and use by touch.
