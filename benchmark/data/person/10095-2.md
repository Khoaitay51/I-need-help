The NCIS is led by Director Leon Vance. The first director seen in the series, Thomas Morrow (Alan Dale), left after accepting the position of Deputy Director of the Department of Homeland Security. NCIS was originally referred to as Navy NCIS during season one; Navy was later dropped from the title. In season six, a two-part episode led to a spin-off series, NCIS: Los Angeles. A two-part episode in season eleven led to a second spin-off series, NCIS: New Orleans.
