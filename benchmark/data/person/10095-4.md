WASHINGTON (NNS) -- Secretary of the Navy Ray Mabus announced the next director and deputy director of the Naval Criminal Investigative Service (NCIS) Jan. 22. Special Agent Mark Clookie, NCIS executive assistant director for combating terrorism, was named the next director of NCIS.
