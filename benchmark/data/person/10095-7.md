Jerome Craig (played by Greg Germann) is the current NCIS deputy director. He first appears in the episode  Shiva . He is brought in when Director Vance is put on temporary leave and describes himself as soft and appears to be a bit confused at times. Cyril Taft (played by Jon Cryer) is a doctor who appears in the NCIS Season 13 premiere episode, Stop the Bleeding where he performs surgery on Gibbs who has been left badly injured after being shot by Luke Harris.
