The Many Loves of Dobie Gillis was created by Max Shulman from stories that he wrote about the title character, but oddly, the Maynard G. Krebs character, the most famous beatnik in TV history, was not in either of Max Shulman s books. This series preceded Bob Denver s trip aboard the S.S. Minnow in the role of Gilligan with the Skipper, the Professor, Maryann, Ginger, and Mr. and Mrs. Howell. Maynard G. Krebs began as a stereotypical beatnik with a goatee who used hip slang.
