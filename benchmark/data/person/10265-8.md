Maynard lived in his own world with its own twisted logic. His name was also illogical, considering the “G” in Maynard G. Krebs stood for Walter. According to the show’s creator, Maynard was named after his Aunt Walter who was married to his Uncle Edith. Maynard still remains the most famous beatnik in history. Just ask TV Guide, who voted Maynard one of the 50 best TV characters ever! In 1958 Bob was a school teacher and working parttime at the post office when he got to audition for the series pilot.
