In the role of vindictive call girl turned corporate executive Kate Roberts, Koslow flourished on Days of Our Lives quickly becoming a woman fans 'love to hate'. 1 In 2000 Koslow was nominated for a Soap Opera Digest Award as Outstanding Supporting Actress for her work on Days of Our Lives. 2  In 2009 Koslow was pre-nominated for a Daytime Emmy as Outstanding Lead Actress.
