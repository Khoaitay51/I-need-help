Koslow’s daytime career was off and running. After exiting Y&R, the actress landed the role of fashion designer Margo Lynley on The Bold and the Beautiful. Then, in 1996, she was cast as Kate Roberts on Days of Our Lives. Off camera, Koslow is the devoted mother of two: son Zachariah and daughter Milikate. The brood resides on a farm in Northern California with a menagerie of pets from dogs to chickens to horses.
