A great character on and off air, Billy Brownless continues to rank as a hit with audiences everywhere. The former high-flying Geelong forward is best known for his decade long involvement with the-Nine Network's Logie award winning program The Footy Show. However, Billy Brownless had to contend with a number of injuries in the final few years of his career and at the end of the 1997 season Brownless announced his retirement, finishing the career of one of Geelong’s most effervescent characters. Billy Brownless was an exciting forward for Geelong for over ten years.
