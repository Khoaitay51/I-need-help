Billy Brownless joined fellow country boys Garry Lyon and Tim Watson in 2003 to start Up Country Punt, a radio show for footy fans across rural Australia. However, Billy Brownless had to contend with a number of injuries in the final few years of his career and at the end of the 1997 season Brownless announced his retirement, finishing the career of one of Geelong’s most effervescent characters. Billy Brownless was an exciting forward for Geelong for over ten years.
