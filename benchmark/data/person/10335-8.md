Billy Brownless is a regular panel member, previewing the upcoming round of AFL while also holding down a regular role in the show's soap parodies such as The House of Bulger and Bullet and Gunn. Also a member of the Sunday Footy Show team, Brownless lends his talents to radio. However, Billy Brownless had to contend with a number of injuries in the final few years of his career and at the end of the 1997 season Brownless announced his retirement, finishing the career of one of Geelong’s most effervescent characters. Billy Brownless was an exciting forward for Geelong for over ten years.
