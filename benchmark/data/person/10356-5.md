The Executive Branch is comprised of the President and his Cabinet (Secretaries). Secretaries serve as the heads of government departments. Departments, commissions, and agencies are responsible for interpreting the laws passed by Congress and administering government programs created by those laws. The Legislative Branch is the lawmaking body of government. This branch is comprised of the Congress which is broken into two houses: the House and Senate. There are 435 members of the House of Representatives and 100 members of the Senate.
