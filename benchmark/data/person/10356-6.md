While the federal Constitution identifies only the federal branches of government, the principle of checks and balances applies to the states as well. The Judicial Branch. The judicial branch is responsible for interpreting all laws, including statutes, codes, ordinances, and the federal and state constitutions. This power is all encompassing and is the basis for judicial review, referenced in Chapter 1 Introduction to Criminal Law .
