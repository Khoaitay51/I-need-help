Specialists (e.g. psychiatrists, neurologists) may apply for authorisation to prescribe psychostimulant medication for the treatment of ADHD in a person aged 18 years or over using the form: 1  Application for Authority to Prescribe Dexamphetamine or Methylphenidate for ADHD in Adults (Form 1AA). Specialists (respiratory & sleep physicians, neurologists, psychiatrists) may apply for authorisation to prescribe psychostimulant medication for a person with a diagnosis other than ADHD
