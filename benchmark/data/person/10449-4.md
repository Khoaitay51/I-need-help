It is the 4th best selling soft drink in America behind Coca Cola, Pepsi-Cola and Diet Coke. Mountain Dew was invented in 1940′s in Marion, Virginia (USA) by two brothers, Barney and Ally Hartman. It was originally invented to be a mixer drink with whiskey, and it had a very different formula to today’s Mountain Dew.
