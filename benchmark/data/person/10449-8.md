In spring 2014, Mountain Dew began distributing its Baja Blast flavor in standard 12 ounce cans, 20 ounce bottles and 24 ounce cans for sale at supermarkets and retail businesses in the US. The new retail product bears the Taco Bell logo. Beginning in 2007, Mountain Dew began a promotion entitled DEWmocracy, which involved the public electing new flavors, colors, names, packaging graphics and advertisements for upcoming Mountain Dew products.
