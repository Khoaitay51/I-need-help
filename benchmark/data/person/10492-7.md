The Hopi are a Native American tribe, who primarily live on the 2,531.773 sq mi (6,557.26 km 2) Hopi Reservation in northeastern Arizona. The Hopi language is one of the 30 of the Uto-Aztecan language family. The majority of Hopi people are enrolled in the Hopi Tribe of Arizona but some are enrolled in the Colorado River Indian Tribes.
