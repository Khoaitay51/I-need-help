With a median pay of $62K, Dell, Inc. Inside Sales Representatives make much more than the national average ($40K annually) for those in this field. Inside Sales Representatives can also look forward to large paychecks at Oracle ($51K), Ryerson, Inc. ($48K), and United Parcel Service (UPS), Inc. ($46K). Job Description for Inside Sales Representative. An inside sales representative works for a company that wants to sell a product or service. It is the inside sales representative's job to complete the process of that sale from start to finish. This may be done over the phone or in person via walk-in clients.
