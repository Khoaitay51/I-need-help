For Inside Sales Representatives, Boston provides a pay rate that is 33 percent greater than the national average. Inside Sales Representatives can also look forward to large paychecks in cities like New York (+19 percent), Minneapolis (+15 percent), Seattle (+15 percent), and Houston (+13 percent). Job Description for Inside Sales Representative. An inside sales representative works for a company that wants to sell a product or service. It is the inside sales representative's job to complete the process of that sale from start to finish. This may be done over the phone or in person via walk-in clients.
