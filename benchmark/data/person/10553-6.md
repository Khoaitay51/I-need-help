The typical salary for an inside salesperson is $24,000 to $36,000 per year and, similar to the outside rep, 40% of that is fixed salary and 60% is commission. Commission: On average, an outside salesperson's commission is about 8% of sales when you factor in out-of-pocket expenses. Posted Oct 07, 2011, Published 2011-10-01. The primary objectives of an outside vs. inside sales rep differ: the outside rep is responsible for developing customer relationships and sales whereas the inside rep handles account maintenance. Consequently, their compensation packages vary significantly
