When the calcium level in the blood is too low, the cells of the parathyroids sense it and make more parathyroid hormone. Once the parathyroid hormone is released into the blood, it circulates to act in a number of places to increase the amount of calcium in the blood (like removing calcium from bones). The four parathyroid glands make more or less parathyroid hormone (PTH) in response to the level of calcium in the blood.
