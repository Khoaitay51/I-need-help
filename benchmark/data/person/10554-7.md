Parathyroid hormone (PTH) works with vitamin D to regulate the amounts of calcium that go into bone & teeth and float around in the blood. Normally about 90% of your body's calcium is in your skeleton. If your parathyroids become overactive, some of the calcium that should go into your bones stays in your blood instead. Parathyroid hormone (PTH) and calcitonin regulate blood calcium levels. PTH increases the calcium concentration in the blood, and calcitonin decreases it.
