Brother Lester Roloff One of America's most compassionate Preachers Lester L. Roloff was born on June 28, 1914 in Dawson, Texas. He grew up there on a cotton farm. At the age of 12, he was saved, and at the age of 18, he surrendered to the Lords call to preach.
