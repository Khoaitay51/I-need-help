Performing a reverse phone search is theoretically much more straightforward -- you enter a phone number in a Web page and learn the person's name and address. Unfortunately, reverse cell phone lookups remains one of the few kinds of online searches that it's almost impossible to get for free. In principle, the simply named Phone Lookup is like the Google of phone lookup Web pages -- just enter the phone number you want to reverse engineer and click Lookup. In my experience, at times, this site is quite good at identifying the personal information of whoever is behind the phone number you enter.
