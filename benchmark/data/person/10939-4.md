In 1939 Shche ne vmerla Ukrayina was adopted as the official state anthem of Carpatho-Ukraine. When Ukraine was annexed by the Soviet Union in 1920 and the Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic was formed, the anthem was immediately banned by the Soviet regime. However, the lyrics for the anthem were not officially adopted until March 6, 2003, when the Verkhovna Rada passed a law on the state anthem of Ukraine (Ukrainian: закон про державний гімн україни), proposed by then president Leonid Kuchma.
