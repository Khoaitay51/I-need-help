Timeline of the Microscope. 14th century: spectacles first made in Italy. 1590: Two Dutch spectacle-makers and father-and-son team, Hans and Zacharias Janssen, create the first microscope. 1667: Robert Hooke 's famous Micrographia is published, which outlines Hooke's various studies using the microscope. 1675: Enter Anton van Leeuwenhoek, who used a microscope with one lens to observe insects and other specimen. Leeuwenhoek was the first to observe bacteria.
