Composed by Andrew Lloyd Webber, the production of Cats is based on T. S. Eliot 's Old Possum's Book of Practical Cats (1939), which the composer recalled as having been a childhood favourite. The musical was produced by Cameron Mackintosh and Lloyd Webber's Really Useful Group, directed by Trevor Nunn, with associate director and choreographer Gillian Lynne, design by John Napier, and lighting by David Hersey. The conductor was Harry Rabinowicz. It played a total of 8,949 performances in London.
